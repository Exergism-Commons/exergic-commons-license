# Restricted Parties Schedule

Schedule ID: **ECL-RP-0.2-DRAFT**  
Status: **Draft / experimental**  
Evidence cutoff: **2026-08-11**  
Applies only to software releases that expressly incorporate this exact Schedule ID.

> This is a draft ethical licensing schedule, not a judicial determination and not legal advice. A Restricted designation means that the available evidence is considered sufficient for the ECL licensing threshold defined in `EXERGIC-GOVERNANCE.md`; it does **not** declare criminal guilt. Criminal allegations remain allegations unless finally adjudicated.

## 0. Scope and epistemic limit

ECL-RP-0.2-DRAFT is an evidence-backed baseline, not a claim that every qualifying actor in the world has been discovered or evaluated.

The word **Restricted** is used only where the current evidence supports a reasoned conclusion that an actor materially and systematically participates in, directs, enables, or substantially benefits from conduct matching one or more ECL restriction grounds. **Under Review** is used where there is serious evidence but the actor, scope, attribution, persistence, or evidentiary status is not yet sufficiently precise for a Restricted designation.

No person is restricted merely because of nationality, ethnicity, religion, political affiliation, citizenship, residence, passive investment, incidental contact, or remote association.

Unless expressly stated otherwise:

- employees, contractors, advisers, investors, customers, collaborators and users are Covered Associates only when their relevant activity materially supports a Restricted Party or Restricted Project;
- a collaborator of a collaborator is not recursively restricted merely through social or professional proximity;
- restrictions on state or military bodies apply to the named body and materially participating controlled bodies, not to the population of the relevant country;
- later changes to external lists or findings do not silently change this Schedule; and
- the anti-circumvention and Restricted Project rules in `LICENSE` apply to every designation below.

## 1. Palantir Technologies Inc. — PROVISIONAL RESTRICTED

**Entity:** Palantir Technologies Inc.  
**SEC CIK:** 0001321655  
**Status:** Provisional Restricted in this draft pending a dedicated adversarial evidence record before ECL 1.0.

The designation includes:

1. Palantir Technologies Inc.;
2. entities directly or indirectly Controlled by Palantir Technologies Inc.;
3. entities under common Control where the relationship is materially relevant to the proposed use of ECL-covered Software; and
4. entities established, directed, or materially used to circumvent this designation.

### 1.1 Directors, officers, employees and contractors

Directors and executive officers are Restricted Parties when exercising ECL rights in their professional capacity, through an entity they Control for the relevant activity, or in relation to a project materially connected to Palantir.

Employees, contractors, consultants and material collaborators are Covered Associates when the relevant use or project:

- is performed as part of their work for Palantir;
- materially supports Palantir;
- incorporates Palantir products or services in a material operational role;
- is jointly designed, developed, governed, operated, financed or deployed with Palantir; or
- is performed on behalf of or for the material benefit of Palantir.

The designation does not permanently follow a person into unrelated activity solely because of present or historical employment.

### 1.2 Investors and controlling owners

Passive ownership through a diversified fund, pension, index product or ordinary portfolio does not create Restricted status.

A controlling owner, person with material governance influence, knowing financier of a Restricted Project, or intermediary whose ownership structure is materially used to circumvent ECL may be a Restricted Party or Covered Associate.

### 1.3 Customers, services and joint projects

A customer does not become permanently Restricted merely by purchasing or accessing a Palantir product.

A project is a **Restricted Project** where Palantir software, services, personnel, contractors or associated infrastructure materially participates in its design, operation, analysis, targeting, decision-making, deployment or material outputs and ECL-covered Software would be used within or materially for that project.

## 3. Fixed class: public ICC atrocity-warrant subjects — RESTRICTED

A natural person is Restricted under this class where, as of **2026-08-11**:

1. the International Criminal Court has publicly identified that person as the subject of an arrest warrant for genocide, crimes against humanity or war crimes;
2. the published factual basis materially matches ECL grounds such as persecution, torture, murder of civilians, unlawful coercive targeting, starvation, unlawful deportation, sexual violence, enslavement or comparable destruction of agency; and
3. the warrant or case had not been publicly terminated before the evidence cutoff.

This is a licensing classification, not a declaration that the accused is guilty. ICC arrest warrants are based on the Court's applicable pre-trial legal threshold, and defendants retain the presumption of innocence.

The class includes, among others where the above test is met, persons publicly identified by the ICC in the situations in Ukraine, the State of Palestine, Libya, Afghanistan, the Philippines, Uganda, the Central African Republic and other ICC situations.

Examples supported by public ICC records include Vladimir Putin, Maria Lvova-Belova, Sergei Kobylash, Viktor Sokolov, Sergei Shoigu, Valery Gerasimov, Benjamin Netanyahu, Yoav Gallant, Osama Elmasry Njeem and other persons satisfying the frozen class definition.

## 15. Commercial and state anti-circumvention rule

For every Restricted Party in this Schedule, a project remains a Restricted Project when ECL-covered Software is introduced through a subcontractor, university, consultancy, open-source intermediary, affiliate, shell entity, customer, cloud provider, integration partner, financing vehicle or other third party for the purpose or substantial effect of doing indirectly what the ECL terms prohibit directly.

A third party is not automatically Restricted merely because it has once worked with a Restricted Party. The restriction follows **Material Participation in the relevant Restricted Project**, Control, direction, substantial benefit, knowing financing or circumvention.

## 17. Review rule

Every Restricted designation in ECL-RP-0.2-DRAFT should be eligible for removal, narrowing or reclassification when material evidence changes.

Before any ECL 1.0 stable schedule:

1. each designation should receive its own public issue or decision record;
2. primary evidence should be archived or pinned where legally possible;
3. material counter-evidence and responses should be recorded;
4. exact organizational boundaries should be clarified;
5. conflicts of interest should be disclosed; and
6. an independent or multi-maintainer review mechanism should replace unilateral final designation where practical.

The purpose is to restrict conduct that destroys agency, not to create permanent hereditary, national, ideological or associational stigma.

## 18. Non-retroactivity

ECL-RP-0.2-DRAFT applies only when a software release expressly identifies this Schedule ID.

Later additions, removals, judicial outcomes, changes to external sanctions lists, organizational restructurings or new factual findings do not alter rights attached to an earlier software release unless that release expressly incorporated a mechanism that lawfully provides otherwise.
