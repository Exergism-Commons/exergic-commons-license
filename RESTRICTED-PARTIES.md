# Restricted Parties Schedule

Schedule ID: **ECL-RP-0.3-DRAFT**  
Status: **Draft / experimental**  
Evidence cutoff: **2026-08-11**  
Applies only to software releases that expressly incorporate this exact Schedule ID.

> This is a draft ethical licensing schedule, not a judicial determination and not legal advice. A Restricted designation is an ECL licensing classification based on the evidentiary threshold in `EXERGIC-GOVERNANCE.md`; it is not a declaration of criminal guilt.

## 0. General scope

ECL-RP-0.3-DRAFT is an evidence-backed baseline. It does not claim that every qualifying actor worldwide has been identified.

No person is Restricted merely because of nationality, ethnicity, religion, citizenship, residence, political opinion, passive investment, incidental contact or remote association.

Unless expressly stated otherwise:

- a designation of a State applies to the State as a legal/governing apparatus, its organs, agencies and controlled entities acting in an official or materially supporting capacity; it does **not** designate the population or nationality;
- employees, contractors, advisers, suppliers and collaborators are Covered Associates only where their relevant activity materially supports a Restricted Party or Restricted Project;
- participation is not recursively transitive without limit;
- later changes in evidence or external lists do not silently modify this Schedule; and
- the anti-circumvention and Restricted Project provisions of `LICENSE` apply throughout.

## 5. State of Israel and state-controlled apparatus — RESTRICTED

**Restricted Party:** the **State of Israel as a governmental/legal apparatus**, including its ministries, armed forces, intelligence and security agencies, police, prison and detention structures, occupation administrations, settlement-support authorities, and state-controlled entities when acting for or materially supporting the State.

This designation does **not** restrict Israeli citizens, Jewish persons, Israeli civil society, universities, independent researchers, independent private businesses, humanitarian organisations or other persons merely because of nationality, ethnicity, religion or location.

### ECL basis

The designation is grounded in conduct attributable to the State rather than identity. The International Court of Justice concluded in its 19 July 2024 Advisory Opinion that:

- Israel's continued presence in the Occupied Palestinian Territory is unlawful;
- Israel must bring that unlawful presence to an end as rapidly as possible;
- Israeli policies and practices violate the Palestinian people's right to self-determination;
- Israeli policies amount to annexation of large parts of the Occupied Palestinian Territory;
- the regime of restrictions imposed on Palestinians constitutes systemic discrimination; and
- legislation and measures maintain a near-complete separation between Palestinian and settler communities and breach Article 3 of CERD.

The ICJ did not itself choose between the labels "racial segregation" and "apartheid" when identifying the Article 3 breach. ECL therefore relies on the operative findings rather than overstating the Court's wording.

OHCHR reporting in January 2026 again documented discriminatory administration of the occupied West Bank and warned that Israel is violating international-law obligations concerning racial segregation and apartheid. The UN Committee against Torture also expressed grave concern in 2026 regarding reports of an organised and widespread de facto State policy of torture and ill-treatment, collective punishment, restrictions on humanitarian aid, civilian targeting and systemic discriminatory restrictions.

Named individuals satisfying Section 3 remain independently Restricted.

Evidence:
- https://www.icj-cij.org/index.php/node/204160
- https://www.icj-cij.org/node/204176
- https://searchlibrary.ohchr.org/record/35209?ln=en
- https://docstore.ohchr.org/

## 6. United States immigration-enforcement and military targeting apparatus — RESTRICTED WITH DEFINED SCOPE

### 6.1 U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE)

**Restricted Party in its immigration-enforcement, detention, investigation and removal capacity:** U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement, including components and contractors materially operating detention, coercive investigation, removal or data-analysis projects for ICE.

The designation is based on the scale and coercive character of the detention system together with documented oversight failures that have created serious risks to life, health and due process. In June 2026 the U.S. Government Accountability Office found that ICE's Camp East Montana opened without meeting key detention standards, with medical-service gaps and unsanitary conditions; GAO also documented a January 2026 detainee death classified by the coroner as homicide by asphyxia and missing or destroyed evidence associated with the incident.

Palantir is additionally a material technology supplier to ICE. A January 2026 federal procurement notice describes a sole-source procurement to Palantir for ICE's Investigative Case Management and Investigative Analytics modernization, systems described as integral to ICE investigative operations.

Evidence:
- https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-26-108886
- https://sam.gov/opp/5477991867424397affe85e2fad8b5fc/view

### 6.2 Operation Epic Fury / Minab targeting chain

**Restricted Project:** Operation Epic Fury targeting activities and the U.S. military/intelligence units, targeting cells, command structures, contractors and technical systems materially involved in the 28 February 2026 strike on Shajareh Tayyebeh Elementary School in Minab, Iran, and in any continuation of the same targeting process where the identified defects remain unremediated.

Amnesty International concluded after investigation that the United States was responsible for the strike, which killed **156 people, including 120 children**, and that U.S. forces violated international humanitarian law by failing to take all feasible precautions to avoid civilian harm. Public reporting indicates that obsolete target intelligence was a central failure and that Palantir's Maven system formed part of the targeting infrastructure. ECL does not assert, absent conclusive evidence, that a software defect in Maven alone selected the school or caused the strike.

Evidence:
- https://www.amnesty.org/en/latest/news/2026/03/usa-iran-those-responsible-for-deadly-and-unlawful-us-strike-on-school-that-killed-over-100-children-must-be-held-accountable/

### 6.3 Historical U.S. coercive programmes

The following are Historical Restricted Projects:

- the CIA post-9/11 detention and interrogation programme insofar as it involved torture, cruel treatment, secret detention or rendition; and
- the NSA Section 215 bulk telephone-records programme in the form found legally and constitutionally defective by the U.S. Privacy and Civil Liberties Oversight Board.

Historical classification does not automatically Restrict unrelated present-day activity of every employee who once belonged to the relevant agency.

## 17. Anti-circumvention and collaborative-project rule

For every Restricted Party, a project remains Restricted when ECL-covered Software is introduced through a subcontractor, university, consultancy, open-source intermediary, affiliate, shell entity, customer, cloud provider, integration partner, financing vehicle or other third party for the purpose or substantial effect of doing indirectly what ECL prohibits directly.

A third party is not permanently Restricted merely because it has once worked with a Restricted Party. The restriction follows **Material Participation in the relevant Restricted Project**, Control, direction, substantial benefit, knowing financing or circumvention.

---

## 18. Review and non-retroactivity

Every designation must remain eligible for narrowing, removal or reclassification when material evidence changes.

Before ECL 1.0, each stable designation should receive its own public evidence/decision record with primary sources, counter-evidence, scope, conflicts of interest and a reasoned decision.

ECL-RP-0.3-DRAFT applies only to software releases expressly identifying this Schedule ID. Later additions, removals, judicial outcomes, changes to sanctions lists, organisational restructurings or new factual findings do not retroactively alter rights attached to an earlier release.
