# Restricted Parties Schedule

Schedule ID: **ECL-RP-0.1-DRAFT**  
Status: **Draft / experimental**  
Applies only to software releases that expressly incorporate this exact Schedule ID.

> This is a draft policy schedule, not a final adjudication and not legal advice. Before ECL 1.0, each stable designation should have a public evidence record produced under `EXERGIC-GOVERNANCE.md`.

## 1. Restricted organization: Palantir Technologies Inc.

**Entity:** Palantir Technologies Inc.  
**SEC CIK:** 0001321655  
**Designation status:** Restricted in ECL-RP-0.1-DRAFT for purposes of testing and reviewing the ECL mechanism.

The designation includes:

1. Palantir Technologies Inc.;
2. entities directly or indirectly Controlled by Palantir Technologies Inc.;
3. entities under common Control where the relationship is materially relevant to the proposed use of ECL-covered Software; and
4. entities established, directed, or materially used to circumvent this designation.

### 1.1 Directors and executive officers

Current directors and executive officers of Palantir Technologies Inc. are treated as Restricted Parties when exercising ECL rights in their professional capacity, through an entity they Control for the relevant activity, or in relation to a project materially connected to Palantir.

A future stable Schedule may designate specific individuals without this professional-capacity limitation where the evidence record independently supports doing so.

### 1.2 Employees, contractors, consultants, and collaborators

Current employees, contractors, consultants, and material collaborators of Palantir are Covered Associates when the relevant use or project:

- is performed as part of their work for Palantir;
- materially supports Palantir;
- incorporates Palantir products or services in a material operational role;
- is jointly designed, developed, governed, operated, financed, or deployed with Palantir; or
- is performed on behalf of or for the material benefit of Palantir.

Such a person is not permanently restricted from unrelated personal or professional activity solely because of employment or historical association unless separately named as a Restricted Party.

### 1.3 Investors and controlling owners

An investor is not automatically restricted merely because a diversified fund, pension, index product, or passive portfolio contains Palantir securities.

The following may be treated as Restricted Parties or Covered Associates where reasonably ascertainable and materially relevant:

- persons or entities exercising Control over Palantir;
- specifically designated beneficial owners with material governance influence;
- persons or entities financing a Restricted Project with knowledge of its restricted purpose; and
- intermediaries whose investment or ownership structure is materially used to circumvent ECL restrictions.

A future stable schedule may adopt a numerical beneficial-ownership threshold after legal review.

### 1.4 Customers and users of Palantir services

A person or organization does not become permanently restricted merely by having purchased, accessed, or incidentally used a Palantir product or service.

However, a project is a **Restricted Project** where Palantir software, services, personnel, contractors, or associated infrastructure materially participates in the project's design, operation, analysis, targeting, decision-making, deployment, or material outputs, and the ECL-covered Software would be used within or materially for that project.

This includes projects where a nominally independent participant uses ECL-covered Software while another material collaborator provides Palantir-based infrastructure or Palantir-connected capabilities to the same project.

### 1.5 Joint projects and collaboration rule

If an otherwise permitted entity participates in a project together with:

- Palantir Technologies Inc.;
- an entity covered by Section 1;
- a Restricted Party; or
- a Covered Associate acting in the restricted capacity,

then the project is a Restricted Project where that restricted participant has Material Participation.

This restriction applies to the **project**, not automatically to every unrelated activity of every project participant.

### 1.6 Anti-circumvention

A Restricted Project remains restricted when ECL-covered Software is introduced through a subcontractor, university, consultancy, open-source intermediary, affiliate, customer, cloud provider, integration partner, or other third party for the purpose or substantial effect of doing indirectly what the applicable ECL terms prohibit directly.

## 2. Governments and public bodies

No government is automatically restricted in this draft merely by nationality, geopolitical alignment, or form of government.

Governments, agencies, armed forces, intelligence bodies, police bodies, public contractors, or other state-associated entities may be added under the same evidence-based exergic criteria as any private organization.

Relevant grounds include material and systematic participation in ECL prohibited uses such as repressive surveillance, unlawful persecution, coercive targeting, systematic destruction of fundamental agency, or deliberate circumvention.

Future government designations must identify the exact legal or organizational body where reasonably possible rather than using an unnecessarily broad population-level designation.
