## CODEX-0.3-022 — P1 — Reserve fallback identities across both component slots

**Finding.** The pass-11 fix reserved canonical fallback identities only within their same-named manifest slots. An ordinary date-shaped Bundle could therefore place the registered empty Schedule identity in `license`, or the registered draft License identity in `schedule`, and evade both schema and runtime checks. Partial borrowing of only a reserved ref/path/hash across slots was also possible.

**Disposition: resolved in candidate.**

- `tools/ecl_resolve.py` now builds one global reserved namespace from every registered canonical-empty License and Schedule component across `ref`, `path`, and SHA-256 dimensions.
- Both manifest slots are checked against that global namespace for every non-canonical Bundle.
- `schemas/bundle.schema.json` mirrors the same cross-slot rule through a reusable reserved-fallback identity definition applied to both `license` and `schedule`.
- `tests/test_codex_pass12_regressions.py` exercises complete Schedule→License and License→Schedule swaps plus partial cross-slot borrowing.

Primary fix commits: `01e8ecb4330e08cb5ed32640bf8ce6f5e000fb7f`, `92ced5315c7af780d749d5f6f77d121195b6a983`, with regression coverage finalized at `7437cd9517c4f15259ea7121749bd1bb8a0cb40d`.

## CODEX-0.3-023 — P2 — Keep the mutable draft channel and Schedule compatibility metadata aligned with LICENSE

**Finding.** The working `LICENSE` had advanced to Version 0.3 — DRAFT while `channels/draft.json` still advertised `ECL-0.2-DRAFT`, and `tools/render_schedule.py` plus the stored partial Schedule still declared ECL 0.2-DRAFT-only compatibility. A consumer following the documented mutable development pointer could therefore associate the current 0.3 draft text with stale 0.2 compatibility metadata.

**Disposition: resolved in candidate.**

- `channels/draft.json` now points its mutable License ref at `ECL-0.3-DRAFT` while remaining non-operative and bundle-less.
- `tools/render_schedule.py` now emits `ECL 0.3-DRAFT only` compatibility.
- `schedules/ECL-RP-0.5-PARTIAL-DRAFT.md` carries the same 0.3-DRAFT compatibility label and its remaining 0.2-specific review wording was advanced to 0.3.
- `tests/test_codex_pass12_regressions.py` asserts the working License, draft channel, renderer output, and stored Schedule stay aligned.

Primary fix commits: `35b1f235bf976201c7f7752131a825b5f72cb7ee`, `0a6d5e50d52d27162d8e762697ae3c9da1aeb1c7`, `f86e1f067ebc44e8640e848fcd176226c8c5b85b`, with regression coverage finalized at `7437cd9517c4f15259ea7121749bd1bb8a0cb40d`.
