## CODEX-0.3-021 — Reject fallback components under ordinary Bundle IDs

Severity: P1
Disposition: candidate resolution implemented

Codex identified the inverse of CODEX-0.3-020. The runtime canonical-empty allowlist was entered only when the manifest's Bundle identifier itself contained `@RP-EMPTY-`; therefore a manifest could attempt to relabel the canonical ECL 0.3-DRAFT License + `ECL-RP-EMPTY-1` tuple under an ordinary date-shaped Bundle ID. The schema likewise constrained the exact fallback tuple only under `ECL-0.3-DRAFT@RP-EMPTY-1`.

Candidate resolution:

- canonical fallback identifiers **and their reserved component identities are indivisible**;
- `tools/ecl_resolve.py` now rejects an ordinary Bundle when any `ref`, `path`, or `sha256` dimension of its License or Schedule borrows a component identity reserved by a registered canonical empty fallback;
- a registered fallback still requires exact equality of Bundle ID, `operative` state, License component, and Schedule component;
- an unregistered `@RP-EMPTY-N` identifier remains rejected;
- `schemas/bundle.schema.json` mirrors the invariant: outside the exact registered fallback Bundle, the canonical ECL 0.3-DRAFT License ref/path/hash and canonical Empty-1 Schedule ref/path/hash are reserved and invalid;
- tests now cover relabelling the complete fallback tuple under an ordinary date-shaped Bundle ID and mixed attacks where only the License or only the Schedule remains a reserved fallback component.

This closes the identified path for an ordinary-looking manifest to reuse the draft fallback tuple, attach `operative: true`, and attempt to route it through the ordinary release/legal-review path.
