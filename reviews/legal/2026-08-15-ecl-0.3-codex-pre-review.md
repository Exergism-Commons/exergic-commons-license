## CODEX-0.3-001 — Dynamic-Schedule retroactivity escape

**Severity:** P1 / candidate BLOCKER

**Attack:** The Schedule definition said a later Schedule did not retroactively alter an earlier release **unless that earlier release itself expressly and validly provided otherwise**. That caveat could be read to revive a dynamic/future-Schedule mechanism despite the same draft rejecting mutable `latest`/registry/channel state and despite the exact-Bundle/non-retroactivity model.

**Resolution in candidate:** The caveat was removed. The Schedule definition now states that a later Schedule **never** retroactively alters Licensed Rights or restrictions attached to an earlier release; changing Schedule requires a new exact ECL Bundle for a new Software release.

**Status:** resolved in drafting; still subject to qualified legal review under LAR-08.

## CODEX-0.3-002 — Downstream empty-Schedule laundering

**Severity:** P1 / candidate BLOCKER

**Attack:** The first empty-Schedule fallback applied whenever `the Licensor` supplied release materials without an exact Schedule. Because a downstream modifier can itself be a Licensor for its additions, a downstream release could omit inherited Schedule metadata and argue that the Schedule for the whole derivative release became empty. Combined with direct downstream grants, this risked laundering away an upstream Bundle even though the omission itself breached redistribution conditions.

**Resolution in candidate:** The fallback is now strictly per original Licensor/per grant/per material/per release:

- it applies only when a Licensor originally applies ECL to material over which that Licensor controls the relevant Licensed Rights;
- it empties only that Licensor's own Schedule for that own grant/material/release;
- it cannot erase, replace, weaken or re-resolve an upstream Bundle;
- downstream distributors must preserve every applicable upstream Bundle;
- omission/corruption is a breach, not a grant expansion; and
- downstream omission cannot give a recipient broader rights than the upstream Licensor granted.

**Status:** resolved in drafting; still subject to qualified review under LAR-03/LAR-08 and #209.

## CODEX-0.3-003 — Mandatory gate references drift after renumbering

**Severity:** P1 / release-process BLOCKER

**Attack:** ECL 0.3 inserted statutory-rights, patent and trademark sections, shifting warranty/liability/severability/exact-Bundle section numbers. The first legal-review specification hard-coded old section numbers, so a qualified reviewer could mechanically disposition all LAR identifiers while reviewing the wrong clauses.

**Resolution:** Implemented in the separate release-gate fix PR #215. `spec/LEGAL-ADVERSARIAL-REVIEW.md` now binds attack surfaces to **operative subjects/headings rather than section numbers** and requires the final review record to map each LAR surface to the exact provision heading/location in the content-addressed candidate. The Schedule/non-retroactivity, warranty/liability, severability, patent and other surfaces are expressed semantically.

This is kept separate from PR #212 because it corrects the reusable legal-review process rather than the ECL 0.3 operative draft itself. PR #215 must merge before #212 is considered ready.

**Status:** resolved in review-process candidate; pending PR #215 merge/CI/adversarial review.
