# ECL State Adjudication 2026

> **Status: PROVISIONAL GOVERNANCE RECORD — NO LICENSING EFFECT BY ITSELF.**

Research cutoff: **2026-08-11**  
Coverage: **195 / 195 State dossiers** (193 UN Member States, the State of Palestine, and the Holy See).

This document records a first complete State-by-State adjudication under the Exergic Commons License (ECL) governance standard. It does **not** use Freedom House, V-Dem, geopolitical alignment, ideology, nationality, religion, or any external ranking as a decision rule. External reports, courts, UN mechanisms, official investigations, public records, reproducible research and credible investigative reporting are evidence inputs only.

A result becomes legally operative for a software release only if a specific Restricted Party or class is separately incorporated into the versioned `RESTRICTED-PARTIES.md` Schedule adopted by that release. These findings concern governmental institutions, organs, projects and attributable conduct — **never populations or nationalities**.

## Method

Each dossier was evaluated against `LICENSE` §5 and `EXERGIC-GOVERNANCE.md`, asking whether available evidence supports a reasoned conclusion of material and systematic participation in repressive surveillance, coercive targeting/unlawful violence, systematic discriminatory domination, deceptive population manipulation, irreversible coercive capture, or circumvention. The review also considers institutional attribution, persistence, severity, counter-evidence, functioning redress mechanisms, fragmented/de facto authority, and whether a narrower project/agency finding is more accurate than a whole-State designation.

Outcomes:

- **State apparatus Restricted (`R`)**
- **Restricted with defined scope (`S`)**
- **Under Review / insufficient evidence (`U`)**
- **No current state-level basis (`N`)**

## Interpretation and limits

- `N` is not an ethical endorsement. It means the evidence reviewed did not establish the ECL threshold for a State-level or defined scoped restriction at the cutoff.
- `U` records material concerns but insufficient evidence, attribution, persistence, or precision for a stable designation.
- `S` means specific organs, projects, security systems, detention systems, surveillance systems, military structures or other defined functions meet the threshold, while unrelated State functions do not.
- `R` means the coercive pattern is sufficiently material, systematic, persistent and cross-institutional to justify treating the State/governing apparatus itself as the provisional Restricted Party under ECL governance.
- Fragmented States and territories are not treated as unitary merely for convenience; conduct of de facto or non-State armed authorities is separately attributed wherever necessary.
- All findings are reviewable. New primary evidence, institutional reform, accountability, cessation of conduct, factual error or changed attribution can justify narrowing, removal, escalation or reclassification.
