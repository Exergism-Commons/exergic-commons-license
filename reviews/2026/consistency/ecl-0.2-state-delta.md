## 1. Purpose

This delta audit follows completion of factual/adversarial normalization for all 195 State dossiers and the global license-fit audit in `global-license-fit-audit.md`.

It does **not** re-run 195 factual investigations from zero. It asks whether the current outcome and scope of every State dossier remains coherent after ECL 0.2-DRAFT:

- translated the existing non-domination principle into operative §5.6;
- clarified direct Scheduled Restricted Projects;
- added the Independent Remediation Activity boundary; and
- strengthened the narrowest-accurate-attribution / Schedule-knowability rules.

Pre-delta distribution: **`R 36 / S 84 / U 28 / N 47 = 195`.**

## 2. Audit invariants

The following rules control the delta:

1. **A new clause is not new evidence.** ECL 0.2 may make existing conduct fit the license more clearly, but it cannot supply missing deployment, attribution, persistence or severity.
2. **No rights issue is automatically an ECL issue.** Capital punishment, detention, deportation, conscription, protest regulation and other coercive State action remain non-automatic; the qualifying project must satisfy an operative §5 criterion.
3. **Counter-institutions affect scope.** A whole-apparatus designation is overbroad where materially independent courts, auditors or other functions remain institutionally distinct and produce real contrary outcomes.
4. **Independent Remediation Activity is a safe harbour, not a substitute for scope discipline.** It protects genuinely independent remedial uses but does not justify designating unrelated ordinary functions where a narrower party/project can be identified.
5. **Project → agency/unit → coercive apparatus → whole State/apparatus** remains the escalation order.

## 4. `R → S` — United States (`USA`)

### Prior scope

`United States federal governmental apparatus`.

### Delta problem

The factual dossier itself recognizes materially independent federal counter-functions: the Federal Judiciary, GAO, inspectors general, prosecutors investigating official abuse, public defenders and other remedial functions. Official federal sources describe the Judiciary as a separate/independent branch and GAO as an independent non-partisan accountability agency.

ECL 0.2's Independent Remediation Activity rule protects some of those functions when remedial, but that safe harbour does not justify blocking unrelated ordinary uses of institutions that are not themselves materially participating in the qualifying coercive project.

### Delta result

**`R → S`.** Scope follows identified current federal immigration/detention, unlawful military-targeting and other coercive-security projects and their materially participating agencies/units. It does not generically designate all three federal branches or unrelated civilian programmes.

This is a scope correction, not a finding that the qualifying evidence disappeared.

## 5. `R → S` — Israel (`ISR`)

### Prior scope

`State governmental/legal apparatus`.

### Delta problem

The dossier recognizes judicial review and the State Comptroller/Ombudsman as materially distinct accountability functions. The Comptroller's legal independence from the Government and executive-audit role make a blanket governmental/legal-party class broader than necessary.

The evidence continues to support specific occupation/settlement-administration, military/security/intelligence, detention/prison, displacement and discriminatory projects. The question is attribution level, not disappearance of the underlying evidence.

### Delta result

**`R → S`.** Scope follows exact organs/projects materially participating in qualifying §5.1, §5.2 or §5.6 conduct. Independent judicial/audit/remedial functions and unrelated civilian administration are outside the generic class absent their own project-specific evidence.

## 6. Singapore (`SGP`) — `S` upheld, execution basis removed

POFMA/information-control and qualifying public-order enforcement can independently engage **§5.6 unlawful political repression** where they materially criminalize or systematically suppress protected political, journalistic, rights or peaceful civic activity.

However, ECL 0.2 expressly states that capital punishment is not a standalone prohibited-use category. The previous Singapore scope therefore improperly treated `capital-punishment/execution projects` as independently ECL-relevant merely because executions occurred.

**Result: uphold `S`, narrow scope.** Capital punishment remains a human-rights fact but is not an ECL designation basis unless a particular execution-related system independently satisfies §5.2, §5.6 or another operative criterion.

Japan (`JPN`) remains `U`, making the cross-dossier standard consistent: the existence of executions does not by itself determine an ECL tier.

### License-fit families

The audit confirms at least one operative 0.2 theory for every retained scoped dossier. The theories are not mutually exclusive:

- **Direct surveillance / data / information-system fit:** repressive spyware, biometric/facial-recognition, algorithmic welfare profiling, discriminatory data systems, targeted cyber/information control or comparable projects can fit §§5.1, 5.3, 5.5 and/or 5.6 depending on facts.
- **Unlawful force / disappearance / conflict targeting:** military, police, drone, torture/disappearance and targeting projects can fit §§5.2 and/or 5.6.
- **Political detention / prosecution / civic repression:** arbitrary detention, systematic criminalization of protected dissent, severe public-order repression and comparable coercive projects can fit §5.6 even when surveillance is not the mechanism.
- **Migration / forced transfer / detention:** these remain scoped only where the project materially implements arbitrary detention, persecution, severe discriminatory domination, collective/summary forced displacement or expulsion without meaningful individualized protection, unlawful violence or another exact criterion. Restrictive migration policy by itself is insufficient.
- **Forced labour / coercive labour:** projects are ECL-relevant only where the §5.6 threshold and actor/project attribution are materially established.
- **Forced eviction / territorial coercion:** property or development disputes are not automatically ECL issues; scoped findings survive only where the evidence establishes qualifying forced displacement, severe discriminatory domination, unlawful violence or comparable coercive denial of agency.

### Important examples

- `ISL` survives under §5.6 only because its scope concerns specifically evidenced pretrial solitary-confinement practices potentially amounting to arbitrary detention or comparably severe ill-treatment; solitary confinement is not prohibited merely because it exists.
- `LTU`, `MLT`, `HRV`, `CYP`, `POL`, `DOM` and other migration/border scopes survive only to the extent their dossiers establish qualifying summary/collective forced transfer, arbitrary detention, serious discriminatory domination, unlawful force or denial of meaningful individualized protection.
- `UKR` is already aligned: ordinary lawful conscription/recruitment is excluded; only substantiated abusive TCC confinement/violence or separately qualifying security/prosecution projects remain in scope.
- `DNK` and `NLD` retain direct §5.3-style relevance through automated welfare-risk/discriminatory administrative systems.
- `FRA` and `SRB` retain direct technology-specific surveillance/public-order relevance.

No existing `S` is upheld merely because the country has a poor human-rights record.

## 9. Full `U` cohort — 28 retained after 0.2 recheck

All current `U` dossiers were re-read for this delta because §5.6 could, in principle, resolve cases previously blocked by the narrower 0.1 wording:

`AUS, AUT, BEL, CAN, CHL, CRI, DJI, FIN, GMB, HND, JPN, LVA, LBN, MRT, MNG, NRU, NPL, NOR, PNG, ROU, WSM, SEN, SLE, ZAF, ESP, CHE, TTO, URY`.

**Result: all 28 remain `U`.**

The remaining uncertainty is factual or attribution-based rather than merely lexical:

- deployment remains prospective or insufficiently current (`FIN`, `WSM`, parts of `AUT`/`SLE`);
- current recurrence/persistence is insufficient (`CHL`, `LVA`, `LBN`, `HND`, `NPL`, `TTO`);
- attribution is fragmented or insufficiently granular (`AUS`, `DJI`, `MRT`, `NRU`, `PNG`, `ZAF`);
- substantial counter-institutions/remediation prevent stable escalation (`BEL`, `CAN`, `MNG`, `NOR`, `ROU`, `SEN`, `ESP`, `CHE`, `URY`); or
- the new criterion changes the legal question but the evidence is still insufficient for a stable scoped designation (`JPN`).

### Japan consistency result

ECL 0.2 means systematic arbitrary detention can be relevant under §5.6 without requiring spyware or an automated system. Japan's prolonged pretrial detention/`hostage justice` therefore has a clearer potential ECL theory than under 0.1. The dossier nevertheless remains `U` because the current record does not yet close systematicity/attribution/remedy strongly enough for a stable `S`.

Capital punishment itself remains outside the standalone criterion.
