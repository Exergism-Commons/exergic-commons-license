# Maven Smart System

> **Governance record only.** No licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**U — Under Review.** Maven Smart System is a large current military software/AI programme with substantial Palantir participation. Military targeting capability and defense contracting are not standalone ECL prohibited uses; a Restricted Project finding requires proof that an exact Maven deployment materially enables unlawful targeting or another ECL §5 use.

## 2. Exact project identity and boundary

U.S. Department of Defense contract records identify **Maven Smart System** software awards to Palantir USG Inc., including a $480 million 2024 contract and a $795 million 2025 modification, with performance extending to 2029.

The project boundary for ECL purposes must be deployment-specific: generic Maven software/platform capability is broader than any one targeting chain.

## 3. ECL criteria tested

Potential fit is principally **§5.2 Coercive targeting** where an exact deployment materially facilitates unlawful violence or intentional attacks against protected persons. §5.6 may apply to a deployment used for systematic coercive domination.

Ordinary target identification, intelligence analysis or military operations do not satisfy ECL merely because they use AI/data analytics.

## 4. Material participants and roles

- U.S. Department of Defense / U.S. Army — operator/customer structures reflected in public contracts.
- Palantir USG Inc. — software contractor/licensor reflected in DoD awards.

Other participants require deployment-specific evidence.

## 5. Evidence supporting continued review

Public DoD awards prove substantial, current Palantir participation in Maven. The broader U.S. State dossier includes current military-targeting projects that require project-level scrutiny where authoritative evidence identifies unlawful targeting/civilian-harm failures.

## 6. Counter-evidence / causation limits

The contract records do not establish that Maven itself selected an unlawful target, caused civilian harm or was used in every military operation. Palantir disputes claims concerning certain alleged automated targeting systems in Israel and no inference about those systems is imported here.

The existence of a targeting platform cannot substitute for proof of the exact prohibited deployment.

## 7. Attribution and exclusions

Generic Maven development, testing, logistics and lawful deployments remain outside a stable ECL restriction absent project-specific evidence. Any restricted phase should identify the operation/targeting chain, timeframe and materially participating technical components.

## 8. Adversarial determination

**RETAIN `U`.** Current evidence proves capability and participation but not a sufficiently exact prohibited deployment to designate Maven Smart System wholesale.

## 9. Review / removal triggers

Escalate when authoritative investigation links a defined Maven deployment or workflow to conduct satisfying §5.2/§5.6 and establishes material technical participation. Narrow/remove on verified remediation or separation of affected workflows.
