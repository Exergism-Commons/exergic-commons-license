# Mitiga Prison / SDF-RADA detention apparatus

> **Governance record only.** No licensing effect by itself. The criminal allegations described below remain allegations unless adjudicated.

## 1. Current determination

**R — Restricted Project candidate.** The exact detention apparatus is a narrower and more knowable project than an unbounded Libyan actor class. The ICC warrant record describes alleged severe coercive conduct occurring through Mitiga Prison over a prolonged period and connects that conduct to SDF/RADA operational structures.

## 2. Exact project identity and boundary

The project is the **Mitiga Prison detention/security apparatus** operated through SDF/RADA structures in Tripoli, including operational processes materially continuous with the detention conduct described in the ICC record.

This project entry does not cover every prison, police function or armed group in Libya.

## 3. ECL criteria engaged

- **§5.6 Systematic coercive domination** — alleged prolonged imprisonment, torture/cruel treatment, persecution and coercive detention based on religion/belief, perceived morality, alleged armed affiliation or coercive purposes.
- **§5.2 Coercive targeting** where operational processes materially enable alleged murder/sexual violence or other unlawful violence against detainees.

## 4. Material participants and roles

The ICC public record identifies **Special Deterrence Forces / RADA** members as alleged participants and describes Osama Elmasry Njeem as alleged to have been in charge of prison facilities/associated conduct. Person-level ECL status remains separately `U` pending the heightened individual standard.

Other contractors, authorities or suppliers require their own Material Participation evidence.

## 5. Evidence supporting the determination

The ICC states that its January 2025 warrant concerns alleged crimes at Mitiga Prison from 2015 onwards, including imprisonment, torture, cruel treatment, murder, rape/sexual violence and persecution. It states that the alleged conduct was committed personally/ordered by Njeem or with his assistance by SDF/RADA members.

The described victim-selection grounds include religion/belief, perceived violation of SDF/RADA ideology, alleged affiliation with other armed groups and coercive purposes.

## 6. Counter-evidence / due-process limits

The ICC warrant is not a final merits judgment. The ECL project determination therefore addresses the detention apparatus as a licensing-risk project based on an authoritative reasonable-grounds record; it does not declare individual criminal guilt.

A stable Schedule should include review/removal mechanics tied to judicial developments and independently verified project reform/cessation.

## 7. Attribution and exclusions

Scope is limited to Mitiga detention/security operational processes and materially continuous SDF/RADA structures implicated in the described conduct. Other Libyan prisons, authorities, civilians and unrelated functions are excluded.

## 8. Adversarial determination

**R at project level; SDF/RADA remains `S` at organization level.** This combination is more precise than making every SDF/RADA function a whole-organization `R` while allowing the specifically evidenced detention project to receive the strongest project outcome.

## 9. Review / removal triggers

Review on final ICC/judicial findings, closure/reform of Mitiga detention operations, independently verified release/remediation, command/organizational succession, or material contrary evidence.
