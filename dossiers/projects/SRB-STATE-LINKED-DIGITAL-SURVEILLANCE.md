# Serbia State-linked digital-surveillance campaign

> **Governance record only.** This dossier has no licensing effect by itself. It captures the project/campaign boundary already reviewed for Serbia and does not classify Serbian police/intelligence activity generally.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Scoped project/campaign candidate.** The canonical Serbia review preserves only BIA/police or State-linked projects materially using spyware/mobile-forensic access for unlawful surveillance of journalists, activists or lawful opposition, plus separately substantiated protest-repression operations.

This dossier covers the digital-surveillance project family only; it does not absorb every protest-policing allegation into the same technical object.

## 2. Exact project identity and boundary

The tracked object is the **State-linked digital-surveillance campaign documented in the Serbia review**, including reviewed NoviSpy/mobile-forensic targeting and the documented Pegasus targeting of BIRN journalists where the source record found a strong likelihood of Serbian State-actor involvement.

The boundary is use/deployment-specific. `NoviSpy`, `Pegasus`, mobile-forensic tooling, BIA/police and supplier names are not themselves blanket Restricted Parties or Projects through mere association.

## 3. ECL criteria engaged

The existing review tests unlawful/repressive surveillance of journalists, activists and lawful opposition and related coercive civic-space effects.

## 4. Material participants and roles

The reviewed record refers to BIA/police or other State-linked actors at the campaign level. Exact organizational/person/vendor participation remains proposition-specific and is not inferred here.

No semantic Claim in this tranche asserts that NSO Group, Cellebrite or another vendor controls, operates or materially participates in this project.

## 5. Evidence supporting the determination

Amnesty forensic work, as summarized in the canonical Serbia dossier and scoped adversarial review, documented systematic use of NoviSpy and mobile-forensic tools against activists/journalists. Later forensic reporting found two BIRN journalists targeted with Pegasus in February 2025 and assessed a strong likelihood of Serbian State-actor involvement.

The 2026 review also records continuing intimidation, harassment, unlawful surveillance and police repression through April 2026 without an identified independent Serbian investigation producing accountability or demonstrated cessation of the State-linked surveillance campaign.

## 6. Counter-evidence / remediation

Cellebrite halted product use in Serbia following the surveillance reporting, demonstrating that supplier intervention can materially reduce one technical capability. Serbian law formally regulates police/intelligence activity and independent journalism/civil society continues exposing abuse.

The supplier intervention is preserved as counter/remediation evidence; it does not erase other reviewed surveillance evidence and does not make Cellebrite a Serbian Restricted Party.

## 7. Attribution and exclusions

Excluded absent separate evidence: ordinary Serbian law-enforcement/intelligence functions, unrelated police activity, journalists/activists as targets, software suppliers by identity, former personnel, and any deployment independently shown to have ceased or been remediated.

Tracking this project from `STATE-SRB` creates review relevance only, not State-wide guilt or inherited restriction.

## 8. Adversarial determination

**Preserve the already-reviewed narrow digital-surveillance `S` boundary; reject supplier and agency-wide inheritance.** Exact vendor/project participation remains available for later Claim curation if the repository acquires sufficiently explicit evidence.

## 9. Review / removal triggers

Independent investigation/accountability, verified cessation of unlawful spyware/mobile-forensic targeting and durable safeguards for journalists, activists and lawful opposition.
