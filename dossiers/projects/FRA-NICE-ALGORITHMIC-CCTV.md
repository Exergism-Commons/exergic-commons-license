# Nice algorithmic public-street CCTV processing at school entrances

> **Governance record only.** This dossier has no licensing effect by itself. It exists specifically so remediation/removal evidence remains first-class and auditable after the project left the France scoped finding.

## 1. Current determination

**N — No current project-level ECL basis in the reviewed record.** On 30 January 2026 the Conseil d'Etat held that Nice's algorithmic processing of public-street CCTV at school entrances was not authorized under existing law. The canonical France adversarial review therefore expressly removed this project from the current scoped finding.

`N` here does not adjudicate the legality or ECL status of other French algorithmic video-surveillance systems.

## 2. Exact project identity and boundary

This dossier is limited to the **Nice municipal algorithmic processing of public-street CCTV images at school entrances** addressed by the Conseil d'Etat decision referenced in the France dossier.

It is not the Olympic-derived national VSA experiment, later national legislative proposals, all Nice CCTV, or French public-order policing generally.

## 3. ECL criteria engaged

The project was reviewed in the context of algorithmic public-space surveillance. The current record does not maintain a project restriction because an effective judicial/remedial institution blocked the processing under the existing legal framework.

## 4. Material participants and roles

The canonical record identifies the Nice municipal system as the object of the judicial decision. This dossier does not create actor-level `operates`, `controls`, supplier or contractor Claims.

## 5. Evidence supporting the current determination

The Conseil d'Etat decision is directly relevant primary judicial material establishing the narrow proposition that the specified Nice algorithmic public-street CCTV processing was not authorized under the law then in force.

The France State dossier and scoped adversarial review treat that judicial block as binding counter-evidence with operative analytical effect and explicitly remove the Nice project from current scope.

## 6. Counter-evidence / historical concern

The project entered review because it involved algorithmic processing of public-street CCTV. That historical concern remains auditable, but it cannot override a current project record showing effective judicial blockage/remediation.

Other national VSA systems require their own exact identity and current-deployment evidence.

## 7. Attribution and exclusions

Excluded: the national Olympic-derived VSA experiment, proposed national extensions, ordinary CCTV without the reviewed algorithmic processing, and unrelated French/municipal activity.

Tracking this project from `STATE-FRA` preserves review/remediation history only. It does not mean France currently operates a Restricted Project.
