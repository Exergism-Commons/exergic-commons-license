# Islamic State in Iraq and the Levant (ISIL / Da'esh)

> **Governance record only.** No licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**R — Restricted Party candidate.** The organization itself is the narrowest accurate actor given authoritative records describing core participation in planning, facilitating, perpetrating, recruiting for and supporting violent operations.

## 2. Exact identity and knowability

The UN Security Council's current ISIL/Al-Qaida sanctions architecture identifies ISIL through the **Al-Qaida in Iraq** entry, permanent reference **QDe.115**, and related current aliases/derivatives. A future Schedule should state the exact aliases/reference used and must not silently absorb every affiliate carrying `ISIL` in its name.

## 3. ECL criteria engaged

The principal fit is **§5.2 Coercive targeting**, with **§5.6** applicable to systematic coercive domination/persecution and **§5.7** to circumvention.

## 4. Evidence supporting the determination

The UN Security Council narrative record for QDe.115 identifies the organization as participating in financing, planning, facilitating, preparing or perpetrating violent acts/activities and otherwise supporting Al-Qaida-linked operations. The narrative summary was updated again in May 2026, confirming the current identity record.

## 5. Counter-evidence and scope discipline

UN sanctions are not automatically ECL law. The ECL outcome rests on the underlying core organizational conduct described by the official record.

Affiliates, territorial branches, individuals and associated entities require independent ECL identity/scope treatment unless a future Schedule defines a reasonably knowable controlled class.

## 6. Attribution and exclusions

The entry concerns ISIL/Da'esh itself. It never restricts Muslims, Iraqis, Syrians, residents of former ISIL-controlled areas, humanitarian actors, victims or persons merely alleged to have contact with the organization.

## 7. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD `R`; reject automatic inheritance by all UN-listed associates.** The core organization is a sufficiently coherent Restricted Party candidate, but external list association is not recursively transitive under ECL.

## 8. Review / removal triggers

Review on dissolution, successor-identity questions or evidence that a named successor is organizationally distinct. Successor/affiliate status must remain knowable.
