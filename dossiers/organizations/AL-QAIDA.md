# Al-Qaida

> **Governance record only.** No licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**R — Restricted Party candidate.** The organization itself is the narrowest accurate unit because its documented organizational activity includes planning, facilitating, perpetrating and supporting violent operations that directly fit ECL coercive-targeting criteria.

## 2. Exact identity and knowability

The UN Security Council ISIL (Da'esh) and Al-Qaida Sanctions List identifies **AL-QAIDA**, permanent reference **QDe.004**. A future ECL Schedule can use that permanent reference as an identity anchor while remaining independently governed by ECL criteria.

Other listed affiliates are not automatically part of this ECL entry merely because the UN describes them as associated with Al-Qaida.

## 3. ECL criteria engaged

The principal fit is **§5.2 Coercive targeting**, including operational support for intentional attacks/unlawful violence, with **§5.6** relevant for systematic coercive domination and **§5.7** for deliberate circumvention.

## 4. Evidence supporting the determination

The UN Security Council's narrative summary records Al-Qaida's listing for participating in financing, planning, facilitating, preparing or perpetrating acts/activities and otherwise supporting violent activities associated with its network. The current sanctions list continues to identify Al-Qaida under QDe.004.

The ECL conclusion relies on the underlying organizational conduct described in the official record, not merely on the fact of UN sanctions.

## 5. Counter-evidence and scope discipline

UN sanctions criteria are not identical to ECL. The current whole-organization `R` is justified because the evidence concerns core organizational planning/support activity rather than an isolated branch/customer/project.

No nationality, religion, community, charity, individual member, former associate or remote affiliate is restricted solely by proximity to Al-Qaida.

## 6. Attribution and exclusions

This dossier concerns Al-Qaida itself. Affiliates, splinters and associated actors require either:

- their own exact ECL dossier/Schedule entry;
- proof they are controlled by Al-Qaida within a knowable class; or
- project-specific Covered Associate/Material Participation treatment under the operative license.

The full UN ISIL/Al-Qaida list is **not** incorporated automatically.

## 7. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD `R`; reject blanket import of all UN-associated entries.** The core organization is supported by direct organizational evidence, while ECL's anti-recursion and knowability rules require independent treatment of other actors.

## 8. Review / removal triggers

Review on dissolution, organizational succession, identity changes or material evidence that the named organization no longer exists as the same operational actor. Successor entities require their own identity analysis.
