# Rapid Support Forces (RSF)

> **Governance record only.** No licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**R — Restricted Party candidate.** Current UN Special Procedures records attribute severe current detention/torture and conflict-abuse allegations directly to the RSF as an armed organization, supporting organization-level rather than merely isolated-project attribution.

## 2. Exact identity and knowability

The intended actor is the **Rapid Support Forces (RSF)** in Sudan. A future Schedule should separately address controlled/associated militias only where control or Material Participation is reasonably knowable; the label `RSF-associated` alone is insufficient.

## 3. ECL criteria engaged

- **§5.2 Coercive targeting** — unlawful attacks/killing and operational support where substantiated.
- **§5.6 Systematic coercive domination** — arbitrary detention, torture/ill-treatment, enforced disappearance, forced displacement, sexual violence/persecution and comparable coercion.

## 4. Evidence supporting the determination

A May 2026 UN Special Procedures communication to the RSF describes serious allegations that approximately **9,000 persons** were arbitrarily detained at Shala Prison in El Fasher, with death in custody and detention conditions amounting to torture/ill-treatment and life-threatening deprivation.

Other UN communications describe allegations against RSF forces involving killings, enforced disappearance, torture, sexual and gender-based violence, displacement and destruction/looting in the continuing Sudan conflict.

The current evidence is directed to the RSF organization and recurring operations rather than a single historical unit.

## 5. Counter-evidence and scope discipline

Allegations in Special Procedures communications are not criminal convictions and must be described as allegations. RSF public directives or claimed disciplinary commitments are relevant counter-evidence but have not rebutted the recurring current pattern documented in the reviewed records.

The entry does not automatically encompass every person living in RSF-controlled territory or every local armed group alleged to cooperate with RSF.

## 6. Attribution and exclusions

Scope follows RSF command, armed operations, detention facilities and materially controlled structures. Individuals or allied forces require their own identity/material-participation analysis unless clearly controlled within the same organization.

## 7. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD organization-level `R`.** Current detention evidence plus the broader recurring organizational abuse record makes a project-only finding artificially narrow. The exact Schedule should still avoid undefined `associated forces` classes.

## 8. Review / removal triggers

Review on ceasefire/dissolution/restructuring, verified command accountability, closure/remediation of abusive detention structures, release/remedy for arbitrary detainees, or evidence that successor armed entities are organizationally distinct.
