# Organization dossiers

Canonical evidence records for companies, armed organizations, governmental bodies and other organizational actors belong here.

A Schedule entry should not be created or carried forward merely because it existed in an older Schedule. Organization-level restriction requires a canonical ECL-native dossier and adversarial review.

Use [`_TEMPLATE.md`](_TEMPLATE.md).

## Outcomes

- **`R` — Restricted Party candidate:** the organization itself is the narrowest accurate actor because qualifying conduct is sufficiently central/systematic across the relevant organization.
- **`S` — Scoped organization restriction candidate:** only a business line, operational wing, controlled unit, professional capacity or defined class of projects qualifies.
- **`U` — Under Review:** substantial concerns exist but whole-entity/scoped attribution, current deployment, normative fit or counter-evidence prevents stable restriction.
- **`N` — No current organization-level basis:** current evidence does not establish an ECL designation basis.

These are governance outcomes only. Only an exact Schedule incorporated with an exact ECL version has licensing effect.

## Attribution rules

- Government/defense/customers are not automatically prohibited customers.
- Supplying dual-use software does not by itself make the supplier Restricted.
- A supplier may be a Material Participant / Covered Associate for a specific Restricted Project without becoming a permanent Restricted Party for unrelated activity.
- Controlled entities should be included only where control and identity are reasonably knowable.
- Former employees, passive shareholders, customers and remote affiliates do not inherit organizational status automatically.
