# Sudanese Armed Forces (SAF)

> **Governance record only.** No licensing effect by itself. This dossier is subordinate to the canonical Sudan State dossier for scope consistency.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Scoped organization candidate.** The canonical Sudan dossier already identifies defined SAF/state-aligned conflict and security operations that meet the ECL review threshold. This organization dossier exists to prevent the next Schedule from duplicating that State scope inconsistently.

## 2. Exact identity and knowability

The intended actor is the **Sudanese Armed Forces (SAF)**. Other armed groups, allied formations or security bodies require separate evidence of command/control or Material Participation and are not included merely by association.

## 3. ECL criteria engaged

The controlling `SDN.md` record maps the relevant current operations to ECL 0.2 criteria concerning qualifying coercive targeting and systematic coercive domination. Ordinary military activity is not an ECL prohibited use merely because it is military.

## 4. Evidence supporting review

UN Special Procedures records concerning Sudan describe current serious allegations involving civilians in the conflict and distinguish communications addressed to Sudan/State authorities from parallel communications addressed to the rival Rapid Support Forces. The canonical Sudan State dossier contains the detailed current evidentiary analysis.

## 5. Counter-evidence and scope discipline

The conflict is fragmented. Conduct attributed to RSF, other armed actors or non-State groups cannot be assigned to SAF merely because it occurred in Sudan. The organization entry must remain no broader than the State dossier's current SAF/state-aligned scope.

## 6. Attribution and exclusions

Scope follows only SAF units/commands or clearly State-aligned structures materially participating in the qualifying operations identified by the canonical State dossier. Unrelated civilian State functions, independent remediation and other armed actors are outside this organization entry.

## 7. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD `S` only as a synchronized organization view of `SDN.md`.** Reject the old Schedule approach of listing SAF separately without an explicit linkage to the State scope.

## 8. Review / removal triggers

Any change to the canonical Sudan scope should trigger synchronized review here. Organizational restructuring, command changes, verified remediation and cessation of the qualifying operations also require review.
