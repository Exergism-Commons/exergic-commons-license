# Hamas

> **Governance record only.** No licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Scoped organization restriction candidate.** Authoritative findings support restriction of Hamas structures materially involved in armed command, hostage-taking/detention, intentional civilian targeting and related coercive projects. The current record does not require an ECL inference that every political, social, administrative or unrelated function bearing the Hamas organizational label is the same prohibited apparatus.

## 2. Exact identity and knowability

A future Schedule should distinguish **Hamas** from its military wing, **Izz al-Din al-Qassam Brigades**, which has its own dossier. It should not restrict Palestinians, Gaza residents, public-sector workers, charities, civil institutions or persons merely because of geography, family relationship, political environment or non-material contact.

## 3. ECL criteria engaged

- **§5.2 Coercive targeting** — intentional attacks on civilians, hostage-taking-related operational support and unlawful violence.
- **§5.6 Systematic coercive domination** — torture/cruel treatment, enforced disappearance/hostage detention and comparable coercive denial of agency.
- **§5.7 Circumvention** where intermediaries deliberately enable an incorporated restriction to be evaded.

## 4. Evidence supporting the determination

The UN Independent International Commission of Inquiry found that the 7 October 2023 attack was led and coordinated by Hamas and implemented by the military wings of Hamas and other Palestinian armed groups. It found intentional targeting/killing of civilians, hostage-taking, torture/cruel treatment and other war crimes.

Later Commission reporting found that Hamas and other Palestinian armed groups committed torture, inhuman/cruel treatment and enforced disappearance against hostages, among other violations.

Those findings support ECL restriction of materially participating Hamas command/armed/detention structures and projects.

## 5. Counter-evidence and scope discipline

The UN findings concern specific attacks, armed wings and hostage/detention conduct; they do not establish that every organizational function or every person associated with Hamas materially participated in the same prohibited conduct.

ECL therefore uses `S` rather than automatically treating an undefined political/social ecosystem as one Restricted Party.

## 6. Attribution, projects and exclusions

Scope follows:

- armed-command structures materially directing or enabling intentional attacks on civilians;
- hostage-taking, hostage-detention and coercive interrogation/treatment structures;
- procurement/logistics/technical projects knowingly and materially enabling those prohibited operations; and
- other exact Hamas-controlled projects independently shown to satisfy ECL §5.

Civilian population, humanitarian activity, unrelated civil administration, political opinion and remote association are excluded.

## 7. Adversarial determination

**NARROW old whole-organization `R` concept to `S`.** The evidence strongly supports coercive Hamas projects and structures, while scope discipline counsels against presuming every organizational function is materially participating.

The military wing receives separate treatment in `IZZ-AL-DIN-AL-QASSAM-BRIGADES.md`.

## 8. Review / removal triggers

Re-review on organizational restructuring, independently verified cessation/release/remediation of hostage/detention projects, command changes, or new evidence clarifying which civilian/political functions are or are not materially controlled by prohibited operational structures.
