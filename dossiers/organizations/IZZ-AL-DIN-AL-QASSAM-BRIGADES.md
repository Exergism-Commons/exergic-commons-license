# Izz al-Din al-Qassam Brigades

> **Governance record only.** No licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**R — Restricted Party candidate.** The military wing itself is the narrowest accurate organizational actor because authoritative findings concern core armed-command and operational conduct rather than an incidental or peripheral business line.

## 2. Exact identity and knowability

The organization is commonly identified as the **Izz al-Din al-Qassam Brigades / al-Qassam Brigades**, the military wing of Hamas. A future Schedule should state the aliases it intends to cover and should not extend this entry to civilians or other Palestinian armed groups without separate evidence.

## 3. ECL criteria engaged

- **§5.2 Coercive targeting** — intentional attacks against civilians, hostage-taking and related operational support.
- **§5.6 Systematic coercive domination** — torture/cruel treatment, hostage detention/enforced disappearance and related coercion.

## 4. Evidence supporting the determination

The UN Independent International Commission of Inquiry found that the military wings of Hamas and other Palestinian armed groups intentionally targeted civilians on 7 October 2023, committed killings and hostage-taking, and engaged in torture/cruel treatment and other serious violations. The Commission found the attacks were led/coordinated by Hamas and implemented through its military wing and other armed groups.

ICC proceedings concerning Mohammed Deif, described by the Court as commander of the Hamas military wing/al-Qassam Brigades, likewise recorded reasonable grounds concerning murder, extermination, torture, rape/sexual violence and hostage-taking, although proceedings against Deif were later terminated after reported death. The person proceeding is not the basis for collective guilt; it corroborates the identity/command context of the armed wing.

## 5. Counter-evidence and scope discipline

This dossier does not treat every Hamas-associated person or Palestinian armed actor as a member of al-Qassam. The future Schedule must use exact organizational identity/aliases and should not infer membership from political sympathy, residence or family relationship.

## 6. Attribution and exclusions

The entry follows the military wing's command, combat, hostage/detention, targeting, logistics and technical structures when acting for the organization. Humanitarian actors, civilians, coerced persons and unrelated Palestinian institutions are excluded.

## 7. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD organization-level `R`.** Unlike the broader Hamas structure, the core identity of al-Qassam is the armed operational wing directly implicated in the qualifying conduct.

## 8. Review / removal triggers

Review on dissolution, merger/successor identity, meaningful organizational restructuring or reliable evidence that a successor body is legally/operationally distinct.
