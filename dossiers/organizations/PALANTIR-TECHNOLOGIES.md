# Palantir Technologies Inc.

> **Governance record only.** This dossier does not itself restrict Palantir or its software. Only an exact incorporated Schedule can create a Restricted Party or Restricted Project.

## 1. Current determination

**U — Under Review at organization level.** The whole-company `RESTRICTED` entry in `ECL-RP-0.4-DRAFT` does not survive ECL 0.2 scope review. Palantir has extensive participation in sensitive U.S. immigration, defense and targeting-related software programmes, but the current record does not show that Palantir Technologies Inc. as a whole is the narrowest accurate Restricted Party.

Specific Palantir-supported deployments can independently become Restricted Projects when the exact deployment is shown materially enabling an ECL §5 prohibited use.

## 2. Exact identity and knowability

The relevant public company is **Palantir Technologies Inc.** Public procurement also uses **Palantir USG Inc.** for U.S. government contracts. A future Schedule must distinguish the parent/public company, contracting subsidiaries and exact project participants rather than using an undefined controlled-affiliate class.

## 3. ECL 0.2 criteria tested

Review principally tests:

- §5.1 repressive surveillance;
- §5.2 coercive targeting;
- §5.6 systematic coercive domination/unlawful political repression; and
- §5.7 circumvention where relevant.

Government, defense, intelligence or immigration contracting is not prohibited merely because it is sensitive or dual-use.

## 4. Evidence supporting continued review

U.S. ICE procurement records identify a January 2026 sole-source procurement to Palantir Technologies for modernization of ICE's **Investigative Case Management (ICM)** and **Investigative Analytics (IA)** systems. ICE describes ICM as its official investigative case system, used to connect subject records, conduct target telephone/address searches and link investigations.

U.S. Department of Defense records show Palantir USG receiving major **Maven Smart System** software awards, including a $795 million modification in May 2025. The U.S. Army subsequently announced a 2025 enterprise agreement allowing Army/DoD purchases of Palantir products up to a $10 billion ceiling over as many as ten years.

These records establish substantial Material Participation in sensitive government projects. They do not by themselves establish that every deployment produces a Prohibited Use.

## 5. Counter-evidence and ordinary activity

Palantir's 2025 Form 10-K describes a broad commercial and public-sector business across healthcare, industry, insurance, telecommunications, automotive, aviation, space and other sectors. In 2025, 46% of revenue came from commercial customers and 54% from government customers.

Palantir also publicly disputes claims that it developed certain alleged automated targeting systems used by Israel and states that its current ICE work is intended to improve data quality and reduce erroneous immigration enforcement. Those claims do not resolve project-level concerns, but they are material counter-evidence against inferring prohibited use from contract existence alone.

## 6. Attribution, projects and exclusions

Current review follows exact Palantir/Palantir USG participation in:

- ICE ICM / Investigative Analytics and related enforcement-data projects;
- Maven Smart System deployments and any specific targeting chain shown to satisfy §5.2; and
- other named deployments where Palantir software/personnel materially enable qualifying surveillance, targeting, arbitrary detention, forced transfer or political repression.

Ordinary commercial deployments, public-health/scientific uses, unrelated government contracts, passive shareholders, former employees and customers are excluded absent their own material participation.

## 7. Adversarial determination

**REMOVE old whole-company `R`; retain `U` organization review.** Procurement evidence proves sensitive participation, not a company-wide prohibited purpose. ECL 0.2 requires project-level proof before Palantir is treated as a Restricted Party or Covered Associate for an exact project.

A future project dossier may establish a Restricted Project without changing this organization-level outcome.

## 8. Review / removal triggers

Escalate to `S` or `R` only if authoritative evidence shows that a defined Palantir business line or the company itself materially and systematically enables qualifying ECL conduct beyond isolated/project-specific customer use. Move toward `N` if sensitive projects cease or project review repeatedly fails to establish prohibited-use participation.
