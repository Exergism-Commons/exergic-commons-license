# Special Deterrence Forces / RADA

> **Governance record only.** No licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Scoped organization restriction candidate.** Current authoritative judicial material supports a defined finding around SDF/RADA's Mitiga detention/security apparatus and materially continuous structures implicated in severe coercive detention conduct. The record does not justify silently treating every actor using the RADA label or every unrelated security function as the same project.

## 2. Exact identity and knowability

The ICC refers to the **Special Deterrence Forces**, also known colloquially as **RADA**. A future Schedule should include the exact known aliases and avoid ambiguous expansion to unrelated Libyan forces.

## 3. ECL criteria engaged

- **§5.6 Systematic coercive domination** — arbitrary imprisonment, torture/cruel treatment, persecution and coercive detention.
- **§5.2 Coercive targeting** where specific operational conduct materially facilitates unlawful violence against protected persons.

## 4. Evidence supporting the determination

The ICC's January 2025 public record concerning the arrest warrant for Osama Elmasry Njeem states that the alleged crimes occurred at Mitiga Prison and included imprisonment, torture, cruel treatment, murder, rape/sexual violence and persecution. The Court states that the alleged crimes were committed personally, ordered by Njeem or committed with his assistance by members of the Special Deterrence Forces / RADA.

The ICC record identifies victims allegedly imprisoned for religion/belief, perceived contraventions of SDF/RADA ideology, alleged affiliation with other armed groups or coercive purposes.

## 5. Counter-evidence and due-process limits

The ICC record concerns allegations supporting an arrest warrant; it is not a conviction of Njeem or a judgment establishing criminal liability for every SDF/RADA member. ECL therefore scopes the organization finding to the documented detention/security apparatus and current materially continuous structures rather than inferring personal guilt across membership.

## 6. Attribution and exclusions

Scope follows the Mitiga detention/security apparatus and organizational structures materially participating in qualifying detention/persecution projects. Individual persons require their own evidence or project-based Covered Associate treatment; unrelated Libyan authorities and civilians are excluded.

## 7. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD scoped `S`; reject undefined whole-label guilt.** The ICC material directly links organizational members and a defined detention facility/project to qualifying conduct, while due-process and identity discipline require a narrower scope than an unbounded organization/person class.

## 8. Review / removal triggers

Review on organizational dissolution/restructuring, verified closure/reform of the implicated detention system, judicial findings materially changing the factual basis, or evidence that successor structures are distinct.
