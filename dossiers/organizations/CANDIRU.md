# Candiru

> **Governance record only.** No licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Scoped organization restriction candidate.** The authoritative record supports a defined restriction around Candiru's commercial-spyware activity where it materially enables repressive surveillance or transnational repression.

## 2. Exact identity and knowability

A stable Schedule must identify the exact Candiru legal entity and aliases with sufficient registration/jurisdiction information to avoid ambiguity. Controlled affiliates are not silently included unless control is knowable.

## 3. ECL criteria engaged

The primary fit is **§5.1 Repressive surveillance**, with **§5.6** relevant where the surveillance materially enables persecution/unlawful political repression and **§5.7** for deliberate circumvention.

## 4. Evidence supporting the determination

The U.S. Department of Commerce stated in November 2021 that Candiru and NSO Group developed and supplied spyware to foreign governments that used the tools to maliciously target government officials, journalists, businesspeople, activists, academics and embassy workers, and that the tools enabled transnational repression.

## 5. Counter-evidence and ordinary activity

The Commerce Entity List is evidence, not automatic ECL law. A stable designation should incorporate any current company response, technical safeguards, cessation or remediation evidence. Current evidence supports `S` rather than an unlimited whole-entity inference.

## 6. Attribution, projects and exclusions

Scope follows commercial-spyware development, licensing, operation, distribution and technical support only where the project materially enables qualifying surveillance/repression. Employees, former employees, passive owners, customers and unrelated activity do not inherit status automatically.

## 7. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD scoped `S`.** Unlike ordinary dual-use supply, the authoritative evidence directly links the organization's supplied spyware to malicious targeting and transnational repression.

## 8. Review / removal triggers

Review on independently verified cessation/restructuring, effective safeguards preventing repressive deployment, material contrary evidence or legal-entity changes.
