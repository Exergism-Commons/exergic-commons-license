# NSO Group

> **Governance record only.** No licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Scoped organization restriction candidate.** Current authoritative evidence supports restriction of NSO Group's commercial-spyware development/supply activity where it materially enables repressive surveillance, persecution or transnational repression. The finding is framed around the spyware business/projects rather than permanent status for employees, former personnel, shareholders or unrelated activity.

## 2. Exact identity and knowability

A future Schedule must use the exact legal entity/entities intended by `NSO Group` and any controlled subsidiaries only where their identity/control is publicly knowable. The trade name alone should not silently expand to unknown affiliates.

## 3. ECL criteria engaged

- **§5.1 Repressive surveillance** — direct fit where spyware is supplied/operated for persecution, repression, arbitrary detention or suppression of dissent.
- **§5.6 Systematic coercive domination/unlawful political repression** where surveillance materially enables persecution or transnational repression.
- **§5.7 Circumvention** for deliberate intermediary structures used to evade an incorporated restriction.

## 4. Evidence supporting the determination

The U.S. Department of Commerce added NSO Group to the Entity List in November 2021 based on evidence that NSO Group and Candiru developed and supplied spyware to foreign governments that used those tools to maliciously target government officials, journalists, businesspeople, activists, academics and embassy workers. Commerce stated that the tools also enabled transnational repression against dissidents, journalists and activists outside authoritarian governments' borders.

That evidence connects NSO's own product/supply activity to the exact surveillance mechanism at issue rather than merely showing a controversial customer relationship.

## 5. Counter-evidence and ordinary activity

Entity-List designation is not itself an ECL designation and Commerce's legal standard is not ECL's standard. A future stable review should incorporate current NSO responses, product safeguards and evidence of remediation/non-recurrence where available.

The current ECL result therefore remains `S`, not an unbounded conclusion about every corporate function.

## 6. Attribution, projects and exclusions

Scope follows NSO-controlled commercial-spyware development, licensing, operation, support and distribution **when the relevant project materially enables ECL-prohibited repressive surveillance or persecution**.

Former employees, passive investors, ordinary vendors, unrelated customers and unrelated corporate activity are excluded absent their own Material Participation.

## 7. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD a scoped organization-level restriction, but reject guilt-by-association.** The direct product/use evidence is stronger than ordinary dual-use contracting because the authoritative record specifically ties the supplied spyware to malicious individual targeting and transnational repression.

## 8. Review / removal triggers

Re-review on independently verified product/business restructuring, durable controls preventing repressive deployment, cessation of implicated spyware lines, successful remediation or material contrary evidence. Escalation to whole-entity `R` would require evidence that qualifying conduct is central across the exact legal entity rather than the scoped spyware business/project.
