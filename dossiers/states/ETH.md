# Ethiopia

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope.** Remediated/suspended project segments are excluded unless abuse recurs.

## 2. Evidence supporting the determination

Mass forced evictions under Corridor Development affected many cities with inadequate notice/consultation. Journalists faced arbitrary/incommunicado detention, surveillance, accreditation revocation and media-license actions; reporting in May 2026 described an intensified crackdown before the June election.

## 3. Counter-evidence with operative effect

Addis Ababa officials temporarily suspended part of Corridor Development in October 2025 and promised improved consultation. Journalist Million Beyene, abducted on 15 April 2026, was released by Ethiopian authorities on 28 April.

## 4. Attribution and exclusions

Scope follows unremediated forced-eviction implementations and specified security/media-regulatory/surveillance projects producing prohibited conduct. Successfully remediated segments and unrelated State functions are excluded.

## 5. Adversarial determination

**NARROW S.**

## 6. Review triggers

Rights-compliant eviction redesign, compensation/consultation, end to incommunicado journalist detention, independent accountability and durable media/civic-space restoration.
