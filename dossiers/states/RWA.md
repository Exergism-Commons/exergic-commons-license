# Rwanda

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**R — State apparatus Restricted**, while recognizing real accountability functions that must not be collaterally blocked.

## 2. Evidence supporting the determination

Domestic political contestability remains severely constrained, including continued detention/trial of opposition leader Victoire Ingabire. UN reporting has continued to identify Rwandan troop presence in DRC and support for M23, implicating core military/executive organs rather than isolated units.

## 3. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

The constitutional Office of the Ombudsman decides injustice/corruption complaints, follows referrals and may ask the Supreme Court to review a final judgment marred by injustice. It reports concrete case-resolution activity, including more than one hundred citizen cases resolved during a January 2026 outreach exercise.

## 4. Attribution and exclusions

Independent Ombudsman/Supreme Court/accountability activity must be excluded where genuinely remedial and not materially supporting a Restricted Project.

## 5. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD R provisionally.** The reviewed Ombudsman evidence is strong for ordinary injustice but not yet for politically sensitive security/external-military conduct.

## 6. Review triggers

Binding remedies in politically sensitive detention/security cases, verified end to military support/presence connected to M23, and accountability for external/domestic abuses.
