# Antigua and Barbuda

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**N — No current State-level or defined-project basis.** The State retains the death penalty in law and has ordinary governance/rights gaps, but no reviewed evidence established a current materially deployed ECL §5 project.

## 2. ECL criteria tested

The review tested political surveillance, unlawful software-enabled targeting, automated discriminatory systems, deceptive population manipulation and irreversible coercive technological capture.

## 3. Evidence requiring scrutiny

Death-penalty retention, detention conditions, media/defamation rules and Barbuda land/autonomy disputes remain rights concerns. Capability or legal retention alone does not establish an ECL prohibited use.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

The 2026 record shows competitive elections, generally protected expression/assembly and continued engagement with UN human-rights mechanisms, including commitments to strengthen implementation/reporting structures.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions

No government organ or population is Restricted by this dossier. Future review must identify a concrete attributable prohibited project.

## 6. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD `N`.** The current record does not cross the ECL material/systematic deployment threshold.

## 7. Review triggers

Reopen if capital punishment is operationalized through an independently qualifying ECL project, or if new surveillance, targeting or coercive information-control systems are deployed.
