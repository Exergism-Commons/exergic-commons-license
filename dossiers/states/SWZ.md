> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**R — State apparatus Restricted**, with lower confidence on the adequacy of the counter-institution review than most other `R` cases.

## 2. Evidence supporting the determination

Political-party competition remains structurally constrained under an absolute-monarchy model, with continuing police/detention and political-control concerns. The 2026 review did not identify an independent institution producing remedies substantial enough to rebut that structure.

## 3. Counter-evidence

## 4. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD R**, but require a deeper institution-specific pass before any stable Schedule adoption.

## 5. Review triggers
