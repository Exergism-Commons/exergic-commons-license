# Syria

> **Governance record only.** This dossier does not itself create licensing restrictions. Only a versioned Schedule expressly incorporated by a software release has operative effect.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope.** The finding is limited to specifically implicated transitional-government security/military/detention and government-backed militia projects. Transitional-justice and missing-person mechanisms are expressly excluded.

## 2. ECL criteria engaged

- unlawful/extrajudicial killing and sectarian coercive targeting;
- arbitrary detention, torture or serious ill-treatment;
- material obstruction of investigation/accountability;
- coercive targeting of minority communities through attributable security operations.

## 3. Evidence supporting the determination

Government and government-backed forces committed unlawful and extrajudicial killings of Alawite civilians during the 2025 coastal violence and Druze civilians in Suwayda. Arbitrary detention and ill-treatment remained documented under transitional security structures. EUAA's 2026 material records continuing arbitrary arrests/detention, including in the first quarter of 2026, and serious detention conditions.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

The transitional authorities established a National Commission for Transitional Justice and a National Commission for Missing Persons and began proceedings against former Assad-era officials. These mechanisms directly address prior and current abuse and therefore materially defeat any whole-State finding. Fragmented armed control likewise requires actor-specific attribution.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions

Scope covers **identified transitional-government security, military, detention or government-backed militia projects materially involved in extrajudicial/unlawful killing, arbitrary detention, torture/ill-treatment, sectarian coercive targeting or material obstruction of investigation/accountability**. Transitional-justice/missing-person mechanisms, independent courts/remedial functions, unrelated civilian administration, SDF/AANES and independent/non-State armed actors are excluded absent separate attribution.

## 6. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD / NARROW S; TRANSITIONAL-JUSTICE EXCLUSIONS.** Current attributable abuse remains sufficient for a scoped finding, but the new accountability architecture and fragmented control make whole-State attribution inaccurate.

## 7. Review / removal triggers

Independent prosecution/remedy for coastal/Suwayda abuses, durable protection of Alawite/Druze and other communities, elimination of arbitrary detention/torture, effective civilian command control and demonstrated independence/effectiveness of transitional-justice and missing-person mechanisms.
