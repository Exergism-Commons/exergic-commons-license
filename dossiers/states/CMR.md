# Cameroon

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope.**

## 2. Evidence supporting the determination

Current 2025/26 reporting describes security forces using excessive force in post-election demonstrations, killing at least 48 people, and arbitrary arrest/detention of political activists. Peaceful opposition protesters from the 2020 MRC demonstrations remained imprisoned years later after military-court convictions. A Human Rights Committee decision adopted in March 2026 addressed fair-trial, ill-treatment, arbitrary-detention and detention-condition claims in Marafa v. Cameroon.

## 3. Counter-evidence and attribution limits

Dorgelesse Nguessan was released in January 2025 after appellate sentence reduction, showing that judicial relief can occur. The trial concerning journalist Martinez Zogo's killing resumed. Conflict violence by non-State separatist groups must be separately attributed and cannot be imputed to Cameroon wholesale.

## 4. Attribution and exclusions

Scope follows identified election/protest security operations, units involved in arbitrary political detention or excessive/lethal force, and military-court/detention projects materially used against protected political assembly/expression. Unrelated judiciary/public services are excluded.

## 5. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD / DEFINE S.**

## 6. Review triggers

Release/remedy for remaining peaceful political detainees, independent investigation/prosecution of election-period killings, end to military-court use against protected civilian political activity and effective judicial review.
