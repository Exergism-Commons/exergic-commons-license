# Lebanon

> **Governance record only.** This dossier does not itself create licensing restrictions. Only a versioned Schedule expressly incorporated by a software release has operative effect.

## 1. Current determination

**U — Under Review / insufficient current evidence for a stable active restriction.** The prior `S` remains important historical evidence, but the August 2026 record reviewed here does not establish enough fresh recurrence to treat the former speech/extradition pattern as a stable current ECL-prohibited project.

## 2. ECL criteria engaged

The continuing review concerns:

- political repression through criminalization of protected journalism or activism;
- coercive information control;
- extradition or transfer into a substantiated enforced-disappearance/torture risk;
- meaningful access to contestation, remedy and institutional reform.

## 3. Evidence supporting continued review

The 2025 record documented repeated summonses of activists and journalists under criminal defamation and other vague offences. It also included Lebanon's extradition of Abdul Rahman Al-Qaradawi to the United Arab Emirates, after which he was reported forcibly disappeared. These events remain serious attribution evidence and prevent immediate movement to `N`.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

In 2026 parliamentary work materially advanced a new media-law proposal, including rights-protective elements relevant to decriminalizing or limiting coercive speech enforcement. On 9 July 2026 joint parliamentary committees approved the media-law proposal for further legislative consideration. Judicial and administrative remedies in the broader record also show continuing institutional contestability.

The reviewed evidence did not establish a sufficiently current, repeated 2026 speech-prosecution or abusive-extradition project to justify retaining `S` solely by inertia.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions

Current review follows only recurrence of speech-based criminal summons/prosecution; implementation of the pending media-law framework; accountability/remedy for the Al-Qaradawi extradition; and future extradition/security cooperation materially exposing critics to disappearance, torture or comparable abuse.

Lebanon, Lebanese people/nationality, the judiciary, parliament, media institutions and security/prosecutorial bodies are not Restricted generically while the dossier is `U`.

## 6. Adversarial determination

**DOWNGRADE `S → U`.** ECL's reversibility rule requires a current restriction to rest on a live, sufficiently evidenced prohibited project. Historical seriousness alone does not substitute for current persistence when material reform and counter-institutions are also present.

## 7. Review / removal triggers

**Re-escalate to `S` if:**

- protected journalism/activism is again systematically criminalized through vague speech offences;
- the new media framework is implemented coercively against protected expression; or
- another attributable extradition/transfer exposes a critic or defender to a substantiated disappearance/torture risk.

**Move to `N` if:**

- expression protections are durably strengthened and coercive speech prosecutions do not recur;
- effective remedy/accountability addresses abusive extradition practice; and
- extradition/security-transfer safeguards reliably prevent high-risk transfer.
