# Guinea-Bissau

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**R — current Military High Command / de facto transitional governing apparatus.** This is not a permanent classification of Guinea-Bissau, its people or a future civilian constitutional government.

## 2. Evidence supporting the determination

The military seized power in November 2025 before completion/publication of the electoral result and detained major political leaders. The African Union in March 2026 demanded unconditional release of political detainees, restoration of constitutionalism, an end to military interference and civilian subordination of the security sector.

## 3. Counter-evidence and reversibility

The transitional authorities announced an inclusive transition, scheduled elections for 6 December 2026 and pledged political-prisoner releases; some detainees were released or moved from prison. These are meaningful but incomplete remediation signals.

## 4. Attribution and exclusions

The finding follows the current military/de facto governing apparatus and projects materially sustaining unconstitutional military political control or political detention. Civil society, population and a future civilian government are excluded.

## 5. Adversarial determination

**ESCALATE S → R** because the coercive military apparatus became the governing authority itself.

## 6. Review triggers

Immediate review on unconditional release of political detainees and mandatory review after the scheduled 6 December 2026 elections/civilian transfer or any earlier restoration of constitutional government.
