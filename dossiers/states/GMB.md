# Gambia

> **Governance record only.** No licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination
**U — Under Review.**

## 2. ECL criteria engaged
Suppression of protected expression/assembly and coercive cyber/media enforcement.

## 3. Evidence supporting continued review
Sedition/media laws remain restrictive and recent years included arrests of activists and journalists. The legal framework continues to justify scrutiny.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions
Key criminal cases were withdrawn, an Access to Information Commission was established, and accountability mechanisms for Jammeh-era crimes advanced. The review did not identify enough fresh 2026 deployment for `S`.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions
No State apparatus is restricted; review follows any current police/prosecutorial/cyber project that materially criminalizes protected activity.

## 6. Adversarial determination
**RETAIN U.**

## 7. Review / removal triggers
Escalate on renewed systematic speech/protest prosecutions; move to `N` on repeal/non-use of repressive provisions and durable accountability/reform.
