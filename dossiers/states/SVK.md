# Slovakia

> **Governance record only.** This dossier does not itself create licensing restrictions. Only a versioned Schedule expressly incorporated by a software release has operative effect.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope.** The finding attaches to enacted civic-control/discriminatory systems and specifically evidenced police-abuse accountability failures, not to Slovakia's public institutions generally.

## 2. ECL criteria engaged

- political/civic repression through intrusive control of protected association;
- systematic discriminatory domination through legally coercive unequal-rights measures;
- coercive targeting and accountability failure in specifically evidenced police ill-treatment cases.

## 3. Evidence supporting the determination

Slovakia's 2025 constitutional amendments entrenched discriminatory rules affecting LGBTI people and other rights. The NGO law imposed intrusive administrative requirements and increased State oversight. Amnesty's 2025/26 record also documents systemic Roma discrimination and persistent shortcomings in impartial investigation of credible police ill-treatment allegations.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

The Slovak Ombudsperson challenged the NGO law before the Constitutional Court. Active constitutional litigation and oversight materially limit attribution and make a broad State or legislature-wide finding inappropriate.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions

Scope covers **implementation/enforcement of NGO/civic-control rules materially burdening protected association or privacy; legally coercive discriminatory constitutional/administrative measures materially denying equal rights; and specific police/complaint-investigation projects where racially discriminatory violence or torture/ill-treatment is credibly alleged and effective independent investigation is materially obstructed**. The Constitutional Court, Ombudsperson and unrelated public functions are excluded.

## 6. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD / NARROW S.** Current enacted systems support a stable scoped finding, while functioning constitutional challenge prevents broader attribution.

## 7. Review / removal triggers

Repeal/narrowing or constitutional invalidation of coercive NGO/discriminatory provisions, effective equality remedies and demonstrably independent investigation/accountability for police ill-treatment.
