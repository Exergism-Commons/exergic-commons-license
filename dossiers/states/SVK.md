# Slovakia

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope.** The former NGO-law component is no longer active. Current review follows only operative discriminatory constitutional/administrative implementation and separately evidenced police-abuse/accountability projects.

## 2. ECL criteria engaged

Systematic discriminatory domination where legally coercive measures materially deny equal rights, plus qualifying coercive targeting/accountability failure in specifically evidenced police-abuse projects.

## 3. Evidence supporting the determination

Constitutional Act No. 255/2025 entered into force on 1 November 2025 and changed constitutional rules concerning family/parenthood, adoption, sex recognition and related matters. It remains operative. Any Schedule scope must follow concrete implementation that materially denies equal rights rather than restrict constitutional text merely for its political content.

## 4. Material remediation and counter-evidence

On 17 December 2025 the Constitutional Court held Act No. 109/2025, which imposed the challenged NGO disclosure/control regime, incompatible with multiple constitutional and Convention rights. That ruling is credited as effective remediation. The NGO-law component is therefore removed from current `S` scope rather than preserved by inertia.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions

Scope may follow implementation of Constitutional Act 255/2025 only where an identified administrative/judicial process materially denies equal rights under ECL 0.2. The invalidated NGO regime, the Constitutional Court, Ombudsperson, independent remedies and unrelated public functions are excluded.

## 6. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD / NARROW `S`.** Current operative legal effects preserve a scoped review basis, while successful constitutional invalidation requires removal of the NGO-control project.

## 7. Review / removal triggers

Rights-protective interpretation/reform, effective equality remedies, non-discriminatory implementation and independent accountability for any separately evidenced police abuse.
