# Palestine

> **Governance record only.** This dossier does not itself create licensing restrictions. Only a versioned Schedule expressly incorporated by a software release has operative effect.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope**, limited here to specifically attributable Palestinian Authority security/detention projects in the West Bank. Fragmented authority requires actor-specific treatment.

## 2. ECL criteria engaged

- arbitrary detention and denial of meaningful contestability;
- torture and other ill-treatment;
- political/journalistic repression;
- disregard of judicial release/remedial orders;
- obstruction of accountability.

## 3. Evidence supporting the determination

On 22 July 2026 OHCHR reported that Palestinian authorities continued arbitrary or unlawful detention of Palestinians in the West Bank for security reasons, including journalists, dissidents and political activists. OHCHR also documented detainees held despite court release orders and reports of severe beatings, whippings, stress positions and prolonged solitary confinement.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

Palestinian authorities continue engagement with OHCHR and the UN Committee against Torture and have undertaken commitments toward torture prevention, including a National Commission against Torture and work toward a National Preventive Mechanism. Palestinian courts issuing release orders are meaningful counter-institutions even when security bodies fail to comply.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions

Scope follows **Palestinian Authority security/intelligence, detention and prosecutorial projects in the West Bank materially involved in arbitrary detention, torture/ill-treatment, detention contrary to judicial release orders or repression of protected journalism/political dissent**. Courts ordering release, torture-prevention/remedial functions and unrelated civilian services are excluded. **Hamas and other de facto/non-State armed structures are not collapsed into this State dossier and require separate actor/project attribution.**

## 6. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD / DEFINE S.** Fresh July 2026 OHCHR evidence preserves a strong PA security/detention finding while reinforcing the need to avoid collective Palestinian or cross-authority attribution.

## 7. Review / removal triggers

Compliance with judicial release orders; independent torture investigations/prosecutions; operational National Preventive Mechanism; durable cessation of political/journalistic arbitrary detention; and transparent detention safeguards.
