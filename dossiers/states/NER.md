# Niger

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**R — military/de facto governing apparatus.**

## 2. Evidence supporting the determination

Amnesty's 2026 UPR material describes a serious rights setback since the July 2023 coup, including arbitrary detention, restrictions on defenders and press freedom. Current records include union dissolution, detention of journalists/defenders/opposition figures, prolonged detention of the former president and an unresolved disappearance of a former minister. A July 2026 Central Sahel review describes authoritarian governance without effective opposition and systematic repression of critical voices.

## 3. Counter-evidence

2025 national consultations included recommendations on violence against women/children and authorities announced migrant-repatriation measures. Formal judicial/administrative institutions remain. These do not demonstrate an independent counter-institution capable of restraining military political-security power.

## 4. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD R.**

## 5. Review triggers

Civilian transition, political-prisoner remedy, restoration of media/union/opposition space, independent investigation of disappearances and demonstrable judicial restraint.
