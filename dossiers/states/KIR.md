# Kiribati

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**N — No current State-level or defined-project basis.** Kiribati faces existential climate-displacement and rights challenges, but the review does not establish a State-directed ECL §5 prohibited project.

## 2. ECL criteria tested

Repressive surveillance, unlawful software-enabled targeting, automated discriminatory domination, deceptive population manipulation and coercive technological capture were tested.

## 3. Evidence requiring scrutiny

Climate displacement, mobility rights and adaptation policy are major human-rights issues. Harm or vulnerability caused by climate change is not by itself attributable as a coercive State project under ECL.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

Current international engagement is focused on climate justice, adaptation and protection rather than coercive deployment; no reviewed evidence establishes systematic prohibited State conduct.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions

No Kiribati institution, population or nationality is designated through this dossier.

## 6. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD `N`.** The ECL project threshold is not met.

## 7. Review triggers

Reopen on specific State deployment of repressive surveillance, unlawful targeting, discriminatory automated systems or coercive information-control infrastructure.
