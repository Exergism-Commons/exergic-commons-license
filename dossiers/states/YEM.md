# Yemen

> **Governance record only.** This dossier does not itself create licensing restrictions. Only a versioned Schedule expressly incorporated by a software release has operative effect.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with fragmented, actor-specific scope.** Yemen is not treated as a single coercive State apparatus. Any future operative designation must identify the actual de facto/government/security structure participating in the prohibited project.

## 2. ECL criteria engaged

- arbitrary detention, enforced disappearance, torture and ill-treatment;
- reprisal against humanitarian, civil-society, journalistic or political activity;
- unlawful coercive targeting by conflict/security actors;
- material obstruction of humanitarian or accountability functions.

## 3. Evidence supporting the determination

As of June 2026, 73 UN personnel remained arbitrarily detained by Huthi de facto authorities, alongside civil-society and humanitarian workers; one UN colleague had died in detention. The wider current record documents arbitrary detention, enforced disappearance, torture and ill-treatment by Huthi forces, internationally recognised-government actors and UAE-backed forces.

These abuses support scoped restrictions, but only with actor-level attribution.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

A May 2026 agreement between the internationally recognised government and Huthi authorities provided for a major prisoner exchange under international facilitation. Dialogue, prisoner-exchange mechanisms, humanitarian organisations and independent civilian institutions are material counter-evidence/remedial functions.

The country's territorial and institutional fragmentation itself is also an attribution constraint: Huthi, government, STC/UAE-backed and other armed structures cannot inherit one another's conduct.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions

Any operative designation must identify the specific **Huthi de facto detention/security apparatus, internationally recognised-government detention/security unit, STC/UAE-backed security structure, or other attributable conflict-party project** materially involved in arbitrary detention, disappearance, torture, reprisal against humanitarian/civic actors or unlawful coercive targeting.

The Yemeni population/nationality, humanitarian institutions, independent civilian administration and conduct of rival actors are excluded from one another's attribution.

## 6. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD S; REFRAME AS FRAGMENTED-AUTHORITY MAP.** A unitary Yemen restriction would be analytically false and incompatible with ECL attribution rules.

## 7. Review / removal triggers

Actor-specific release/remedy, cessation of arbitrary detention/disappearance/torture, independent detention monitoring and durable transfer to rights-compliant civilian legal control. Each actor/project must be reviewed separately.
