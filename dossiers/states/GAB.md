# Gabon

> **Governance record only.** No licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination
**S — Restricted with defined scope.**

## 2. ECL criteria engaged
Information control, repressive surveillance and coercive suppression of protected civic/political activity.

## 3. Evidence supporting determination
In 2026 authorities imposed a nationwide social-media suspension amid criticism and protests; contemporaneous reporting described detentions, device confiscation and scrutiny of circumvention/VPN use. This supplies the current deployment missing from the first pass.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions
Civil society, media and formal political institutions remain distinct from the implicated security/information-control project; the evidence does not support whole-State restriction.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions
Scope is limited to security/telecommunications/information-control projects materially blocking communications, inspecting devices or targeting protected dissent. Ordinary communications administration, courts and unrelated public functions are excluded.

## 6. Adversarial determination
**ESCALATE U → S.**

## 7. Review / removal triggers
End shutdowns/device targeting, provide independent legal authorization and remedy, and protect peaceful political/media activity.
