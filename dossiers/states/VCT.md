# Saint Vincent and the Grenadines

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**N — No current State-level or defined-project basis.** Criminalization of consensual same-sex activity and death-penalty retention are serious rights concerns, but current evidence does not establish a materially deployed ECL §5 project.

## 2. ECL criteria tested

Repressive surveillance, unlawful software-enabled targeting, automated/data-driven discriminatory domination, deceptive population manipulation and coercive technological capture were tested.

## 3. Evidence requiring scrutiny

A 2026 UPR stakeholder submission documents discriminatory impact from criminalization of consensual same-sex activity. ECL §5.3, however, requires an automated/data-driven system whose primary purpose is unlawful discrimination; legal capacity alone is insufficient.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

Courts, democratic institutions and UN review provide contestability. No reviewed evidence shows systematic technological enforcement matching ECL's prohibited-use categories.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions

No institution, population or nationality is designated through this dossier.

## 6. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD `N`.** The underlying rights concern does not presently establish the narrower ECL prohibited-project nexus.

## 7. Review triggers

Reopen on automated discriminatory enforcement, systematic surveillance/targeting of LGBTI people or other qualifying coercive technology.
