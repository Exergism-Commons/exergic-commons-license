# Libya

> **Governance record only.** This dossier does not itself create licensing restrictions. Only a versioned Schedule expressly incorporated by a software release has operative effect.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope.** Libya is not treated as one unitary coercive apparatus. Restriction follows specifically attributable de facto security, detention and migration structures.

## 2. ECL criteria engaged

- arbitrary detention, torture and coercive targeting/unlawful violence;
- systematic domination of persons deprived of liberty;
- material participation in unsafe coercive transfer/interception;
- obstruction of accountability where specifically attributable.

## 3. Evidence supporting the determination

OHCHR and UNSMIL's 2026 report on migrants, asylum seekers and refugees documents persistent widespread and systematic violations and abuses, including conduct by **State-affiliated actors involved in migration and border management** alongside traffickers, smugglers and armed groups. Wider UN reporting continues to identify arbitrary detention and severe abuse across facilities controlled by competing authorities and affiliated security structures.

The evidence supports continuing restrictions on particular institutional structures but also demonstrates why violence by every armed actor cannot be imputed to a single Libyan State apparatus.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

Libyan authorities continue engagement with UN mechanisms and the UPR process, while justice, local-governance and institutional-reform functions exist within a fragmented political environment. Such functions are not Restricted merely by association with one of the competing authorities and must be assessed on their own conduct.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions

Scope follows **attributable GNU-, LAAF- or other de facto-authority security, detention and migration/border-management units**, plus State-affiliated or Covered Associate actors materially participating in arbitrary detention, torture, coercive abuse, unsafe transfer/interception or obstruction of accountability. Non-attributable private armed-group conduct, civilian populations, independent/remedial functions and unrelated public services are excluded.

## 6. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD / DEFINE S.** Current evidence remains strong enough for scoped restrictions. Fragmented authority and mixed attribution make a whole-State `R` finding materially inaccurate.

## 7. Review / removal triggers

Review on independently verifiable closure or compliant reform of abusive facilities, prosecution/remedy for torture and arbitrary detention, enforceable safeguards against unsafe transfer/interception, or clear civilian/legal control over implicated units. Remove/narrow scope when a particular attributable project ceases or is durably remediated.
