# Trinidad and Tobago

> **Governance record only.** This dossier does not itself create licensing restrictions. Only a versioned Schedule expressly incorporated by a software release has operative effect.

## 1. Current determination

**U — Under Review / insufficient current evidence for a stable active restriction.** The migration/asylum framework remains vulnerable to abuse, but current evidence does not establish enough fresh recurrence of the earlier summary-return pattern to preserve `S` by inertia.

## 2. ECL criteria engaged

Current review concerns:

- coercive targeting through collective or summary return without effective individualized protection review;
- arbitrary immigration detention;
- material obstruction of access to asylum, UNHCR or effective remedy.

## 3. Evidence supporting continued review

The 2025/26 evidence baseline describes summary pushbacks/deportations of people seeking asylum, and Trinidad and Tobago still lacks asylum-specific legislation. A 2026 UPR submission by detention/protection organisations continued to identify criminalisation of irregular entry and immigration detention as structural concerns.

These facts justify continued review, but structural legal vulnerability and past incidents do not automatically prove a current systematic prohibited project.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

In 2026 the government introduced a Migrant Registration Framework allowing qualifying undocumented migrants to regularise residence and work through 31 December 2026. Current UNHCR guidance records Special Inquiry procedures, the ability to raise an asylum claim, a right to challenge deportation decisions and possible supervised release.

The Office of the Ombudsman remains an independent parliamentary institution for complaints of public-sector maladministration. These mechanisms materially weaken the case for a stable active `S` absent fresh evidence of systematic unlawful return.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions

Review is limited to **actual deportation/return practice, access to protection/non-refoulement safeguards, immigration detention and implementation of the 2026 registration framework**. Ordinary immigration administration, courts/appeals, Ombudsman functions, humanitarian actors and the population/nationality are excluded absent independent evidence of prohibited conduct.

## 6. Adversarial determination

**DOWNGRADE S → U.** The historical basis remains serious, but current persistence is insufficiently established at the 2026-08-11 cutoff.

## 7. Review / removal triggers

**Re-escalate to `S`** on new attributable collective or summary deportation of protection-seekers without effective individualized non-refoulement review, systematic arbitrary immigration detention or obstruction of access to UNHCR/legal remedy. **Move to `N`** on durable individualized protection review, effective appeal, rights-compliant detention practice and no recurrence of unlawful return.
