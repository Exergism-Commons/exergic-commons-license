# Democratic Republic of the Congo

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope.**

## 2. Evidence supporting the determination

Amnesty documented abuses involving FARDC and Wazalendo, and in June 2026 documented killings, torture, looting and sexual slavery by CMC-FDP, a Wazalendo group backed by FARDC.

## 3. Attribution correction

Major current crimes by M23 and ADF are separately attributable to those armed groups and any evidenced external sponsor. FARDC failure to prevent ADF attacks is not treated as perpetration of ADF crimes.

## 4. Scope and exclusions

Scope follows FARDC units and State-backed proxies where support/command/collaboration/knowledge materially connects the State to killings, torture, sexual violence, looting, child recruitment or comparable ECL-prohibited conduct. Independent civilian-protection/accountability work is excluded.

## 5. Adversarial determination

**NARROW S.**

## 6. Review triggers

End to support for abusive proxies, command accountability, prosecutions/remediation and civilian-protection safeguards.
