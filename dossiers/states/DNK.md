# Denmark

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope**, limited to particular Udbetaling Danmark/ATP automated fraud/risk-profiling models and materially connected data-processing workflows where discriminatory, opaque or disproportionate surveillance effects remain unremediated. ATP, Udbetaling Danmark and Danish welfare administration are **not** restricted generally.

## 2. ECL criteria engaged

Repressive/disproportionate data surveillance, discriminatory profiling, opacity/non-contestability and reduction of meaningful agency in welfare administration.

## 3. Evidence supporting the determination

Amnesty's technical investigation documents dozens of data-driven fraud-control algorithms used to flag welfare recipients for investigation, including models using foreign affiliation/citizenship and atypical household patterns, with mass-data processing, opacity and discrimination risks. The authorities declined requests for collaborative audit/full code and data access, and the review did not locate authoritative evidence that the implicated architecture had been terminated or independently cleared by August 2026.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

Danish authorities dispute parts of Amnesty's legal/technical characterization, including whether the systems constitute prohibited social scoring under the EU AI Act. Denmark also has ordinary judicial/administrative review and EU data-protection/AI-law safeguards, weighing strongly against any broader State designation.

## 5. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD S, NARROW TO SPECIFIED SYSTEMS.**

## 6. Removal trigger

Independently auditable retirement/redesign, validated non-discrimination/proportionality assessment, adequate notice/contestability and effective remediation for affected persons.
