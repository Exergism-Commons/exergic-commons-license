# Nepal

> **Governance record only.** This dossier does not itself create licensing restrictions. Only a versioned Schedule expressly incorporated by a software release has operative effect.

## 1. Current determination

**U — Under Review / insufficient current evidence for a stable active restriction.** Serious historical evidence remains, but ECL's reversibility rule does not permit the prior `S` to survive solely by institutional inertia after a material change in government and active accountability proceedings.

## 2. ECL criteria engaged

Current review concerns:

- unlawful lethal force and coercive protest policing;
- arbitrary detention or suppression of protected expression/assembly;
- coercive development enforcement that materially defeats meaningful FPIC/contestability.

## 3. Evidence supporting continued review

The September 2025 youth protests generated serious allegations and documented evidence of extrajudicial/unlawful killings, excessive force and arbitrary detention involving police/security bodies. Earlier evidence also raised concerns about coercive enforcement against Indigenous communities resisting development projects without adequate free, prior and informed consent.

These events remain relevant for accountability and for testing recurrence, but historical severity does not by itself establish a current active project under the post-election administration.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

Nepal subsequently changed government following the 2026 election. The National Human Rights Commission completed its investigation into human-rights violations during the `Gen Z` protests and transmitted recommendations to the Government in May 2026. Accountability proceedings concerning the former leadership/security response also materially change the institutional picture. At the review cutoff, no sufficiently strong evidence of comparable renewed protest-repression by the new administration was identified.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions

Current review is limited to **implementation of accountability for the September 2025 killings; any renewed unlawful protest-policing, arbitrary detention or expression-suppression project; and any current coercive development/FPIC enforcement that is specifically re-established with evidence**. Past conduct is not automatically attributed to new officials or institutions. NHRC, courts/remedial bodies, peaceful political actors and the population/nationality are excluded.

## 6. Adversarial determination

**DOWNGRADE S → U.** The prior restriction was supported by severe evidence, but continuity of the attributable coercive project is no longer sufficiently established after government change and active independent accountability. Retaining `S` without fresh project-level evidence would contradict ECL reversibility.

## 7. Review / removal triggers

**Re-escalate to `S`** if new attributable unlawful lethal protest policing, systematic arbitrary detention/suppression of peaceful dissent or coercive development enforcement crosses ECL's material/systematic threshold. **Move to `N`** on sustained non-recurrence plus credible accountability/remediation and durable rights-compliant policing/FPIC safeguards.
