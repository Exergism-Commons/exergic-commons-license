# Togo

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**R — State apparatus Restricted.**

## 2. Evidence supporting the determination

An April 2026 UN communication describes prolonged deterioration of civic space and alleged **systematic** enforced disappearance, arbitrary detention, torture, excessive force and chemical-agent use during demonstrations, alongside restrictive legislation and surveillance technologies. Current reporting also describes media suspensions, internet disruption, protest bans and detention of critics.

## 3. Counter-evidence

Togo entered a new constitutional/governance phase. UN experts themselves described an opportunity to provide justice, accountability and non-repetition. At the cutoff that opportunity had not yet become demonstrated remediation, and the constitutional change further concentrated political power.

## 4. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD R**, with mandatory re-review when there is evidence on accountability and institutional reform.

## 5. Review triggers

Investigations/prosecutions, resolution of disappearance cases, repeal/narrowing of restrictive laws, independent oversight, restoration of media/protest rights and verified non-recurrence.
