# Albania

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**N — No current State-level or defined-project basis.** The 2025/26 record contains rule-of-law, discrimination and policing concerns, but the review did not establish a current materially deployed project satisfying ECL §5 with sufficient persistence and attribution.

## 2. ECL criteria tested

The review tested repressive surveillance, coercive targeting, automated/data-driven discriminatory domination, deceptive population manipulation and irreversible coercive technological capture.

## 3. Evidence requiring scrutiny

Police accountability, detention safeguards, discrimination and any future State surveillance deployment remain reviewable. Those concerns are not treated as ethically immaterial; they simply do not currently establish the ECL project threshold.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

Albania retains plural political contestation, ordinary judicial review, ombuds/human-rights mechanisms and European human-rights supervision. No reviewed evidence showed those counter-institutions being displaced by a unitary ECL-prohibited project.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions

No Albanian ministry, court, police body, municipality, population or nationality is designated through this dossier. Any future finding must identify the specific organ/project and prohibited-use nexus.

## 6. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD `N`.** Current concerns do not meet the material/systematic/project-specific threshold.

## 7. Review triggers

Reopen on evidence of systematic political surveillance, software-enabled unlawful targeting, deployed discriminatory automated decision systems, or coercive information-control infrastructure materially denying contestability.
