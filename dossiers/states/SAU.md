# Saudi Arabia

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**R — State apparatus Restricted.**

## 2. Evidence supporting the determination

Releases coexist with long sentences, unfair trials and travel bans against critics/defenders. Saudi Arabia executed a record number of people in 2025; by 22 June 2026 it had executed 96 more, including 61 for drug offences, with foreign nationals heavily represented. Fair-trial concerns include lack of representation/interpretation and allegations of torture-tainted confessions.

## 3. Counter-evidence

Authorities released a number of expression-related detainees during 2025 and pursued labour reforms intended to reduce employer control and digitize contracts/payments. These may materially improve worker agency and are credited where effective.

## 4. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD R.** Partial labour/release reforms do not dismantle political coercion and execution systems or create an independent counter-institution capable of restraining them.

## 5. Review triggers

Sustained political-prisoner releases, fair-trial reform, major execution reduction/abolition, end to retaliatory travel bans and verified labour reforms that create real worker exit/autonomy.
