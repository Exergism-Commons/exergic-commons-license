# Switzerland

> **Governance record only.** No licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination
**U — Under Review.**

## 2. ECL criteria engaged
Potential unlawful return and coercive suppression of protected assembly.

## 3. Evidence supporting continued review
The State Secretariat again permitted certain deportations to Afghanistan and Parliament authorized coercive measures in federal asylum centres. Peaceful protests also faced prosecution and restrictive policing.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions
Federalism, courts and local review of police force provide substantial safeguards; CEDAW and other international remedies have produced corrective findings. The cutoff record does not establish a sufficiently systematic current deportation or protest-repression project for `S`.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions
No Swiss State/police/asylum system is restricted. Review follows actual unsafe-return operations or specific protest-policing projects when deployment is established.

## 6. Adversarial determination
**RETAIN U.**

## 7. Review / removal triggers
Escalate on actual returns to serious persecution risk without effective individualized review or systematic protest suppression; move to `N` on safe-return policy and durable assembly safeguards.
