# South Africa

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**U — Under Review.** South Africa's former `N` is no longer sufficiently cautious after current 2026 evidence of coercive migration-enforcement operations involving State officials, while xenophobic violence by non-State actors remains separately attributable. The present record does not yet establish a specific technology/prohibited-use project satisfying ECL §5 for stable `S`.

## 2. ECL criteria tested

Review focused on discriminatory automated/data-driven enforcement, surveillance or profiling used for persecution/intimidation, software-enabled unlawful targeting and coercive information systems denying meaningful contestability in migration enforcement.

## 3. Evidence supporting review

Current 2026 rights reporting describes State officials forcibly loading refugees/asylum seekers onto buses during verification/removal operations and a broader intensification of migration enforcement. These events are materially more current than the record underlying the initial `N`.

## 4. Counter-evidence and attribution limits

South African courts, the Human Rights Commission and civil-society organisations actively contest unlawful migration practices. Much xenophobic violence and obstruction of migrant access is attributable to private/vigilante actors rather than the State. The reviewed evidence does not yet establish that State data or surveillance systems are primarily designed for prohibited discriminatory domination or repressive targeting.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions

Review follows specific Home Affairs/police or other attributable State migration operations and any supporting data/surveillance systems. Non-State vigilante conduct, courts, SAHRC, humanitarian/remedial functions and the South African population are excluded absent separate evidence.

## 6. Adversarial determination

**ESCALATE `N → U`.** Current State-linked coercive conduct defeats a confident no-basis finding, but the exact ECL §5 technological/project nexus and systematic attribution require further proof before `S`.

## 7. Review triggers

Escalate to `S` on evidence of State-deployed profiling/surveillance, automated discriminatory denial or software-enabled unlawful targeting materially facilitating persecution, arbitrary detention or collective removal. Return to `N` if unlawful operations cease/remedy is effective and no qualifying ECL project is established.
