# Germany

> **Governance record only.** No licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination
**S — Restricted with defined scope.**

## 2. ECL criteria engaged
Unlawful coercive return and suppression of protected peaceful assembly.

## 3. Evidence supporting determination
Current reporting records deportations to Afghanistan and Syria despite serious non-refoulement concerns, alongside repeated allegations of excessive force against peaceful demonstrators. These are current identifiable enforcement projects.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions
Germany's constitutional/federal courts, parliamentary oversight, prosecutors and civil society remain strong counter-institutions. No whole-State inference follows.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions
Scope follows specific deportation/return operations lacking adequate protection safeguards and public-order operations materially using unlawful force against protected assembly. Courts, independent remedies and unrelated administration are excluded.

## 6. Adversarial determination
**ESCALATE U → S.**

## 7. Review / removal triggers
Suspend unsafe returns, ensure individualized non-refoulement review and independent accountability for unlawful protest policing.
