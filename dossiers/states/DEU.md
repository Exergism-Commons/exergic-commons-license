# Germany

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**U — Under Review.** Germany continues returns to Afghanistan and Syria in 2026, but the current official record describes individualized asylum/protection and return decisions and an initial focus on convicted offenders/security-risk cases. Deportation itself is not an ECL prohibited-use category.

## 2. Potential ECL criteria

A specific return project could engage ECL 0.2 where software materially enables forced transfer without meaningful individualized protection or non-refoulement safeguards. A specific public-order project could separately engage unlawful political repression.

## 3. Evidence requiring continued review

The Federal Government confirms that returns to Afghanistan and Syria are an active policy objective and that direct removals have occurred. Human-rights organizations continue to raise non-refoulement and country-risk concerns.

## 4. Material counter-evidence

Current Federal Government statements repeatedly say asylum/protection decisions and resulting returns are considered individually. The federal/Land allocation of responsibility, strong judicial remedies and continuing case-by-case review make a generic `Germany deportation project` too broad for an operative ECL designation at this cutoff.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions

No generic German federal/Land migration authority, police service or return programme is currently Restricted through this dossier. Courts, independent remedies and ordinary lawful migration/public-order administration are excluded.

## 6. Adversarial determination

**DOWNGRADE `S → U`.** The earlier escalation treated active controversial returns as sufficient evidence of an ECL project. Under the stricter 0.2 Schedule-knowability and prohibited-use test, current evidence does not establish a frozen operation that bypasses meaningful individualized protection strongly enough for `S`.

## 7. Review / escalation triggers

Escalate toward `S` on authoritative evidence of a named return operation systematically bypassing individualized protection/non-refoulement review, or a current named public-order operation materially suppressing protected peaceful assembly. Move toward `N` on sustained absence of qualifying projects and effective remedies.
