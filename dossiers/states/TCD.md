# Chad

> **Governance record only.** No licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination
**S — Restricted with defined scope.**

## 2. ECL criteria engaged
Coercive targeting and suppression of protected expression/association.

## 3. Evidence supporting determination
Current reporting records pressure on journalists investigating sensitive security incidents, dissolution/banning of civil-society activity and political/security prosecutions. The pattern is attributable to specific administrative/security functions.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions
Civil society, courts and international monitoring remain present; the evidence does not justify whole-State attribution.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions
Scope is limited to security/public-order and administrative enforcement materially censoring journalism, dissolving protected organizations or arbitrarily detaining peaceful critics. Independent courts/remediation and ordinary services are excluded.

## 6. Adversarial determination
**ESCALATE U → S.**

## 7. Review / removal triggers
Restore independent media/association, end arbitrary political detention and produce effective investigation/remedy for unlawful force.
