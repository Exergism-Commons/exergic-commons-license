# Belgium

> **Governance record only.** No licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination
**U — Under Review.**

## 2. ECL criteria engaged
Potential discriminatory domination, coercive detention and civic-space restriction.

## 3. Evidence supporting continued review
Belgium has repeatedly failed to provide ordered reception to asylum seekers despite numerous court judgments, and prison/asylum conditions remain serious. Proposed civic-space controls also warrant monitoring.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions
Domestic courts, the Federal Ombudsman and independent monitoring actively challenge these failures. The record does not yet establish a discrete ECL-prohibited coercive project rather than severe systemic noncompliance.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions
Review is limited to named reception/detention or enacted civic-control projects. Courts, Ombudsman, humanitarian functions and unrelated administration are excluded.

## 6. Adversarial determination
**RETAIN U.**

## 7. Review / removal triggers
Escalate on deliberate systematic denial/detention or enacted coercive civic controls; move to `N` after durable compliance with reception judgments and absence of such deployment.
