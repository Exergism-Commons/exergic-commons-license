# Republic of the Congo

> **Governance record only.** This dossier does not itself create licensing restrictions. Only a versioned Schedule expressly incorporated by a software release has operative effect.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope.** The current finding targets political-security/public-order and detention projects shown to participate in arbitrary political detention, suppression of peaceful opposition activity or serious detention abuse.

## 2. ECL criteria engaged

- political repression through arbitrary detention or suppression of peaceful assembly;
- coercive targeting of opposition/critics;
- serious detention abuse or material obstruction of remedy/accountability.

## 3. Evidence supporting the determination

Amnesty's 2025/26 Congo assessment reports restrictions ahead of the 2026 presidential election, including bans on public demonstrations, continued arbitrary detention of opposition leaders and inhumane detention conditions. The evidence is current and attributable to identified political-security/public-order and detention functions.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

The reviewed record does not establish participation by every court, ministry or public service. The evidence supports a scoped apparatus/project finding rather than a whole-State determination. Genuine independent judicial, monitoring or remedial activity is excluded unless separately implicated.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions

Scope covers **security, police, prosecutorial or detention projects materially carrying out arbitrary political detention, suppression of protected peaceful assembly/opposition activity, serious detention ill-treatment or material obstruction of independent remedy/accountability**. Unrelated public administration, ordinary services and independent remedial functions are excluded.

## 6. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD / DEFINE S.** Current political-security and detention evidence survives review, but does not justify whole-State attribution.

## 7. Review / removal triggers

Release or fair independent review of political detainees, sustained protection of peaceful assembly/opposition activity, independent detention monitoring and effective investigation/remedy for abuse.
