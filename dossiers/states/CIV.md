# Côte d'Ivoire

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope.** Police and judiciary are not restricted generically.

## 2. Evidence supporting the determination

Hundreds were arrested in the October 2025 pre-election opposition-protest crackdown. As of March 2026 some non-participants remained in pretrial detention and others had reportedly been convicted after proceedings lacking counsel.

## 3. Counter-evidence

Other large opposition demonstrations in 2025 occurred peacefully, demonstrating that coercive repression was not uniform across all political assembly. Ordinary court structures remain.

## 4. Attribution and exclusions

Scope follows the 2025 crackdown, responsible units and continuing detention/prosecution processes, plus any renewed materially comparable operation.

## 5. Adversarial determination

**NARROW S.**

## 6. Review triggers

Release/remedy or fair review of remaining cases, abuse investigation and demonstrated non-recurrence.
