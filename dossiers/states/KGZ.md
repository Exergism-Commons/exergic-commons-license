# Kyrgyzstan

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope.**

## 2. Evidence preserving scoped restriction

UN Special Procedures continued to document alleged arbitrary detention/prosecution of peaceful rights advocates, arrests and alleged torture of members of a religious minority and a broader crackdown on independent media. Other 2026 legal concerns involved privacy, association and equality.

## 3. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

A 2025 constitutional law strengthened the Ombudsman/Akyikatchy as a constitutional rights institution and expanded powers during emergency/martial law. The institution actively names violating State bodies, monitors law enforcement and courts, and performs prison inspections.

## 4. Attribution and exclusions

The scoped finding covers identified coercive security, prosecution, media and discriminatory-enforcement systems, not the Ombudsman or unrelated public functions.

## 5. Adversarial determination

**DOWNGRADE R → S.** Active constitutional oversight makes whole-State attribution too broad.

## 6. Review triggers

Effective prosecutions/remedies for torture/arbitrary detention, durable media/civic-space restoration and demonstrated implementation of Ombudsman recommendations.
