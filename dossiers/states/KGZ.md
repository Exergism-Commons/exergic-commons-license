# Kyrgyzstan

> **Governance record only.** This dossier does not itself create licensing restrictions. Only a versioned Schedule expressly incorporated by a software release has operative effect.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope.** The earlier whole-State `R` was correctly narrowed to `S`; current review preserves and clarifies that scope.

## 2. ECL criteria engaged

- political repression through arbitrary/politically motivated detention and prosecution;
- torture or coercive abuse in detention;
- coercive media and civic-space control;
- discriminatory or vague extremism/religious enforcement suppressing protected conduct.

## 3. Evidence supporting the determination

The current record documents prosecution of journalists and activists under broad extremism and mass-unrest provisions, including the continuing 2026 litigation concerning journalist Makhabat Tazhibek-kyzy after the UN Working Group on Arbitrary Detention found her detention arbitrary. The 2025/26 record also includes alleged torture of members of a religious minority, restrictive religious legislation, mandatory/controlled media regulation and foreign-representative/civic-space restrictions.

These are sufficiently persistent and attributable to support `S`, but remain identifiable coercive systems rather than proof that every State function participates in the prohibited project.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

The Ombudsman/Akyikatchy has strengthened statutory/constitutional functions, identifies rights violations by State bodies, monitors law enforcement and courts and conducts detention/prison oversight. That independent institutional role is material counter-evidence even where its recommendations do not fully remediate the underlying conduct.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions

Covered scope follows identified security/prosecutorial projects involving politically motivated detention or torture, coercive media/civic-control enforcement, and discriminatory/vague extremism or religious enforcement materially suppressing protected conduct.

The Ombudsman/Akyikatchy, genuinely independent remedial judicial functions, unrelated State services and Kyrgyz people/nationality are excluded.

## 6. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD / DEFINE S AFTER PRIOR `R → S`.** Current coercive projects remain substantiated, but active oversight functions continue to make renewed whole-State attribution overbroad.

## 7. Review / removal triggers

- effective remedy for arbitrary or politically motivated detention;
- independent investigation/prosecution of torture allegations;
- durable restoration of independent media and civic space;
- rights-compliant religious/extremism enforcement;
- demonstrated implementation of Ombudsman findings and recommendations.
