# Azerbaijan

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**R — State apparatus Restricted.**

## 2. ECL criteria engaged

Political repression, arbitrary/politically motivated detention, media/civic-space suppression and transnational pressure.

## 3. Evidence supporting the determination

PACE Resolution 2663 of 24 June 2026 describes a systemic pattern of silencing critical voices, reports 328 political prisoners as of May 2026, highlights renewed prosecution of Anar Mammadli and notes many leading ECtHR judgments awaiting implementation. The conduct spans prosecution, detention, media, party and NGO regulation.

## 4. Counter-evidence

Azerbaijan resumed cooperation with the Council of Europe's CPT, retains formal courts and remains subject to ECtHR judgments. These mechanisms had not demonstrated effective restraint of the cross-institutional pattern at the cutoff.

## 5. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD R.**

## 6. Review triggers

Release/remedy for political prisoners, implementation of leading ECtHR judgments, restored media/party/NGO contestability and demonstrable independent judicial restraint.
