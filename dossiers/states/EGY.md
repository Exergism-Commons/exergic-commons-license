# Egypt

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**R — State apparatus Restricted.**

## 2. Evidence supporting the determination

April 2026 UN Special Procedures material concerning North Sinai raised allegations of unlawful killings, mass arbitrary detention, enforced disappearance, secret detention, torture, displacement and destruction of civilian property, implicating Egyptian security forces and government-supported militias and potentially engaging international crimes. Other recent communications concern national-security/counterterrorism law used against peaceful activity.

## 3. Counter-evidence

Egypt enacted a new Criminal Procedure Law with stated safeguards on pretrial detention, judicial review, counsel and privacy. It does not enter into force until **1 October 2026**, so it cannot yet be counted as implemented remediation at the August cutoff.

## 4. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD R. Mandatory fresh review after the new law has operated long enough to assess implementation.**

## 5. Review triggers

Post-1 October 2026 implementation data; reduced arbitrary/pretrial detention; independent investigation of North Sinai abuses; effective counsel/judicial review and accountability for torture/disappearance.
