# Kenya

> **Governance record only.** This dossier does not itself create licensing restrictions. Only a versioned Schedule expressly incorporated by a software release has operative effect.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope.** The current restriction follows attributable coercive public-order/security projects, not Kenya's State apparatus or police generally.

## 2. ECL criteria engaged

- enforced disappearance and coercive targeting;
- torture or other serious unlawful treatment in custody;
- arbitrary detention and unlawful force against protected protest;
- coercive surveillance, intimidation or media suppression that materially destroys civic contestability.

## 3. Evidence supporting the determination

On 29 June 2026 the Kenya National Commission on Human Rights (KNCHR) reported serious violations connected to the 25 June protests, including seven incidents of enforced disappearance. Six protesters arrested outside Parliament were subsequently released after alleged secret detention and severe torture; KNCHR also documented arbitrary arrests, unlawful force and media-freedom violations linked to security operations.

The direct documentation by a constitutional national human-rights institution provides strong current evidence of a concrete coercive project and supports high confidence in a scoped `S`.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

KNCHR itself is an important exergic counter-institution: it monitored the protests, documented individual cases and publicly demanded investigation and accountability. Kenya also retains independent oversight, prosecutorial and judicial mechanisms capable of investigating police/security misconduct. Their existence and operation weigh against escalation to `R`.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions

Covered scope follows police, DCI or other attributable security/public-order units and operations materially involved in enforced disappearance, torture, unlawful protest force, arbitrary protest detention, intimidation or coercive protest surveillance/media suppression.

Excluded: KNCHR, courts, genuinely independent oversight and prosecutorial functions, unrelated police/public services, and Kenyan people/nationality.

## 6. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD / DEFINE S.** June 2026 evidence strengthens the scoped finding. It does not support whole-State escalation because attribution is project/unit-specific and functioning counter-institutions are actively contesting the abuse.

## 7. Review / removal triggers

- identification and prosecution/remedy in the June 2026 disappearance/torture cases;
- reliable arrest/custody registration and anti-secret-detention safeguards;
- sustained proportionality and accountability in protest policing;
- independent limits on coercive surveillance;
- durable protection of journalists, monitors and peaceful protesters.
