# El Salvador

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**R — State apparatus Restricted.**

## 2. Evidence supporting the determination

UN Special Procedures continue to raise concerns about detention incompatible with safeguards against arbitrary detention, enforced disappearance, torture and inhuman conditions, including 2026 follow-up involving persons transferred from the United States. The broader emergency record concerns mass detention without ordinary safeguards, short-term disappearance, torture/ill-treatment and excessive police/military force across an emergency legal, prosecutorial and detention architecture.

## 3. Counter-evidence

Formal courts/prosecutors remain and the government has replied to some UN communications. The review did not identify internal remedies operating at a scale sufficient to rebut the emergency system.

## 4. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD R.**

## 5. Review triggers

Mass release/remedy, judicial invalidation of abusive emergency detention practices, prosecutions for torture/disappearance, restoration of ordinary safeguards and demonstrated non-recurrence.
