# Sudan

> **Governance record only.** This dossier does not itself create licensing restrictions. Only a versioned Schedule expressly incorporated by a software release has operative effect.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope.** The finding attaches to SAF and specifically attributable State-aligned conflict/security projects. Sudan's fragmented civil war makes a unitary whole-State finding inaccurate.

## 2. ECL criteria engaged

- unlawful or indiscriminate coercive violence against civilians;
- reprisal targeting of civilians, defenders and perceived collaborators;
- arbitrary detention, torture or killings by attributable security structures;
- material obstruction of humanitarian or accountability activity.

## 3. Evidence supporting the determination

Amnesty's April 2026 assessment documents SAF air strikes on civilian areas and reprisal attacks by SAF and allied forces against civilians and human-rights defenders perceived as RSF collaborators, alongside arbitrary detention and killings. These are current, attributable conflict projects.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

The civil war is deeply fragmented. Amnesty's July 2026 El Fasher investigation separately attributes crimes against humanity and ethnic-cleansing-type conduct to the RSF. Those acts cannot be silently assigned to the SAF/state-aligned apparatus. Civilian, humanitarian, medical and accountability networks are likewise outside the implicated scope unless separately evidenced.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions

Scope covers **SAF and specifically attributable State-aligned military/security units or projects materially carrying out unlawful/indiscriminate attacks on civilians, reprisals, arbitrary detention, torture, killings or material obstruction of humanitarian/accountability activity**. RSF and other independent armed actors require separate attribution/designation. Civilian institutions, emergency-response networks and independent humanitarian/remedial functions are excluded.

## 6. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD / DEFINE S WITH SAF/RSF SEPARATION.** Current SAF-attributable abuse supports a stable scoped finding, while fragmentation makes any whole-State or cross-party attribution unsound.

## 7. Review / removal triggers

Verified cessation of unlawful attacks/reprisals, effective command accountability, protected humanitarian access, release/remedy for arbitrary detainees and a civilian legal order with effective independent oversight of implicated forces.
