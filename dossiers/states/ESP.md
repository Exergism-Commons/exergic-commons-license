# Spain

> **Governance record only.** No licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination
**U — Under Review.**

## 2. ECL criteria engaged
Potential coercive targeting of peaceful protest/expression, covert surveillance/infiltration and displacement enforcement.

## 3. Evidence supporting continued review
The 2025/26 record includes unjust protest convictions, investigations of non-violent climate activists as a criminal organization, unresolved police infiltration of social movements and accountability concerns around force/evictions.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions
A protester was pardoned/released; complaints remain before the Constitutional Court; courts, Parliament, ombuds institutions and democratic contestability remain strong. The current evidence does not isolate a stable systematic ECL project.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions
No Spanish State/police system is restricted. Review follows specific public-order, infiltration/surveillance or eviction projects if current coercive deployment crosses threshold.

## 6. Adversarial determination

## 7. Review / removal triggers
