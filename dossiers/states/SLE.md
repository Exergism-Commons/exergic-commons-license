# Sierra Leone

> **Governance record only.** No licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination
**U — Under Review.**

## 2. ECL criteria engaged
Coercive information control and suppression of protected expression.

## 3. Evidence supporting continued review
The Cybersecurity and Crime Act retains criminal libel/defamation provisions previously used against protected criticism; proposed media legislation could create severe publication penalties.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions
The key Hawa Hunt case ended in release in 2025, and the most severe additional media restrictions remained proposed rather than shown as a fresh systematic 2026 enforcement project. Other rights-protective reforms also proceeded.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions
No State/media system is restricted. Review follows actual cyber/libel or media-regulatory enforcement materially targeting protected journalism/expression.

## 6. Adversarial determination
**RETAIN U.**

## 7. Review / removal triggers
Escalate on renewed systematic cyber/libel detention or enactment/deployment of coercive media controls; move to `N` on decriminalization/non-use and durable press safeguards.
