# Fiji

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**U — Under Review.** The April 2026 death of Jone Vakarisi in military custody is grave and remains an ECL review trigger, but a stable `S` should not be preserved solely from an unresolved incident while police/coronial investigation and military cooperation are materially active.

## 2. Potential ECL criteria

Torture/ill-treatment, arbitrary coercive detention, concealment/obstruction and coercive suppression of protected reporting may be engaged if the investigation substantiates the relevant conduct and attribution.

## 3. Evidence requiring continued review

Vakarisi died after being taken to Queen Elizabeth Barracks for questioning. The RFMF later corrected its initial public account after the post-mortem record and police treated the death as a serious criminal investigation. Human-rights reporting also raised freedom-of-expression concerns around initial national-security messaging.

## 4. Material counter-evidence

Police opened an investigation immediately, the matter was referred for inquest, investigators obtained extensive witness evidence and by June were seeking interviews with identified military personnel. The RFMF publicly committed to cooperation and accountability. These are concrete remedial processes rather than merely announced reforms.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions

No RFMF, police or joint-operation class is currently Schedule-ready from this dossier. Courts, police investigators, coronial processes, independent rights oversight and unrelated military/police activity are excluded.

## 6. Adversarial determination

**DOWNGRADE `S → U`.** The underlying event remains serious, but current evidence supports continued investigation rather than an operative institutional/project restriction before the independent process establishes responsibility, project boundary and remediation status.

## 7. Review / escalation triggers

Escalate toward `S` on substantiated responsibility, obstruction, recurrence or evidence that abusive joint/military detention practices form a continuing project. Move toward `N` on credible accountability, remediation, safeguards and sustained non-recurrence.
