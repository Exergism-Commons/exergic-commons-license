# Fiji

> **Governance record only.** No licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination
**S — Restricted with defined scope.**

## 2. ECL criteria engaged
Torture/ill-treatment, arbitrary coercive detention and suppression of protected expression.

## 3. Evidence supporting determination
In 2026 Jone Vakarisi died in military custody; reporting on the autopsy raised serious assault concerns, while military authorities initially gave a conflicting account and invoked national-security considerations around discussion. Restrictive civic/public-order laws remain in force.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions
Courts, civilian institutions, media and international UPR scrutiny remain operative. The evidence does not support restriction of the State or military generically.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions
Scope is limited to military/police detention and national-security/public-order projects materially involving ill-treatment, concealment/obstruction or coercive suppression of protected speech. Independent courts/remedy and unrelated functions are excluded.

## 6. Adversarial determination
**ESCALATE U → S.**

## 7. Review / removal triggers
Independent investigation/prosecution of custody abuse, transparent detention safeguards, repeal/non-use of repressive civic laws and non-recurrence.
