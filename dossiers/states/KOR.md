## 1. Current determination
**N — No current state-level basis.**

## 2. ECL criteria engaged
Prior review concerned public-order/assembly criminalization.

## 3. Evidence supporting review
Disability-rights and other peaceful protesters have faced prosecution and restrictive assembly practice.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions
Courts invalidated excessive restrictions and the Constitutional Court held the 2024 martial-law declaration unconstitutional. The constitutional response demonstrates strong, effective contestability and no current stable ECL-prohibited public-order project at cutoff.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions
No State/public-order system restriction is supported. Specific future police/prosecution projects remain reviewable.

## 6. Adversarial determination

## 7. Review / removal triggers
