# Qatar

> **Governance record only.** This dossier does not itself create licensing restrictions. Only a versioned Schedule expressly incorporated by a software release has operative effect.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope.** The finding is limited to current expression/surveillance enforcement and specifically evidenced labour-administration failures that materially enable coercive exploitation despite formal reforms.

## 2. ECL criteria engaged

- repressive surveillance and coercive targeting of protected communications/expression;
- political repression of peaceful association/assembly;
- systematic coercive labour domination where State-administered safeguards fail in ways that materially enable forced-labour-like dependency or retaliation.

## 3. Evidence supporting the determination

Amnesty's current Qatar assessment continues to identify tight restrictions on expression and peaceful assembly and continuing migrant-worker vulnerability to exploitation. A June 2026 joint Amnesty/Human Rights Watch statement described intensified phone searches, arrests and restrictions on expression across GCC governments, including a climate of fear affecting migrant workers' communications.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

Qatar has made substantial labour-law reforms. The ILO's February 2026 assessment describes a continuing 2024–2028 technical-cooperation programme built on improvements in labour mobility, wage protection, social dialogue and labour administration. These reforms materially defeat any claim that the pre-reform kafala architecture remains intact as a single current coercive system.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions

Scope covers **security/cybercrime/public-order or surveillance projects materially punishing protected expression, peaceful association or private communications, and specifically evidenced labour-administration/enforcement failures that materially enable forced-labour-like coercion, retaliation, blocked job mobility or unrecoverable wage abuse despite formal safeguards**. The labour system, employers, ministries and migrant-work framework are not Restricted generically. Labour-complaint, remediation and ILO-cooperation mechanisms are excluded.

## 6. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD / NARROW S.** Expression/surveillance concerns remain current, while major labour reforms require much more precise attribution than the original generic labour-migration/guardianship framing.

## 7. Review / removal triggers

Durable cessation of speech/communications repression, independent review of phone/search and cybercrime powers, measurable enforcement of labour mobility/wage safeguards and effective worker complaint/remedy without retaliation.
