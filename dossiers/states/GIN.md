# Guinea

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**R — current governing apparatus Restricted.**

## 2. Evidence supporting the determination

Current records document suspension/dissolution of political parties and media, a protest ban, imprisonment of opposition figures and enforced disappearances/abductions of critics. In July 2026 regional rights organizations reported continuing disappearances with impunity, including cases involving children. Major opposition parties were excluded/dissolved before June 2026 legislative elections.

## 3. Counter-evidence

Guinea adopted a new Constitution and held elections; the Supreme Court formally reviews electoral challenges. Prosecutors also announced investigations into some abductions. These steps are meaningful but had not produced demonstrated remedy at the cutoff.

## 4. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD R**, with a mandatory remediation-milestone review.

## 5. Review triggers

Prosecutions/results in disappearance cases, recovery/remedy for victims, restored opposition participation, lifting of protest/media restrictions and independently competitive elections.
