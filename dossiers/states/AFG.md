# Afghanistan

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**R — Taliban de facto governing/enforcement apparatus.** The finding does not attach to Afghan people.

## 2. ECL criteria engaged

Systematic discriminatory domination, arbitrary detention, coercive morality enforcement, violent suppression of protest and severe destruction of women's and girls' agency.

## 3. Evidence supporting the determination

UNAMA reported in May 2026 that Decree No. 18 further entrenched systemic discrimination against women and girls in law and practice. In June 2026 UNAMA reported arbitrary arrests of women by morality officials and police and excessive force against protesters, including a boy killed by gunfire. The system is implemented through justice, morality, police, education and administrative organs.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

Some detainees are released after short periods, humanitarian/development cooperation continues and earlier decrees formally recognized limited rights such as consent to marriage and inheritance. These do not rebut the foundational cross-institutional character of the coercive system.

## 5. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD R.**

## 6. Review triggers

Material repeal/non-enforcement of discriminatory decrees, restoration of women's education/work/public participation, independent judicial remedies and effective accountability for arbitrary detention/violence.
