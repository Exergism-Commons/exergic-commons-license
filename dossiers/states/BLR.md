# Belarus

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**R — State apparatus Restricted.**

## 2. Evidence supporting the determination

2026 UN Special Procedures communications continued to document politically motivated detention, prolonged incommunicado/solitary confinement, ill-treatment, inadequate medical care and due-process violations. The Human Rights Council adopted a new Belarus resolution in March 2026.

## 3. Counter-evidence

Negotiated releases/expulsions of some prisoners occurred and the government replied to some UN communications. Individual releases reduce harm but do not establish remediation of the detention, security, judicial and information-control systems.

## 4. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD R.**

## 5. Review triggers

Systematic political-prisoner release, independent investigations into torture/ill-treatment, restoration of civic/media space and durable judicial remedies.
