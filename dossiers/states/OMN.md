# Oman

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**R — State apparatus Restricted.**

## 2. Evidence supporting the determination

A 2025 nationality law permits citizenship revocation for vague conduct including offending Oman/the Sultan or joining organizations deemed harmful to State interests. Authorities continued summoning/arresting/prosecuting peaceful activists and expanded media censorship/surveillance regulation. Migrant domestic workers remain exposed to coercive kafala dependence and gender discrimination persists in nationality/family systems.

## 3. Counter-evidence

Oman introduced minimum employment standards for domestic workers and maintains formal courts/appellate review; one activist's prison term was reduced on appeal. Structured nationality procedures also exist. These do not establish independent rights-protective restraint over the core coercive systems.

## 4. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD R.**

## 5. Review triggers

Narrowing/removal of vague nationality powers, durable expression/media reform, effective migrant-worker exit protections and independent judicial remedies for political cases.
