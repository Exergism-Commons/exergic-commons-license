# Bhutan

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope.** The present democratic/constitutional State is not classified wholesale based on legacy political-prisoner cases.

## 2. Evidence supporting the determination

At least 28 known political prisoners remained detained in June 2026, many under life sentences arising from pre-democratic prosecutions involving alleged torture, lack of defence counsel and severe prison conditions. UN mechanisms have found or raised arbitrary-detention concerns in several cases.

## 3. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

On 1 June 2026 Bhutan released Chatur Man Tamang and Hasta Bahadur Rai after more than 18 years pursuant to royal clemency. That is concrete remediation. Bhutan's post-2008 constitutional/democratic order is also institutionally distinct from the historical system that produced many of the convictions.

## 4. Attribution and exclusions

Scope is limited to unresolved political-prisoner detention, responsible detention/security functions and continuing enforcement of legacy convictions where detention remains arbitrary or materially linked to torture/unfair-trial findings.

## 5. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD S, credit 2026 remediation.**

## 6. Review triggers

Release or fair independent review of remaining political prisoners, medical/remedial support, accountability and guarantees of non-repetition.
