# Tuvalu

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**N — No current State-level or defined-project basis.** Tuvalu's central rights challenge is climate displacement and existential environmental harm, not a materially deployed State-directed ECL §5 prohibited project.

## 2. ECL criteria tested

Repressive surveillance, unlawful software-enabled targeting, automated discriminatory domination, deceptive population manipulation and coercive technological capture were tested.

## 3. Evidence requiring scrutiny

Climate-relocation, migration and digital-government systems remain reviewable where they could materially affect consent, identity, mobility or contestability.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

Current State and international work is oriented toward climate adaptation, mobility and protection. No reviewed evidence establishes systematic prohibited State deployment.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions

Climate harms caused by global emitters are not attributed to Tuvalu. No Tuvaluan institution, population or nationality is designated.

## 6. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD `N`.** Severe vulnerability is not equivalent to attributable coercive State project.

## 7. Review triggers

Reopen on project-specific evidence of repressive surveillance, unlawful targeting, automated discrimination or coercive information-control infrastructure.
