# Namibia

> **Governance record only.** No licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination
**N — No current state-level basis.**

## 2. ECL criteria engaged
Prior review concerned discriminatory LGBTI legal/administrative enforcement.

## 3. Evidence supporting review
Discriminatory legal and administrative rules have created serious rights harms and remain appropriate review triggers.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions
Namibian courts have produced major rights-protective judgments, including recognition of same-sex marriages concluded abroad and invalidation of colonial-era sodomy provisions. Current evidence does not establish a stable coercive enforcement project surviving those interventions.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions
No State or administrative project is currently restricted. Future discriminatory enforcement contrary to judicial safeguards remains reviewable.

## 6. Adversarial determination
**DOWNGRADE U → N.**

## 7. Review / removal triggers
Reopen on systematic discriminatory policing/administration, circumvention of rights-protective judgments or new coercive anti-LGBTI legislation/enforcement.
