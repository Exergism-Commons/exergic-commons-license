# Equatorial Guinea

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope.**

## 2. Evidence supporting the determination

Defender Joaquín Elo Ayeto remained unaccounted for after prison transfer, while Anacleto Micha Ndong Nlang remained incommunicado despite a WGAD opinion calling for release. In April 2026 UN experts raised imminent non-refoulement concerns regarding persons transferred from the United States and detained in Malabo.

## 3. Counter-evidence with material effect

A June 2025 presidential pardon covered 476 prisoners, including 37 Annobón protesters whose detention WGAD had found arbitrary. Pardoned/released persons leave the active detention scope.

## 4. Attribution and exclusions

Scope follows identified arbitrary/incommunicado detention, disappearance/torture/political repression and the specific third-country detention/transfer/refoulement project lacking required safeguards.

## 5. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD / NARROW S**, expressly crediting mass clemency.

## 6. Review triggers

Implementation of WGAD opinions, disclosure/release/remedy for defenders, independent detention oversight and lawful individualized protection screening for transferred migrants.
