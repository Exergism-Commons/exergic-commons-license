# Myanmar

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**R — military-backed/de facto governing apparatus.**

## 2. Evidence supporting the determination

The military-backed 2026 electoral process excluded genuine opposition, criminalized criticism/disruption and occurred amid military control and areas where voting was impossible. Political prisoners including Aung San Suu Kyi remain detained; military offensives continued even while regional actors explored talks. The governing/repression architecture remains controlled by the military leadership that seized power in 2021.

## 3. Counter-evidence

The authorities completed an electoral process, installed a formally elected government/president and in August 2026 allowed an unusual Red Cross/foreign-representative visit to Suu Kyi; regional diplomacy has explored talks. These do not amount to independent counter-institutions or effective remediation.

## 4. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD R.**

## 5. Review triggers

Genuine negotiated civilian transition, release of political prisoners, restored political competition, effective accountability and cessation of military attacks/repressive electoral controls.
