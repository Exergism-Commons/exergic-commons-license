# Croatia

> **Governance record only.** No licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination
**S — Restricted with defined scope.**

## 2. ECL criteria engaged
Coercive targeting, unlawful return and violence against vulnerable migrants.

## 3. Evidence supporting determination
The current 2025/26 record reports that refugees and migrants intercepted at Croatia's borders continue to face violence and pushback-type practices. Attribution is sufficiently specific to border policing.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions
Courts, EU/international monitoring, journalists and civil society provide contestability; SLAPP/media pressures do not by themselves justify expanding the restriction.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions
Only border-police operations materially involving violence, summary return or denial of protection are in scope. Courts, independent monitoring, humanitarian aid and unrelated police/public functions are excluded.

## 6. Adversarial determination
**ESCALATE U → S.**

## 7. Review / removal triggers
Cessation of pushbacks/violence, independent border monitoring, individualized asylum access and credible investigation/remedy.
