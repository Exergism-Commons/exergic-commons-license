# Georgia

> **Governance record only.** This dossier does not itself create licensing restrictions. Only a versioned Schedule expressly incorporated by a software release has operative effect.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope.** The 2026 record strongly supports current coercive civic/public-order projects, but a whole-State `R` remains overbroad because independent oversight and constitutional review continue to produce material contrary outcomes.

## 2. ECL criteria engaged

Current review concerns:

- coercive suppression of protected peaceful assembly and political activity;
- unlawful or disproportionate police force and arbitrary detention;
- civic-space/funding controls materially disabling independent association;
- media/information controls materially coercing protected journalism or expression.

## 3. Evidence supporting `S`

The Council of Europe Commissioner for Human Rights' June 2026 memorandum found continuing accountability failures for disproportionate force against protesters and mounting restrictions on peaceful assembly, association and expression. It identified coercive foreign-influence/grant controls, proceedings against civil-society organisations, frozen accounts, restrictions on foreign-funded media and legal actions affecting opposition actors.

These are current, attributable executive/police/prosecutorial/administrative projects rather than historical concerns.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

Georgia's Public Defender continues challenging assembly restrictions before the Constitutional Court and litigating rights questions. The Constitutional Court has accepted and granted rights-protective claims. Five police officers were criminally charged over disproportionate force, and legislation requiring visible police identification was proposed.

These mechanisms remain materially distinct from the coercive projects and defeat a whole-State attribution unless they themselves become captured or ineffective.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions

The active scope covers **executive/police public-order projects materially using unlawful force, arbitrary detention or coercive penalties against protected peaceful assembly; prosecutorial/administrative projects materially targeting opposition or protected civic activity; foreign-influence/grant/registration systems materially suppressing independent civil society; and media/information-control projects materially coercing protected journalism or expression**.

The Public Defender, Constitutional Court, genuinely independent prosecution/accountability and unrelated public services are excluded.

## 6. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD `S` after prior `R → S`.** Current deterioration is serious and persistent, but functioning counter-institutions still make whole-State restriction inaccurate.

## 7. Review / removal triggers

**Move toward `U/N`** on effective accountability for protest violence, repeal or rights-compliant narrowing of coercive civic-space/funding controls, restoration of independent media/political activity and sustained implementation of Constitutional Court/Public Defender safeguards.

**Re-escalate toward `R`** only if rights-protective courts/oversight themselves become materially captured or ineffective across the relevant coercive system.
