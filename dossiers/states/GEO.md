# Georgia

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope.**

## 2. Evidence preserving scoped restriction

The Council of Europe Commissioner reported substantial accountability delays despite hundreds of ill-treatment reports, mounting restrictions on assembly, association, media and civil-society funding and legal actions against opposition actors. The Public Defender continues to challenge restrictions as incompatible with expression/assembly rights.

## 3. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

Georgia's Public Defender actively challenges assembly restrictions before the Constitutional Court and files amicus submissions. In April 2026 the Constitutional Court granted a Public Defender suit concerning compulsory psychiatric treatment. Five police officers were criminally charged over disproportionate force, and police identification reforms were proposed.

## 4. Attribution and exclusions

The Public Defender, Constitutional Court and genuinely independent accountability functions are excluded from the scoped coercive project.

## 5. Adversarial determination

**DOWNGRADE R → S.** Functioning counter-institutions make a whole-State designation overbroad.

## 6. Review triggers

Effective prosecution/remediation of police violence, repeal/narrowing of coercive civic-space controls and restoration of independent political/media activity.
