# Bulgaria

> **Governance record only.** No licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**U — Monitor / unresolved for current restriction.** The record continues to justify close governance monitoring, but the 2026 Schedule revalidation did not establish a sufficiently current, attributable and objectively knowable abusive project narrow enough to sustain `S` without inferring continuity from earlier events.

## 2. Evidence retained in the governance record

The April 2025 Zaharna Fabrika demolitions remain serious evidence concerning forced eviction of Roma residents and adequacy of housing/remedy. The first 2026 FRA migration bulletin also records substantial 2025 Bulgaria-Türkiye border concerns, including summary returns, reports of violence and concerns around delayed or deficient life-protective assistance.

In July 2026 the Council of Europe Commissioner for Human Rights continued to express concern about the incidence of forced evictions of Roma and specifically referenced Zaharna Fabrika, while also recording positive inclusion measures and areas of progress.

## 3. Current-status and counter-evidence

The Zaharna Fabrika operation was a completed 2025 event. Subsequent ECHR-related accommodation/remedial measures materially changed the status of that exact project, so the completed demolition cannot simply be treated as a continuing Restricted Project.

The FRA bulletin covering April-June 2026 does not supply a new Bulgaria-specific border operation, unit or finite project with sufficiently precise identity and current abusive attribution to freeze. Current ordinary anti-smuggling and border-control activity is not restricted merely because it occurs in the same institutional environment.

## 4. Attribution and exclusions

The prior governance concerns do **not** restrict Bulgaria, Bulgarian Border Police, Sofia Municipality, Bulgarian security services, municipal authorities or border functions generically. Courts, Ombudsman functions, accommodation/remediation work, rights monitoring and lawful border enforcement are excluded.

A later `S` finding would require evidence tied to a fresh objectively knowable eviction, border-return, non-assistance or related coercive project, or reliable evidence that a previously identified finite project remains materially unremediated.

## 5. Adversarial determination

**DOWNGRADE `S` → `U`.** The evidence is serious enough to retain and monitor, but not strong enough in August 2026 to support a current Schedule restriction without presuming continuity from 2025.

## 6. Review / re-escalation triggers

- a new named forced-eviction operation or project with inadequate remedy;
- a named border unit, operation or finite project materially participating in unlawful pushback, violence or denial of life-protective assistance;
- reliable current evidence that a finite 2025 project remains materially unremediated; or
- authoritative findings materially changing the current remediation analysis.
