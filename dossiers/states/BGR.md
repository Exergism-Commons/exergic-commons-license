# Bulgaria

> **Governance record only.** No licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination
**S — Restricted with defined scope.**

## 2. ECL criteria engaged
Discriminatory coercion, forced displacement and coercive border targeting.

## 3. Evidence supporting determination
The 2025/26 record documents forced eviction of Roma residents without adequate due process/remedy and serious border non-assistance/abuse concerns affecting migrants. These are attributable enforcement projects rather than abstract legislative risk.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions
Courts, Ombudsman and rights litigation remain active; a proposed foreign-agent measure was rejected. These defeat a broader State/civic-space finding.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions
Scope is limited to identified municipal/public-authority eviction projects and border operations materially involving unlawful coercion, discriminatory displacement, pushback or denial of life-protective assistance. Courts/remediation and unrelated functions are excluded.

## 6. Adversarial determination
**ESCALATE U → S.**

## 7. Review / removal triggers
Adequate housing/remedy for unlawful evictions, independently monitored border safeguards, investigation of deaths/abuse and sustained non-recurrence.
