# Austria

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**U — Under Review.** Austria no longer cleanly supports `N` because restrictive asylum/family-reunification and return measures are actively deployed. The present record still does not establish a sufficiently specific ECL §5 prohibited project for `S`.

## 2. ECL criteria tested

Review focused on coercive targeting, discriminatory automated/data-driven enforcement, repressive surveillance and coercive technological capture in migration administration.

## 3. Evidence supporting review

Austria suspended/froze family reunification for protection beneficiaries and pursued stricter asylum/return policy, including return to Syria. These measures materially affect fundamental rights and require project-level scrutiny, especially if implemented through automated risk, surveillance or targeting systems.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

The measures remain subject to Austrian constitutional/administrative courts, EU law, the European Convention on Human Rights and European Commission scrutiny. Current evidence does not show a qualifying automated discriminatory system or unlawful targeting architecture as the primary purpose of the policy.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions

Review follows specific migration/return implementation, not Austria, its population, courts or public administration generally.

## 6. Adversarial determination

**ESCALATE `N → U`.** Active rights-restrictive policy defeats a confident `N`, but policy severity alone cannot substitute for the software/project nexus required by ECL §5.

## 7. Review triggers

Escalate to `S` on evidence of systematic unlawful targeting, data-driven discriminatory denial, repressive surveillance, or technologically enforced non-contestable exclusion. Return to `N` if measures lapse or operate with effective individualized safeguards and no qualifying ECL project emerges.
