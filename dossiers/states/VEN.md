# Venezuela

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**R — State apparatus Restricted provisionally**, subject to a mandatory transition review.

## 2. Evidence supporting the determination

In March 2026 the UN fact-finding mission reported that the repressive State apparatus remained operational after the January leadership change and that institutional structures facilitating violations had not been dismantled; politically motivated detentions continued and officials previously linked to abuses retained power. Current evidence also indicates incomplete/opaque releases while restrictive judicial/security structures persist.

## 3. Counter-evidence

Authorities adopted an amnesty framework, released significant numbers of prisoners, expanded some humanitarian detention access and announced reforms including proposed transformation of El Helicoide. These changes are material and could justify rapid reclassification if implemented structurally.

## 4. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD R provisionally.** Reform trajectory is real but has not yet dismantled the cross-institutional coercive architecture.

## 5. Review triggers

Transparent completion of political-prisoner releases; restored judicial/prosecutorial independence; reform/dismantling of implicated intelligence/security structures; accountability for torture/killings; credible competitive elections.
