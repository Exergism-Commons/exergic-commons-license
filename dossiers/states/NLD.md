# Netherlands

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope.** OXREC is outside the active scope while its announced temporary suspension remains in effect. The current basis is limited to probation risk algorithms still used without demonstrated responsible validation.

## 2. ECL criteria engaged

Automated/data-driven profiling that can materially influence criminal-justice recommendations while remaining defective, discriminatory or insufficiently contestable.

## 3. Evidence supporting the determination

On 12 February 2026 the independent Justice and Security Inspectorate found that the probation organizations did not use `STATIC-99R`, `STABLE-2007` and `ACUTE-2007` in a demonstrably responsible manner. The same investigation found serious defects in OXREC, but the probation organizations announced that OXREC would be temporarily stopped.

## 4. Counter-evidence and remediation

The Inspectorate is actively supervising remediation and requires periodic progress reporting. OXREC suspension is credited as material remediation and removes that system from current Schedule eligibility unless it is reactivated without adequate correction/validation.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions

Scope follows only the named probation risk tools while they remain materially deployed without adequate validation, safeguards and contestability. OXREC, withdrawn/corrected systems, courts exercising independent judgment, oversight bodies and unrelated public algorithms are excluded.

## 6. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD / NARROW `S`.** Current official evidence supports a live algorithmic-risk project but also requires exclusion of the system that was actually suspended.

## 7. Review / removal triggers

Independent validation, corrected models/data, privacy/equality compliance, meaningful professional override/contestability and verified retirement of defective tools.
