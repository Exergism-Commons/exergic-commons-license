# Netherlands

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope.** OXREC is outside the active scope while its announced temporary suspension remains in effect. The current basis is limited to probation risk algorithms still used without demonstrated responsible validation.

## 2. Formal Exergism assessment

Pilot assessment: [`../../exergism/assessments/NLD.json`](../../exergism/assessments/NLD.json).

The narrow probation-tool object is `scorable`, but the current formal envelopes are deliberately **uncertainty-sensitive**: both immediate ethics and structural balance cross zero under the reference profile. That does not mechanically remove the `S` finding. It means the aggregate formal layer cannot carry the decision by itself; the exact data-driven domination/profiling criterion, the documented validation defects, deployment status and remediation evidence remain decisive. This is a useful falsification result rather than a failure of the model.

## 3. ECL criteria engaged

Automated/data-driven profiling that can materially influence criminal-justice recommendations while remaining defective, discriminatory or insufficiently contestable.

## 4. Evidence supporting the determination

On 12 February 2026 the independent Justice and Security Inspectorate found that the probation organizations did not use `STATIC-99R`, `STABLE-2007` and `ACUTE-2007` in a demonstrably responsible manner. The same investigation found serious defects in OXREC, but the probation organizations announced that OXREC would be temporarily stopped.

## 5. Counter-evidence and remediation

The Inspectorate is actively supervising remediation and requires periodic progress reporting. OXREC suspension is credited as material remediation and removes that system from current Schedule eligibility unless it is reactivated without adequate correction/validation.

In the formal layer, this evidence raises `V_ep`, `L` and `O` and lowers the defensible `C` and `R` ranges compared with a closed, irreversible algorithmic-control system.

## 6. Attribution and exclusions

Scope follows only the named probation risk tools while they remain materially deployed without adequate validation, safeguards and contestability. OXREC, withdrawn/corrected systems, courts exercising independent judgment, oversight bodies and unrelated public algorithms are excluded.

## 7. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD / NARROW `S`.** Current official evidence supports a live algorithmic-risk project but also requires exclusion of the system that was actually suspended.

The formal pilot neither independently confirms nor defeats the legal/governance result; its interval crossing requires continued reliance on exact criterion fit and close attention to remediation.

## 8. Review / removal triggers

Independent validation, corrected models/data, privacy/equality compliance, meaningful professional override/contestability and verified retirement of defective tools. Successful remediation should materially increase `A`, `V_ep`, `L` and `O` while lowering `C`, `S` and `R`.
