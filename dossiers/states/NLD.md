# Netherlands

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope.** Systems that have been withdrawn, corrected and independently validated are outside the scope.

## 2. Evidence supporting the determination

In May 2026 the independent State Commission against Discrimination and Racism concluded that data-driven profiling for fraud/crime detection structurally conflicts with principles of good administration and creates discrimination or unacceptable discrimination risks. In February 2026 the Justice and Security Inspectorate found serious defects in probation risk algorithms, including a formula error causing recidivism misclassification in roughly a quarter of recommendations and variables capable of discriminatory effects.

## 3. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

The government updated the Human Rights and Algorithms Impact Assessment in February 2026, maintains algorithm-governance/registry mechanisms and supported a July 2026 technical agreement for discrimination testing. Independent oversight is active: the Inspectorate required rapid remediation or temporary suspension of a defective algorithm.

## 4. Adversarial determination

**NARROW S** to identified, deployed and unremediated defective/discriminatory/non-contestable systems. ECL should reward successful remediation rather than permanently stigmatize public algorithm use.

## 5. Removal trigger

Correction/withdrawal plus independent validation, robust fundamental-rights assessment, discrimination testing, notice and meaningful contestability.
