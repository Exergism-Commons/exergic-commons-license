# Brazil

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope.** Brazilian police/public security is not treated as one undifferentiated Restricted Party.

## 2. Evidence supporting the determination

Extremely high police lethality and recurring allegations of unlawful killings remain material, including the October 2025 Rio operation with more than one hundred civilian deaths. Structural racial disparities and repeated use-of-force concerns support project/unit-level ECL review.

## 3. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

The Supreme Federal Court actively supervises police policy under ADPF 635; in February 2026 it ordered Rio operation imagery sent to the Federal Police for forensic examination and required clarification of the State prosecutor's role. National Council of Prosecutors Resolution 310 sets standards for prosecutor-led investigation of deaths, sexual violence, torture and disappearance arising from public-security interventions.

## 4. Attribution and exclusions

Scope follows identified State-level police units/commands/operations with substantiated recurring unlawful lethal-force, torture or disappearance patterns and any investigation/forensic project materially obstructing accountability. STF, independent prosecution/forensics and remediation are excluded.

## 5. Adversarial determination

**NARROW S.** Federalism and active counter-institutions make generic national police attribution overbroad.

## 6. Review triggers

Implementation of Resolution 310, compliance with ADPF 635, independent prosecutions/remedies, sustained reduction in unlawful police lethality and racial-disparity remediation.
