# Bangladesh

> **Governance record only.** No licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination
**S — Restricted with defined scope.**

## 2. ECL criteria engaged
Coercive targeting, suppression of protected expression/association and arbitrary detention.

## 3. Evidence supporting determination
In 2026 journalists continued to face vague ICT and anti-terror charges tied to reporting and political speech. The broader transitional record includes arbitrary arrests, expression controls and political-association restrictions.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions
The transition also produced reform commissions, human-rights engagement, OPCAT accession and institutional change. Courts, reform bodies and rights-protective projects therefore defeat whole-system attribution.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions
Scope follows specific anti-terror/cyber/ICT/prosecutorial projects targeting protected journalism or peaceful politics. Courts, reform/remedial bodies, unrelated ministries and the population are excluded.

## 6. Adversarial determination
**ESCALATE U → S.** Current deployment supplies sufficient persistence and attribution.

## 7. Review / removal triggers
End vague political/media prosecutions, release/remedy for expression-only detention, rights-compliant cyber/terror law reform and durable independent judicial review.
