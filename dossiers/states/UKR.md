# Ukraine

> **Governance record only.** This dossier does not itself create licensing restrictions.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope.** The scope is **not ordinary conscription or territorial recruitment as a category**. It is limited to identified abusive TCC units/personnel and substantiated coercive mobilization/detention practices, plus separately evidenced collaboration-prosecution or security projects that independently meet ECL criteria.

## 2. ECL criteria engaged

Arbitrary detention, torture/ill-treatment, coercive deprivation of agency and unfair coercive enforcement where specifically evidenced.

## 3. Evidence supporting the determination

The original adjudication identified reports of forcible apprehension, violence and deaths involving some Territorial Recruitment Centres, prosecution of conscientious objectors and fair-trial concerns in some collaboration cases. Ukraine's status as victim of Russia's continuing aggression does not erase independently attributable ECL-relevant conduct by Ukrainian institutions.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

Ukraine's State Bureau of Investigation actively opens criminal cases against TCC personnel and police for violence, unlawful confinement, torture and registry manipulation. In 2026 it investigated deaths following TCC custody. The Ukrainian Ombudsman/National Preventive Mechanism continues inspections, formal submissions and referrals that can lead to criminal proceedings.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions

Ordinary lawful recruitment/conscription administration is excluded. The finding follows specifically substantiated abusive units, personnel and projects. Russian conduct on Ukrainian territory is separately attributed to Russia and must not be imputed to Ukraine.

## 6. Adversarial determination

**NARROW S.** Active accountability mechanisms make the original broad phrase `territorial recruitment, military-conscription` overinclusive.

## 7. Review / removal triggers

Completion of investigations/prosecutions, effective prevention of coercive TCC abuse, strengthened conscientious-objection safeguards and remediation of substantiated collaboration-prosecution fair-trial failures.
