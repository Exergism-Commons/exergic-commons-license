# Ukraine

> **Governance record only.** This dossier does not itself create licensing restrictions. Only a versioned Schedule expressly incorporated by a software release can have operative effect.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope.** The scope is **not ordinary conscription or territorial recruitment as a category**. It is limited to identified abusive TCC units/personnel and substantiated coercive mobilization/detention practices, plus separately evidenced collaboration-prosecution or security projects that independently meet ECL criteria.

One exact TCC incident is now frozen for Schedule rendering. The freeze follows the charged incident and materially participating roles only; it does not treat an indictment as a final finding of individual criminal guilt.

## 2. ECL criteria engaged

Arbitrary detention, torture/ill-treatment, coercive deprivation of agency and unfair coercive enforcement where specifically evidenced.

## 3. Evidence supporting the determination

The original adjudication identified reports of forcible apprehension, violence and deaths involving some Territorial Recruitment Centres, prosecution of conscientious objectors and fair-trial concerns in some collaboration cases. Ukraine's status as victim of Russia's continuing aggression does not erase independently attributable ECL-relevant conduct by Ukrainian institutions.

A current, objectively identifiable incident now supports a narrow Schedule subset. On 23 April 2026 the State Bureau of Investigation announced that it had completed the pretrial investigation and sent an indictment to court concerning the death of a mobilized man during a May 2025 transport from training centres in western Ukraine to Kyiv. The charged roles are a soldier of the **Shevchenkivskyi District TCC and SP of Kyiv** and the **deputy head of the Podilskyi District TCC and SP**, who was responsible for the transport.

According to the SBI's completed pretrial investigation, the soldier used physical force and an electric shock device against the man during the journey, while the deputy head failed to stop the conduct despite having the opportunity to do so. The man was pronounced dead after arrival in Kyiv. The SBI had previously reported in July 2025 that both suspects were detained while the investigation continued.

For ECL governance, the relevant point is not presumed criminal guilt. It is that the State's own investigative authority has defined a sufficiently concrete institutional incident, roles and alleged coercive conduct to support an incident-level boundary subject to explicit procedural review triggers.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

Ukraine's State Bureau of Investigation actively investigates TCC personnel and police for violence, unlawful confinement, torture and registry manipulation. The Ukrainian Ombudsman/National Preventive Mechanism continues inspections, formal submissions and referrals that can lead to criminal proceedings. In the frozen Kyiv transport incident, the SBI completed the investigation and the Darnytsia Specialized Defense Prosecutor sent the case into the judicial process.

Those accountability functions are material counter-evidence and are expressly excluded from the Schedule freeze. The existence of active investigation and prosecution also makes a generic TCC or mobilisation-system restriction indefensible.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions

Ordinary lawful recruitment/conscription administration is excluded. The finding follows specifically substantiated abusive units, personnel and projects. Russian conduct on Ukrainian territory is separately attributed to Russia and must not be imputed to Ukraine.

The frozen subset is the **May 2025 Kyiv TCC mobilized-person transport death incident — Podilskyi deputy-head / Shevchenkivskyi soldier case**. It covers only the charged conduct and functions materially participating in that incident. It does not restrict either district TCC, Kyiv TCC structures, Ukrainian mobilisation administration, unrelated transport personnel, investigators, prosecutors, courts, defence counsel or oversight/remedial bodies as classes.

Because the criminal case is pending, acquittal, material withdrawal/dismissal of charges or a final judgment materially changing attribution is an express Schedule review/removal trigger.

## 6. Adversarial determination

**NARROW S.** The exact SBI-defined incident preserves a defensible project-level restriction while active accountability mechanisms and the pending judicial process defeat broader TCC or mobilisation-system attribution.

## 7. Review / removal triggers

- acquittal, material charge withdrawal or dismissal in the frozen Kyiv transport case;
- final judgment materially changing the factual attribution or institutional roles;
- completion of investigations/prosecutions in other substantiated TCC cases;
- effective prevention of coercive TCC abuse;
- strengthened conscientious-objection safeguards; and
- remediation of substantiated collaboration-prosecution fair-trial failures.
