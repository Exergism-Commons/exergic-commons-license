# Cyprus

> **Governance record only.** No licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination
**S — Restricted with defined scope.**

## 2. ECL criteria engaged
Unlawful coercive return, public-order repression and obstruction of protected civic activity.

## 3. Evidence supporting determination
The current record documents unlawful summary maritime returns and continuing concerns around use of force/protest enforcement. These are active, attributable operations rather than hypothetical legal capacity.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions
The Supreme Constitutional Court restored KISA's registration and judicial/inquest mechanisms remain active, demonstrating meaningful remedies and defeating a broader State finding.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions
Scope is limited to maritime-return operations lacking individualized non-refoulement safeguards and specific public-order projects materially suppressing protected assembly. Courts, civil-society remediation and unrelated functions are excluded.

## 6. Adversarial determination
**ESCALATE U → S.**

## 7. Review / removal triggers
End summary returns, ensure individualized protection review and effective remedy, and demonstrate rights-compliant protest policing.
