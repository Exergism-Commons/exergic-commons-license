# United Kingdom

> **Governance record only.** This dossier does not itself create licensing restrictions.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope.** The finding concerns specifically evidenced counterterrorism protest-enforcement, covert police-surveillance and asylum/migration systems; it does not restrict the UK State as a whole.

## 2. ECL criteria engaged

Political repression, repressive surveillance, coercive migration/asylum enforcement and disproportionate use of counterterrorism powers against protected civic activity.

## 3. Evidence supporting the determination

The 2026 record includes mass-scale terrorism-law enforcement against peaceful Palestine Action-related protest, covert police-surveillance concerns affecting journalists/lawyers and increasingly restrictive asylum/migration measures. Historical UK bulk-interception and detainee-mistreatment/rendition records remain separately relevant to project-level ECL review.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

Judicial contestability is strong. The Palestine Action proscription underwent full judicial review: a Divisional Court initially quashed the decision, but the Court of Appeal on **15 June 2026** reversed that result and held the proscription lawful. Other Administrative Court cases continued granting interim relief against Home Office decisions.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions

The scope is project/system-specific. Courts, Parliament, independent regulators, public services and unrelated government activity are not restricted merely because they belong to the UK State.

## 6. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD S.** Strong counter-institutions weigh against whole-State restriction but do not require removal of the specified coercive systems from ECL review.

## 7. Review / removal triggers

Termination or material judicial/legislative remediation of the specified systems, enforceable surveillance safeguards, restored proportionality in protest enforcement, and effective asylum/migration remedies.
