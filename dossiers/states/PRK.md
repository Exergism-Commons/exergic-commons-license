> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**R — State apparatus Restricted.**

## 2. Formal Exergism assessment

Pilot assessment: [`../../exergism/assessments/PRK.json`](../../exergism/assessments/PRK.json).

The defined surveillance, political-control, detention, information and movement-control apparatus is sufficiently attributed to be `scorable`. Under the non-normative reference profile, both immediate ethics and structural balance remain strongly negative across the current uncertainty envelope. ## 3. Evidence supporting the determination

The UN Special Rapporteur reported in March 2026 that the overall human-rights situation over the previous decade had not improved and in many respects deteriorated. Surveillance expanded, including facial-recognition CCTV; newer laws added death-penalty offences; movement remains severely restricted; and State economic/control measures further limit individual agency. International organizations remain unable to return at scale.

## 4. Counter-evidence

The government claims improved living standards, and the Special Rapporteur noted isolated improvements in some areas. These do not rebut the comprehensive centralized coercive structure.

## 5. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD R with high confidence.**

## 6. Review triggers

Independent access/monitoring, closure/remediation of political detention systems, substantial movement/information liberalization, abolition of coercive death-penalty/control laws and durable independent remedies. A genuine transition should materially increase `A`, `V_ep`, `L` and `O` while reducing `C`, `S` and `R` in the formal assessment.
