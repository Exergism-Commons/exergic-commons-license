# Nicaragua

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**R — State apparatus Restricted.**

## 2. Evidence supporting the determination

A March 2026 UN Special Procedures communication describes a broad persistent pattern including arbitrary detention, enforced disappearance, ill-treatment/torture, arbitrary deprivation of nationality, forced exile, censorship, closure/confiscation of assets and repression of peaceful religious expression/assembly. Current UN material also treats return as risky for political dissidents/defenders.

## 3. Counter-evidence

Nicaragua remains subject to UN treaty/Special Procedures review and sometimes engages with international mechanisms; formal constitutional/judicial institutions remain. The record does not show domestic reform or accountability capable of restraining the systems underlying the designation.

## 4. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD R.**

## 5. Review triggers

Restoration of nationality/property rights, release/remedy for political detainees/disappeared persons, independent courts/security accountability and reopening of civic/religious/media space.
