# Cuba

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**R — State apparatus Restricted.**

## 2. Evidence supporting the determination

A January 2026 UN Special Procedures communication describes an alleged systematic pattern of criminalizing dissent and using arbitrary detention and forced exile against defenders, activists and artists. The conduct is attributed to political-surveillance, prosecution, detention and information-control structures.

## 3. Counter-evidence

Cuba announced release of 51 prisoners in March 2026 and a pardon covering 2,010 convicted persons in April 2026. These are material acts of clemency but do not establish that the political criminalization/surveillance system has ceased or that independent review now constrains it.

## 4. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD R**, while crediting clemency as partial remediation.

## 5. Review triggers

Durable legal reform, elimination of political criminalization, independent remedies, release/remedy for peaceful dissent cases and restored freedom to organize/exit.
