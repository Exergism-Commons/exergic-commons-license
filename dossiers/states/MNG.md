# Mongolia

> **Governance record only.** No licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination
**U — Under Review.**

## 2. ECL criteria engaged
Suppression of protected journalism and coercive displacement/resource governance.

## 3. Evidence supporting continued review
Journalist pressure, redevelopment/mining consultation failures and allegations of coercive enforcement remain material.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions
The Constitutional Court struck a speech restriction and peaceful protests produced political change. These are strong contrary outcomes, while the cutoff evidence does not isolate a sufficiently stable coercive project.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions
No State/project restriction is current. Any future finding must identify a deployed prosecutorial, redevelopment or extractive-enforcement project with material coercion.

## 6. Adversarial determination
**RETAIN U.**

## 7. Review / removal triggers
Escalate on renewed systematic protected-journalism prosecution or coercive displacement without remedy; move to `N` on durable constitutional compliance and consent safeguards.
