# Iran

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**R — State apparatus Restricted.**

## 2. Evidence supporting the determination

2026 UN Special Procedures communications describe widespread arbitrary arrest/detention around nationwide protests, enforced disappearance, torture/cruel treatment, death-penalty cases involving alleged torture-tainted confessions and deficient fair-trial guarantees, systematic denial of legal representation and detention/disappearance of lawyers and defenders. The alleged conduct cuts across intelligence/security, police, prosecution, judiciary and prison functions.

## 3. Counter-evidence

Iran responds to some UN communications and maintains formal judicial/legal institutions. The review did not identify a counter-institution with demonstrated capacity to independently restrain the relevant coercive system.

## 4. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD R.** The mechanisms of legal accountability are themselves materially implicated in the record.

## 5. Review triggers

Release/remedy for arbitrarily detained persons, independent investigation of torture/disappearance, meaningful defence rights, fair-trial reform, and demonstrable independent judicial control of security/intelligence organs.
