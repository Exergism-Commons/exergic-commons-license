# Sweden

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**N — No current State-level or defined-project basis.** Sweden has significant migration, protest, Sámi-rights and equality controversies, but the reviewed record does not establish a currently deployed ECL §5 prohibited project.

## 2. ECL criteria tested

Particular attention was given to §5.3 automated/data-driven discriminatory domination, alongside repressive surveillance, unlawful targeting, deceptive manipulation and coercive technological capture.

## 3. Evidence requiring scrutiny

European social-rights findings document serious discriminatory exclusion affecting vulnerable EU migrants. However, §5.3 requires an automated/data-driven system with discriminatory denial as its primary purpose. A previously documented discriminatory automated benefits-risk model was discontinued, materially weakening a current ECL project finding.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

Courts, ombuds/equality institutions, EU/European rights review and the discontinuation of the problematic automated model demonstrate material contestability/remediation. Courts have also produced rights-protective outcomes in protest cases.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions

No Swedish institution, population or nationality is designated through this dossier. Social-policy discrimination without the required ECL project nexus is not silently converted into a Restricted Project.

## 6. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD `N`.** The current record does not establish the automated/data-driven or other §5 nexus needed for restriction.

## 7. Review triggers

Reopen on renewed discriminatory automated benefits/risk scoring, systematic political surveillance, software-enabled unlawful targeting or coercive public-sector information systems materially denying contestability.
