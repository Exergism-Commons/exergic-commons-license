# Greece

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself. Only a versioned Schedule expressly incorporated by a software release can have operative effect.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope.** Greek police and the justice system are not restricted generally. One exact protest-policing incident/accountability project is now frozen for Schedule rendering; all residual scope remains governance-only.

## 2. Evidence supporting the determination

Amnesty International's June 2026 protest-policing review documents recurring unnecessary/excessive force against peaceful protesters and journalists, arbitrary liberty restrictions, dangerous less-lethal practices and persistent accountability failures.

The review provides a sufficiently precise incident for a narrow freeze. On 26 January 2025, photojournalist **Marios Lolos** was struck in the head by a stun grenade while covering an Athens demonstration concerning the Tempi rail tragedy, suffering permanent hearing loss and a head injury. Amnesty reports that verified video corroborates his account and suggests deliberate targeting. As of the June 2026 publication, Lolos had lodged a complaint with EMIDIPA, a criminal investigation had been initiated and police had also opened a disciplinary investigation; the incident therefore remained in an active accountability posture rather than being treated as durably remediated.

## 3. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

Criminal/disciplinary investigations, EMIDIPA oversight and judicial remedies exist; a police officer was convicted in 2025 for the fatal shooting of 16-year-old Roma boy Kostas Frangoulis. In the Lolos incident itself, the existence of active EMIDIPA, criminal and disciplinary processes is important counter-evidence and those remedial functions are expressly excluded from the frozen project.

## 4. Attribution and exclusions

Scope follows identified public-order/protest-policing units and operations materially involving unlawful force, arbitrary protest detention, dangerous less-lethal deployment or projects materially obstructing accountability. Courts and independent oversight are excluded.

The frozen subset is narrower still: the **26 January 2025 Athens Tempi-demonstration stun-grenade / Marios Lolos incident and accountability project**, limited to functions materially participating in the unlawful deployment/targeting or materially obstructing identification/accountability for that same incident. Participation in the demonstration-policing operation, riot policing or the Hellenic Police is not enough by itself.

## 5. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD / NARROW S.** The June 2026 evidence preserves a project-level restriction while active oversight and investigative mechanisms defeat any generic police or justice-sector attribution.

## 6. Review triggers

Effective EMIDIPA capacity; completion of the Lolos criminal/disciplinary investigations with credible identification, remedy and accountability; proportionality reform; a ban or rights-compliant restriction on stun grenades in assemblies; and sustained reduction in abusive protest policing.
