# Greece

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope.** Greek police and the justice system are not restricted generally.

## 2. Evidence supporting the determination

Amnesty's June 2026 protest-policing review documents recurring unnecessary/excessive force against peaceful protesters/journalists, arbitrary liberty restrictions, dangerous less-lethal practices and persistent accountability failures.

## 3. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

Criminal/disciplinary investigations, EMIDIPA oversight and judicial remedies exist; a police officer was convicted in 2025 for the fatal shooting of 16-year-old Roma boy Kostas Frangoulis.

## 4. Attribution and exclusions

Scope follows identified public-order/protest-policing units and operations materially involving unlawful force, arbitrary protest detention, dangerous less-lethal deployment or projects materially obstructing accountability. Courts and independent oversight are excluded.

## 5. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD / NARROW S.**

## 6. Review triggers

Effective EMIDIPA capacity, independent prosecutions/remedies, proportionality reform and sustained reduction in abusive protest policing.
