**S — Restricted with defined scope.** Gang violence and general State collapse are not imputed to the Haitian State.

## 2. Evidence supporting the determination

2026 investigations document apparently unlawful lethal drone strikes and probable extrajudicial executions involving a specialized State-linked Task Force and private contractor support, with civilian and child casualties.

## 3. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

Haiti created two specialized judicial units in 2026 to address serious human-rights abuses/financial crimes, and police internal-affairs mechanisms exist. These accountability functions are excluded.

## 4. Attribution and exclusions

Scope follows the Prime-Minister-created Security Task Force, materially participating PNH/security units and private contractors/Covered Associates in unlawful drone or summary-execution projects.
