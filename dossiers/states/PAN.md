# Panama

> **Governance record only.** This dossier does not itself create licensing restrictions. Only a versioned Schedule expressly incorporated by a software release has operative effect.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope.** The active finding is now concentrated on specifically evidenced Indigenous protest-policing and territorial-enforcement projects.

## 2. ECL criteria engaged

- coercive targeting/unlawful force;
- arbitrary detention and torture where attributable;
- criminalisation of peaceful protest/leadership;
- coercive territorial dispossession;
- denial of meaningful Indigenous consultation/FPIC.

## 3. Evidence supporting the determination

UN Special Procedures have recorded allegations of serious and systematic violations against Indigenous peoples in connection with protests from April 2025 onward, including excessive force, arbitrary detention, torture, sexual violence, criminalisation of Indigenous leaders and territorial-dispossession/FPIC failures. Current 2026 Indigenous-rights reporting also describes police incursions, arbitrary detention and coercive enforcement in Indigenous communities.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

In March 2026 the Government introduced legislation expressly framed as protecting peaceful protesters. The Defensoría del Pueblo actively monitored national demonstrations in May and supervised migration reception facilities in Darién in July. Panama/OAS protection programmes also strengthened humanitarian assistance for people in mobility.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions

Scope follows **identified police/security operations materially involving unlawful force, arbitrary detention, torture or criminalisation of peaceful Indigenous protest, and territorial/development enforcement materially defeating Indigenous consultation/FPIC or using coercive dispossession**. The previous generic migration-detention component is removed absent new project-specific evidence. Defensoría, courts, humanitarian/protection programmes and unrelated police functions are excluded.

## 6. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD / NARROW S.** Indigenous protest/territorial coercion remains sufficiently current, but 2026 counter-evidence defeats retaining the former broad migration component by inertia.

## 7. Review / removal triggers

Independent investigation/remedy for protest abuses; sustained rights-compliant crowd policing; effective Indigenous consultation/FPIC and land-remedy mechanisms; and non-recurrence of coercive territorial enforcement.
