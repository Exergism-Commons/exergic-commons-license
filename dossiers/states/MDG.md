# Madagascar

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**R — current military/transitional governing and security apparatus only.** The finding is not a permanent classification of all Malagasy institutions across future constitutional orders.

## 2. Evidence supporting the determination

The current military authorities took power after the October 2025 coup and dissolved most State institutions except the National Assembly. By March–April 2026 Amnesty documented a deepening crackdown on Gen Z activists and civil-society members using vague national-security/destabilization offences, including arbitrary detention and alleged enforced disappearance. Security-force killings from the 2025 protests remain without effective accountability.

## 3. Counter-evidence

Before the takeover Madagascar had functioning constitutional institutions, including a High Constitutional Court capable of rejecting defective legislation. The new authorities promised a maximum two-year transition and initially announced releases of some people imprisoned for opposing the prior government.

## 4. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD R**, but only for the present military/transitional apparatus.

## 5. Review triggers

Genuine civilian constitutional transition, restoration of independent institutions, release/remedy for political detainees and accountability for protest killings.
