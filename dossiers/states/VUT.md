# Vanuatu

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**N — No current State-level or defined-project basis.** Climate-disaster, gender-violence, justice and institutional-capacity concerns remain serious, but current evidence does not establish a materially deployed ECL §5 prohibited State project.

## 2. ECL criteria tested

Repressive surveillance, unlawful software-enabled targeting, automated discriminatory domination, deceptive population manipulation and coercive technological capture were tested.

## 3. Evidence requiring scrutiny

Climate relocation, emergency management and future digital/security systems remain project-level reviewable where coercive targeting, surveillance or non-contestable data systems could emerge.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

Vanuatu continues active international human-rights and climate-justice engagement. Current evidence does not show a State project primarily designed for repression, unlawful targeting or technological capture.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions

Climate harms and external conduct are not silently attributed to Vanuatu. No Vanuatu institution, population or nationality is designated through this dossier.

## 6. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD `N`.** Severe vulnerability/institutional weakness does not establish a current prohibited project.

## 7. Review triggers

Reopen on specific deployment of repressive surveillance, software-enabled unlawful targeting, automated discriminatory systems or coercive information-control infrastructure.
