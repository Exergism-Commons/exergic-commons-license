# Mozambique

> **Governance record only.** This dossier does not itself create licensing restrictions. Only a versioned Schedule expressly incorporated by a software release has operative effect.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope.** The finding concerns specifically attributable police/public-order/security repression projects and does not impute non-State conflict violence to Mozambique.

## 2. ECL criteria engaged

- unlawful lethal force and coercive targeting;
- arbitrary detention/disappearance;
- political repression of opposition, journalism or protected civic activity;
- coercive information suppression.

## 3. Evidence supporting the determination

The 2024/25 post-election crackdown generated extensive evidence of unlawful lethal force, arbitrary arrest, disappearances and information suppression attributable to police/security structures. Current persistence is independently supported by a July 2026 case documented by Amnesty International: video evidence showed a plain-clothes police officer summarily executing Narciso Castigo Sambo after he had been subdued. Police acknowledged the incident and SERNIC opened an investigation.

The broader 2025/26 record also includes detention/intimidation of journalists and opposition-linked actors, preserving a precise current public-order/security basis for `S`.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

SERNIC/prosecutorial investigations, changes in police leadership and other accountability measures are material counter-evidence. They are excluded from the restriction unless independently shown to obstruct accountability or participate in coercive conduct.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions

Scope follows **identified police, public-order and security units/operations materially involved in unlawful lethal force or summary execution, arbitrary detention/disappearance, opposition/journalist repression, intimidation or coercive information suppression**. Abuses by insurgent or other non-State actors, including in Cabo Delgado, are not imputed to the State absent specific attribution. Independent investigation/remedial functions and unrelated public services are excluded.

## 6. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD / DEFINE S.** The July 2026 summary-execution evidence defeats any argument that the coercive basis is merely a closed post-election episode, while active investigation and differentiated attribution make broader State restriction inappropriate.

## 7. Review / removal triggers

Review on credible prosecution/remedy for unlawful killings and disappearances, transparent use-of-force reform, traceable detention safeguards, durable protection of journalists/opposition activity and independent investigation of security-force abuse.
