# Example State

> **Governance record only.** This dossier does not itself create licensing restrictions. Only a versioned Schedule expressly incorporated by a software release has operative effect.

## 1. Current determination

State the current ECL outcome (`R`, `S`, `U`, or `N`), exact institutional/project scope, and confidence.

## 2. ECL criteria engaged

Identify the relevant ECL criteria: repressive surveillance, political repression, coercive targeting/unlawful violence, systematic discriminatory domination, deceptive manipulation, irreversible coercive capture, or circumvention.

## 3. Evidence supporting the determination

Record the material facts and attribution. Prefer primary/authoritative sources and distinguish findings, allegations, and inference.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

Record courts, ombuds institutions, auditors, prosecutors, reforms, releases, remediation, or other evidence tending to narrow or defeat restriction.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions

Explain exactly which institutions/projects are covered and which are excluded. Never extend a State finding to its population or nationality.

## 6. Adversarial determination

Record whether review UPHELD, NARROWED, DOWNGRADED, or REMOVED the prior finding and why.

## 7. Review / removal triggers

List objective developments that should force renewed review or justify narrowing/removal.

## 8. Sources

Use stable source URLs where possible.

## 9. Procedural history

Link the original issue, initial adjudication, adversarial-review tranche, and any later challenge/removal proceedings.
