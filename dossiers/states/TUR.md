# Türkiye

> **Governance record only.** This dossier does not itself create licensing restrictions. Only a versioned Schedule expressly incorporated by a software release has operative effect.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope.** The prior `R → S` correction remains valid: current coercive projects are serious and persistent, but functioning constitutional/external judicial review makes whole-State attribution overbroad.

## 2. ECL criteria engaged

- political repression through counterterrorism/security prosecution and detention;
- coercive suppression of peaceful assembly and civil-society activity;
- unlawful force or ill-treatment linked to protected activity;
- political information blocking/censorship where specifically evidenced.

## 3. Evidence supporting the determination

UN Special Procedures issued multiple 2026 communications concerning counterterrorism charges, detention and alleged ill-treatment of defenders/lawyers, dissolution and prosecution involving an LGBTI association, judicial harassment and arrest/prosecution of environmental defenders. The pattern shows current use of coercive criminal/public-order systems against protected activity rather than a merely historical concern.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

Türkiye's Constitutional Court continues individual-rights review. The European Court of Human Rights remains an operative external constraint; in May 2026 the Grand Chamber found violations in `Yasak v. Türkiye`, including under the principle of legality and the prohibition of inhuman/degrading treatment.

These institutions are directly relevant counter-evidence and remain outside the restricted scope.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions

Scope follows **counterterrorism/security/prosecutorial projects materially criminalising protected defender, lawyer, journalistic or peaceful political activity; police/public-order operations using unlawful force or arbitrary detention against peaceful assembly; coercive association-dissolution/civic-control projects; and specific information-blocking/censorship projects tied to political repression**.

Constitutional/remedial courts, independent legal defence, unrelated administration and the population/nationality are excluded.

## 6. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD S after prior R → S.** Current evidence does not justify either removal or re-expansion to the whole State.

## 7. Review / removal triggers

Implementation of Constitutional Court/ECtHR rulings, cessation/remedy of political/counterterrorism prosecutions targeting protected activity, effective police accountability and durable restoration of civic/media freedom.
