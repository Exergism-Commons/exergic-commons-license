# Türkiye

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope.**

## 2. Evidence preserving scoped restriction

2026 UN Special Procedures communications continued to allege judicial harassment, arbitrary dissolution of associations, counterterrorism criminalization of defenders/lawyers, police violence and prosecution linked to peaceful rights/environmental activity. ECtHR proceedings continue to expose unresolved detention concerns.

## 3. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

Türkiye's Constitutional Court continues to hear individual applications and issue rights-protective judgments, including 2026 findings involving effective judicial review and private life. External ECtHR scrutiny also remains operative; the Grand Chamber found violations in Yasak v. Türkiye in May 2026.

## 4. Attribution and exclusions

The scoped finding follows coercive police/prosecution/counterterrorism/media/civic-space/detention projects. Constitutional-court and independent remediation functions are excluded.

## 5. Adversarial determination

**DOWNGRADE R → S.** Functioning constitutional review makes whole-State attribution overbroad.

## 6. Review triggers

Implementation of Constitutional Court/ECtHR rulings, end to politically motivated detention/prosecution, effective police accountability and restoration of civic/media freedom.
