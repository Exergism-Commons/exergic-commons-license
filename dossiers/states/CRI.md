# Costa Rica

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**U — Under Review.** Costa Rica's prior `N` is no longer fully comfortable because the 2026 political record contains active pressure on judicial independence. The evidence still does not establish a deployed ECL §5 prohibited project.

## 2. ECL criteria tested

Review tested repressive surveillance, coercive targeting, deceptive manipulation and technological/informational systems that could materially disable political or judicial contestability.

## 3. Evidence supporting review

In August 2026 public demonstrations defended judicial independence amid allegations of executive/legislative pressure, budgetary attacks and proposed constitutional changes affecting the judiciary. These developments matter because capture of counter-institutions can transform otherwise scoped projects into broader coercive systems.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

The same events demonstrate active opposition, public protest, functioning courts and plural political contestation. No reviewed evidence establishes systematic surveillance, unlawful software-enabled targeting or coercive information infrastructure used to implement the institutional pressure.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions

Current review follows specific institutional-pressure measures and any coercive implementation, not Costa Rica, its population, judiciary or administration generally.

## 6. Adversarial determination

**ESCALATE `N → U`.** Current institutional-risk evidence is too material for confident `N`, but ECL's prohibited-project threshold is not met for `S`.

## 7. Review triggers

Escalate on capture/neutralization of judicial review combined with qualifying surveillance, targeting or coercive information-control deployment. Return to `N` if institutional independence remains effective and no prohibited project emerges.
