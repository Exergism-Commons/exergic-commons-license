# Finland

> **Governance record only.** No licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination
**U — Under Review.**

## 2. ECL criteria engaged
Potential unlawful return, coercive border exclusion and Indigenous consent failures.

## 3. Evidence supporting continued review
The Border Security Act remains in force through 2026 and permits exceptional restriction of asylum applications; the eastern land border remains closed. Sámi FPIC/resource concerns also persist.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions
The Finnish Government states that the exceptional Act had not been applied at the cutoff and would require a separate activation decision. Courts, Parliament and strong independent institutions provide substantial safeguards.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions
No border authority is restricted merely because the capacity exists. Review activates around actual use of the exceptional mechanism, unlawful returns or specifically evidenced coercive Sámi-resource projects.

## 6. Adversarial determination
**RETAIN U.** Capability without deployment is insufficient for `S`.

## 7. Review / removal triggers
Escalate if the exception is activated without individualized non-refoulement safeguards; move to `N` on expiry/repeal plus durable protection access and FPIC compliance.
