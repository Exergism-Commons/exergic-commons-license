# Armenia

> **Governance record only.** No licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination
**S — Restricted with defined scope.**

## 2. ECL criteria engaged
Repressive surveillance, coercive targeting and abuse of public-order powers.

## 3. Evidence supporting determination
Current law permits police access to live camera feeds and facial-recognition/biometric identification. The 2025/26 record also preserves unresolved excessive-force and accountability concerns. The surveillance capability is operationally attached to policing rather than merely hypothetical.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions
The Human Rights Defender remains an A-status national institution with active complaint/remedy work and reported positive outcomes in 2026. Courts and reform processes also constrain police action.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions
Only police biometric-surveillance deployments and specifically evidenced abusive public-order operations are in scope. The Human Rights Defender, courts, independent oversight and unrelated government services are excluded.

## 6. Adversarial determination
**ESCALATE U → S.** A current attributable surveillance project is now sufficiently concrete; whole-State attribution remains unwarranted.

## 7. Review / removal triggers
Binding necessity/proportionality limits, independent authorization/audit of facial recognition, deletion/minimization safeguards and effective remedies for police abuse.
