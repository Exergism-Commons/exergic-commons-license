# Burundi

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**R — State apparatus Restricted.**

## 2. Evidence supporting the determination

UN Committee against Torture decisions published in January 2026 concern torture/ill-treatment, ineffective investigation/redress and, in one case, reprisals for cooperation with the Committee. Several proceedings record failure by the State party to cooperate. This directly tests whether domestic remedy structures are effective.

## 3. Counter-evidence

Burundi's Ombudsman reported 2025 activity and institutional reforms aimed at rights protection, rule of law and good governance. The institution is meaningful counter-evidence, but current records do not yet show remediation strong enough to rebut the broader coercive pattern.

## 4. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD R**, with a specific future test of Ombudsman effectiveness.

## 5. Review triggers

Binding Ombudsman or court remedies in political/torture cases, investigations/prosecutions, releases and demonstrable non-recurrence.
