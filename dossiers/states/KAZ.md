# Kazakhstan

> **Governance record only.** This dossier does not itself create licensing restrictions. Only a versioned Schedule expressly incorporated by a software release has operative effect.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope.** The prior whole-State `R` was correctly downgraded to `S`; current review preserves the narrower finding.

## 2. ECL criteria engaged

- political repression through detention/prosecution of protected expression, assembly or association;
- coercive media suppression and retaliation against legal/professional representation;
- discriminatory religious enforcement;
- restrictions on meaningful contestability where coercive institutions are used against dissent.

## 3. Evidence supporting the determination

UN Special Procedures communication `KAZ 2/2026` concerns the arrest, detention and criminal prosecution of 20 Atajurt movement activists and raises freedom-of-assembly, association and expression concerns. `KAZ 3/2026` concerns continuing judicial harassment of a religious advocate and denial of registration to an independent religious community. A separate 2026 communication concerns detention/prosecution around the independent outlet Orda and retaliatory professional consequences for legal representation.

The evidence supports a high-confidence scoped finding against identified political-security, prosecutorial, media and discriminatory-enforcement projects.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

Kazakhstan's new Constitution entered into force on 1 July 2026 with explicit detention, defence, compensation and digital/privacy guarantees. The Constitutional Court has produced operative rights-protective outcomes, including findings against rules permitting excessive detention. Kazakhstan also replied to the 2026 UN Special Procedures communications, creating an assessable counter-record.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions

Covered scope follows political-security/prosecutorial projects materially criminalizing protected peaceful assembly or expression, politically motivated detention, media repression, retaliatory professional sanctions and discriminatory religious enforcement.

The Constitutional Court, rights-protective judicial functions, unrelated State services and the Kazakh population/nationality are excluded absent independent evidence.

## 6. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD / DEFINE S AFTER PRIOR `R → S`.** Current coercive projects remain substantiated, but functioning constitutional review and other counter-institutions make renewed whole-State attribution inaccurate.

## 7. Review / removal triggers

- release/remedy in peaceful-expression and assembly cases;
- durable protection of independent media, lawyers and defenders;
- rights-compliant religious registration and enforcement;
- sustained implementation of Constitutional Court safeguards and effective remedies for unlawful detention/prosecution.
