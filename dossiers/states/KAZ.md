# Kazakhstan

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope.**

## 2. Evidence preserving scoped restriction

UN Special Procedures in March 2026 raised detention/prosecution of the former head of independent outlet Orda and disbarment of her lawyer, allegedly linked to journalism/legal representation, and a separate matter concerning judicial harassment of a religious advocate and denial of registration to an independent religious community.

## 3. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

Kazakhstan's new Constitution entered into force on 1 July 2026 with explicit detention, defence, compensation and digital/privacy guarantees. The Constitutional Court has produced operative rights-protective outcomes, including finding part of the Criminal Procedure Code unconstitutional because it allowed excessive detention and invalidating/narrowing other rules.

## 4. Attribution and exclusions

The restriction follows specified political-security, media, prosecutorial and discriminatory-enforcement systems. The Constitutional Court and unrelated State functions are excluded absent independent evidence.

## 5. Adversarial determination

**DOWNGRADE R → S.** Whole-State attribution no longer accurately reflects functioning constitutional review.

## 6. Review triggers

Cessation/remedy of political detention/media retaliation, implementation of Constitutional Court protections and independent accountability for abusive enforcement.
