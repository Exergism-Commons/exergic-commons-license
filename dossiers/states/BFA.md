# Burkina Faso

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**R — current military governing apparatus.**

## 2. Evidence supporting the determination

The military governing structure continues to centralize political control. A decree effective February 2026 required NGOs/associations to domicile liquid accounts with a public Treasury institution, prompting UN concerns over association, expression, assembly and humanitarian access. The transition and political repression have been extended rather than replaced by independent civilian accountability.

## 3. Counter-evidence

A formal Justice Ministry continues to state rule-of-law, human-rights and judicial-independence functions. The review found insufficient evidence that these institutions can independently restrain the military governing apparatus.

## 4. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD R.**

## 5. Review triggers

Genuine civilian transition, restoration of political competition/civic space, independent remedies and repeal/remediation of coercive NGO/media controls.
