# Cambodia

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**R — State apparatus Restricted.**

## 2. Evidence supporting the determination

Current reporting describes Cambodia as a one-party State backed by the military with tight judicial control, while journalists, civil-society leaders and critics face politically motivated criminal charges. Amnesty's 2025/26 investigations also document slavery, forced labour, trafficking and torture in cyber-scam compounds flourishing with official knowledge, with serious shortcomings in the claimed crackdown and victim protection.

## 3. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

OHCHR Cambodia works directly with the judiciary, Supreme Council of Magistracy and Constitutional Council on legal/judicial reform, legal aid, prevention of torture and excessive detention; prison reform includes independent monitoring and dialogue. These are genuine reform channels but do not presently constitute a domestic counter-institution independently restraining the political coercive system.

## 4. Attribution and exclusions

OHCHR-supported reform, victim-rescue and genuinely independent accountability projects should fall within the proposed Accountability and Remediation Exception.

## 5. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD R**, with an explicit reform/accountability carve-out.

## 6. Review triggers

Independent judicial restraint, effective political competition, accountability for scam-compound complicity, victim remediation, and durable civic/media freedom.
