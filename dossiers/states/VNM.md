# Viet Nam

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**R — State apparatus Restricted.**

## 2. Evidence supporting the determination

The government continues using vague Penal Code provisions against dissent/expression, with arbitrary arrest/detention and harsh prison treatment of activists. Current reporting describes party-controlled courts imposing long sentences on peaceful critics. July 2026 evidence documents transnational repression extending into Thailand through bilateral security cooperation targeting dissidents.

## 3. Counter-evidence

Viet Nam abolished the death penalty for eight offences and participates in UN Human Rights Committee review. The death-penalty narrowing is concrete exergic progress but does not establish an independent domestic counter-institution capable of restraining political-security, prosecution, prison and transnational-repression systems.

## 4. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD R**, while crediting the death-penalty reform.

## 5. Review triggers

Release/remedy for peaceful critics, repeal/narrowing of vague security offences, prison accountability, cessation of transnational repression and further irreversible-coercion reduction.
