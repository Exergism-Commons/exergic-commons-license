# Australia

> **Governance record only.** No licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination
**U — Under Review.**

## 2. ECL criteria engaged
Coercive detention, discriminatory domination and public-order enforcement.

## 3. Evidence supporting continued review
Youth-detention conditions, particularly in some states/territories, remain severe; recent oversight has described overcrowded and oppressive facilities. Indigenous over-representation and asylum/protest restrictions remain material concerns.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions
Federal/state fragmentation, courts, ombuds institutions, inspectorates and active legislative reform make a stable nationwide or single-project attribution premature. New youth-justice safeguards have also been proposed or enacted in affected jurisdictions.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions
No Australian government or youth-justice system is Restricted generically. Review follows named state/territory facilities or projects only when current coercive deployment and responsibility are proven.

## 6. Adversarial determination
**RETAIN U.** Severity is clear; attribution/persistence at a sufficiently precise ECL project level remains mixed.

## 7. Review / removal triggers
Escalate on verified systematic unlawful isolation/force or deliberate rights-stripping in a defined detention project; move to `N` on durable compliant reform and independent access/remedy.
