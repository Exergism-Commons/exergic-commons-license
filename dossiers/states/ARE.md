# United Arab Emirates

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**R — federal governing apparatus Restricted.**

## 2. Evidence supporting the determination

In a major political mass trial, the Federal Appeal Court upheld lengthy terrorism-related convictions of 53 defendants, including rights defenders, despite serious fair-trial concerns. Political opponents/activists continue to face enforced disappearance and arbitrary detention. The reviewed evidence directly implicates federal security, prosecution, detention and appellate judicial processes.

## 3. Counter-evidence

The UAE maintains formal federal courts and extensive administrative/legal modernization. Formal appeal is credited where meaningful, but the principal appellate structure in the core political case operated as part of the contested coercive architecture rather than as an effective counterweight.

## 4. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD R.**

## 5. Review triggers

Release/remedy for political detainees, independent review of mass-trial convictions, accountability for disappearance/detention abuse and demonstrable judicial independence in politically sensitive cases.
