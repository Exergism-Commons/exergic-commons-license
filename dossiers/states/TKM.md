# Turkmenistan

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**R — State apparatus Restricted.**

## 2. Evidence supporting the determination

Current records describe enforced disappearances, imprisonment continuing beyond sentence expiry, punishment of independent voices, travel bans, pervasive censorship and widespread torture/ill-treatment. There is no independent national or international monitoring of penitentiary institutions in the reviewed record.

## 3. Counter-evidence

Turkmenistan participates formally in UN treaty-body processes and submitted 2026 follow-up information. It also formally maintains a national human-rights structure. Reporting compliance does not itself establish an effective independent domestic counter-institution.

## 4. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD R.** Information, security, detention, border/travel and judicial systems remain tightly centralized and mutually reinforcing.

## 5. Review triggers

Independent prison monitoring, disclosure/remedy for disappeared prisoners, meaningful rights-institution independence, torture accountability and removal of arbitrary censorship/travel controls.
