# Peru

> **Governance record only.** This dossier does not itself create licensing restrictions. Only a versioned Schedule expressly incorporated by a software release has operative effect.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope.** The current record supports public-order, civic-control and impunity-enabling project restrictions, not a generic restriction of Peru, its armed forces, police or judiciary.

## 2. ECL criteria engaged

- coercive targeting/unlawful force against peaceful demonstrators;
- suppression of protected journalism, defence and association;
- irreversible or coercive institutional capture where accountability is structurally disabled;
- material obstruction of remedy for serious human-rights violations.

## 3. Evidence supporting the determination

Amnesty International's April 2026 assessment reports unnecessary and disproportionate force against demonstrators, continuing lack of justice for victims of the 2022–23 protest killings, attacks on journalists and defenders and legislation affecting civil-society organisations. On 20 July 2026 Peru enacted Law 32735, expanding military/police jurisdiction over alleged offences by security personnel and creating a fresh risk that serious human-rights cases involving civilian victims may be transferred away from ordinary justice.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

Peru retains ordinary courts, prosecutors, electoral institutions, investigative journalists and civil-society organisations that continue to litigate and contest these measures. Domestic and Inter-American review mechanisms remain meaningful counterweights even as institutional independence is under pressure.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions

Scope follows **police/military public-order projects materially using unlawful/disproportionate force against peaceful demonstrators; civic-control projects materially suppressing protected NGOs, journalists or defenders; and legal/jurisdictional projects materially obstructing independent investigation, ordinary-court accountability or remedy for serious security-force violations**. The State, military/police as entire institutions, independent courts/prosecutors and unrelated public functions are excluded.

## 6. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD / DEFINE S.** Fresh July 2026 legislation preserves a current accountability/impunity concern while functioning counter-institutions make broader attribution inaccurate.

## 7. Review / removal triggers

Credible accountability for protest killings/abuse; sustained rights-compliant crowd policing; repeal, narrowing or rights-compliant judicial interpretation of jurisdictional laws that impede accountability; protection of civil society/journalism; and restored independent remedies.
