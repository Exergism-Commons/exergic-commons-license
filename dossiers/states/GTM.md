## 2. Evidence supporting the determination

Current evidence documents allegedly unfounded prosecutions/arbitrary detention of anti-corruption justice actors, defenders, journalists and Indigenous leaders. ## 4. Attribution and exclusions

Scope follows Public Ministry/FECI/prosecutorial, detention and implicated judicial projects materially used for arbitrary/unfounded criminalization. The Constitutional Court and genuinely independent judicial/remediation functions are excluded.
