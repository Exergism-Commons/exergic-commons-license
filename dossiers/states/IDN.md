# Indonesia

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope.**

## 2. Evidence supporting the determination

2026 evidence includes alleged arbitrary arrest/criminalization of peaceful protesters and military-connected coordinated disinformation/intimidation branding critics as foreign agents. Specific Papua/extractive security projects remain separately reviewable where abuse is substantiated.

## 3. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

Draft/expanded military powers are not Restricted merely on paper; material abusive implementation is required.

## 4. Attribution and exclusions

Scope follows specific protest-criminalization, coordinated deceptive/intimidatory operations, implemented abusive military/counterterrorism projects and substantiated Papua/extractive operations. Acquitting courts and remediation are excluded.
