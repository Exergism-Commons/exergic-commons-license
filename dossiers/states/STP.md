# São Tomé and Príncipe

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**N — No current State-level or defined-project basis.** Institutional-rights gaps remain, but current evidence does not establish a materially deployed ECL §5 prohibited project.

## 2. ECL criteria tested

Repressive surveillance, unlawful software-enabled targeting, automated discriminatory domination, deceptive population manipulation and coercive technological capture were tested.

## 3. Evidence requiring scrutiny

The State still lacks a fully developed Paris-Principles NHRI and has institutional implementation gaps. These weaknesses are not themselves prohibited projects.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

São Tomé and Príncipe underwent UPR review in 2026 and has pledged to establish an NHRI and a national implementation/reporting mechanism, providing concrete reform counter-evidence.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions
