# Dominican Republic

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope**, limited to specified migration/border enforcement projects.

## 2. Evidence supporting the determination

At least 265,215 Haitians were expelled in 2025 amid reported collective expulsion, racial profiling, arbitrary detention, excessive force and cruel/inhuman treatment. Official DGM figures show 99,268 deportations through March 2026 and 126,790 through April, overwhelmingly Haitian nationals.

## 3. Counter-evidence

DGM states operations follow Law 285-04 and strict human-rights/legal protocols and uses formal processing centres. Those safeguards are relevant but do not independently rebut documented practices.

## 4. Attribution and exclusions

Scope follows DGM, Rapid Reaction, CESFRONT, military/police and detention/border projects materially participating in ECL-prohibited migration enforcement. Unrelated Dominican State functions are excluded.

## 5. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD / DEFINE S.**

## 6. Review triggers

Individualized protection screening, asylum access, independent detention oversight, end to racial profiling/collective expulsion and accountability for abuse.
