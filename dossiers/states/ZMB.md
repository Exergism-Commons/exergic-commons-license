# Zambia

> **Governance record only.** This dossier does not itself create licensing restrictions. Only a versioned Schedule expressly incorporated by a software release has operative effect.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope.** Current 2026 cases show active use of cyber, State-security and detention powers against protected journalism/political expression, while courts and constitutional litigation prevent a whole-State finding.

## 2. ECL criteria engaged

- political repression through criminalisation/detention of protected expression;
- coercive surveillance or interception lacking meaningful safeguards where used for political targeting;
- suppression of peaceful opposition assembly/activity;
- arbitrary political detention and limits on meaningful judicial review.

## 3. Evidence supporting the determination

In July 2026 journalist MacPherson Mukuka was detained and charged under the 2025 Cyber Crimes Act over publication of a recording, raising concerns that the law was being used to intimidate public-interest journalism. Opposition figure Xavier Chungu was detained after a podcast interview on State-security and sedition charges and denied bail through a `No Bail Certificate`.

The broader current record also documents disruption of opposition meetings and broad surveillance/interception powers under the 2025 cyber legislation.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

Zambia retains competitive elections, courts and active constitutional litigation. The Law Association of Zambia challenged provisions of the Cyber Crimes Act. Government officials have publicly denied that the cyber framework authorises indiscriminate surveillance.

These institutions and legal challenges materially constrain attribution and remain outside the scope.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions

Scope covers **cyber-security/criminal-enforcement and State-security projects materially criminalising protected journalism or peaceful political expression; surveillance/interception deployments lacking meaningful legal safeguards and used for coercive political targeting; and police/public-order projects materially obstructing peaceful opposition assembly or imposing arbitrary political detention**.

Courts, legal challenges, election administration absent independent evidence, unrelated public services and the population/nationality are excluded.

## 6. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD / DEFINE S.** Fresh 2026 cases establish persistence strongly enough to reject downgrade to `U`, but the evidence remains project-specific rather than whole-State.

## 7. Review / removal triggers

Release/remedy in expression-only cases, rights-compliant narrowing/interpretation of cyber and State-security laws, warrant-based independent surveillance safeguards and sustained non-interference with peaceful opposition/journalism.
