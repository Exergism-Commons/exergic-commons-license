# Brunei

> **Governance record only.** No licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination
**N — No current state-level basis.**

## 2. ECL criteria engaged
Prior review concerned morality/religious enforcement and suppression of expression.

## 3. Evidence supporting review
Brunei retains laws capable of severe coercive enforcement. They remain a serious rights concern and justify explicit re-review triggers.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions
At the 2026 cutoff the review did not establish sufficiently current, material and systematic deployment of those provisions matching an ECL prohibited project; severe punishments also remain subject to long-standing non-use in important categories.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions
No current State/project restriction is supported. This does not endorse the legal framework or preclude project-specific future findings.

## 6. Adversarial determination
**DOWNGRADE U → N.** ECL requires current attributable conduct, not punishment based on dormant legal capacity.

## 7. Review / removal triggers
Immediate re-review upon coercive morality/religious enforcement against protected identity/expression, corporal punishment deployment, or systematic peaceful-expression criminalization.
