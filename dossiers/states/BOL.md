# Bolivia

> **Governance record only.** No licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination
**S — Restricted with defined scope.**

## 2. ECL criteria engaged
Unlawful coercive targeting, public-order repression and arbitrary detention.

## 3. Evidence supporting determination
During 2026 social conflict, emergency measures and military/police deployments to clear blockades produced deaths, injuries and arrests. Current regional human-rights monitoring raised concerns about protest rights and State response.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions
The Defensoría del Pueblo actively monitored conflict and called for rights protection. Courts, electoral institutions and civilian oversight remain distinct counter-institutions.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions
Only specific emergency, military and police operations materially using unlawful force/arbitrary detention against protected activity are in scope. Defensoría, courts and unrelated functions are excluded.

## 6. Adversarial determination
**ESCALATE U → S.**

## 7. Review / removal triggers
Independent investigation/remedy for deaths and detention, expiration/non-renewal of coercive emergency measures and rights-compliant public-order practice.
