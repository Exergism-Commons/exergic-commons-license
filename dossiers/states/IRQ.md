# Iraq

> **Governance record only.** This dossier does not itself create licensing restrictions. Only a versioned Schedule expressly incorporated by a software release has operative effect.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope.** The current finding is limited to attributable security/detention and State-connected projects materially involved in enforced disappearance, secret or irregular detention, reprisals or obstruction of search/investigation. Iraq, Iraqi people and all State/security functions are not Restricted generically.

## 2. ECL criteria engaged

- coercive targeting / unlawful detention;
- political repression and retaliation against victims, activists or families;
- destruction of meaningful contestability through disappearance or secret detention;
- material obstruction of investigation, search or remedy.

## 3. Evidence supporting the determination

The UN Committee on Enforced Disappearances reviewed Iraq again at its March 2026 session and published concluding observations `CED/C/IRQ/OAI/2` on 24 March 2026. The Committee's continued supervisory procedure and the evidentiary record before it identify major unresolved implementation problems around enforced disappearance, search and investigation. Civil-society material submitted for the same session documents continuing risks involving security/armed structures, activists, protesters and families of disappeared persons.

The evidence supports a current restriction only where conduct is attributable to identified State, PMF-linked or State-connected security/detention actors. It does not justify assigning all violence by fragmented armed actors to the Iraqi State.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

Iraq continues to engage with the Committee and has missing-person/search, judicial and administrative mechanisms capable in principle of registering cases, issuing warrants and conducting investigations. The State has also submitted implementation information to the treaty body. Those mechanisms are material counter-evidence even where implementation remains deficient.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions

Covered scope follows identified security, detention, PMF-linked or other State-connected units/projects materially participating in enforced disappearance, secret/irregular detention, reprisals, or material obstruction of search and investigation.

Excluded absent separate evidence: unrelated ministries and public services; courts or investigators providing genuine independent remedy; the Iraqi population/nationality; and conduct of non-State armed actors that cannot be attributed to a covered State-connected project.

## 6. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD / NARROW S.** The disappearance record remains strong enough for a scoped restriction, but fragmented armed authority and the existence of formal search/judicial mechanisms make a whole-security-sector or whole-State finding overbroad.

## 7. Review / removal triggers

- autonomous and effectively enforced criminalization of enforced disappearance;
- reliable search outcomes and disclosure for pending cases;
- enforceable custody-registration and anti-secret-detention safeguards;
- credible investigations/accountability across implicated State/PMF-linked structures;
- demonstrated protection of victims, families, lawyers and activists from reprisals.
