# Serbia

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope.** The finding is project/system specific and does not classify Serbia's State apparatus generally.

## 2. Evidence supporting the determination

Amnesty forensic work documented systematic use of NoviSpy and mobile-forensics tools against activists/journalists and later found two BIRN journalists targeted with Pegasus in February 2025, with strong likelihood of Serbian State-actor involvement. 2026 reporting continued to describe intimidation, harassment, unlawful surveillance and police repression through April, without identified independent State accountability or demonstrated cessation.

## 3. Counter-evidence

Cellebrite halted product use in Serbia after the surveillance reporting, showing that supplier intervention can materially reduce a capability. Serbian law formally regulates police/intelligence activity and independent journalism/civil society continues exposing abuse. Cellebrite itself is not a Serbian Restricted Party merely because its technology was misused.

## 4. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD S**, limited to BIA/police or State-linked unlawful digital-surveillance projects and specifically substantiated protest-repression operations.

## 5. Removal trigger

Independent investigation/accountability, verified cessation of unlawful spyware/mobile-forensic targeting and durable safeguards for journalists/activists/opposition.
