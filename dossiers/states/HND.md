# Honduras

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**U — Under Review / insufficient current evidence for a stable active restriction.**

## 2. Historical basis for prior `S`

The 2022–January 2026 nationwide state of exception generated hundreds of complaints alleging arbitrary detention, torture/ill-treatment, false evidence, property abuse and other coercive police/security conduct.

## 3. Material change / counter-evidence

The new government ended/suspended the nationwide state of exception on taking office in January 2026 and later described a national security policy without a nationwide emergency regime. The active project that supported the previous `S` therefore ceased.

## 4. Current review scope

Legacy accountability, defender protection and any newly created localized exceptional-security projects remain under review. Unresolved Berta Cáceres accountability/defender risks are serious but do not, on the current record, substitute for an active systematic nationwide project.

## 5. Adversarial determination

**DOWNGRADE S → U** under ECL's reversibility principle.

## 6. Review triggers

Re-escalate to `S` if new emergency/security projects reproduce arbitrary detention, torture or repression. Move to `N` if non-recurrence and credible legacy accountability/remediation are demonstrated.
