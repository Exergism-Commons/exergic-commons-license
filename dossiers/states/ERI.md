# Eritrea

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**R — State apparatus Restricted.**

## 2. Evidence supporting the determination

The current record continues to concern arbitrary detention, enforced disappearance and prolonged/indefinite national service without independent remedies. In July 2026 the Human Rights Council renewed the Special Rapporteur mandate and continued calling on Eritrea to cooperate fully with OHCHR and the mandate.

## 3. Counter-evidence

The 2026 HRC debate noted an OHCHR human-rights training in Asmara, release of some arbitrarily detained persons and increased EU dialogue. These are meaningful but insufficient to demonstrate systemic cessation/accountability.

## 4. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD R.**

## 5. Review triggers

Independent detention monitoring, end to indefinite national service/forced labour, release/remedy for disappeared/detainees, durable accountability and meaningful OHCHR access.
