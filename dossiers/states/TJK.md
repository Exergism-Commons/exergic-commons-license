# Tajikistan

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**R — State apparatus Restricted.**

## 2. Evidence supporting the determination

Current reporting describes deaths in detention, near-complete impunity for widespread torture/ill-treatment, long politically sensitive sentences after unfair trials, continued imprisonment of human-rights lawyer Buzurghmehr Yorov, prosecution of journalists and persecution of the Pamiri minority. The pattern spans security, detention, prosecution/judicial and political institutions.

## 3. Counter-evidence

Tajikistan decriminalized merely 'liking' critical social-media posts and continued participating in the UN Committee against Torture review process. These are genuine but narrow improvements and do not establish independent domestic restraint.

## 4. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD R.**

## 5. Review triggers

Independent torture accountability, release/remedy in political cases, protection of Pamiri rights, restored expression/media space and demonstrable judicial independence.
