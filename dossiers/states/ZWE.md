# Zimbabwe

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope.**

## 2. Evidence preserving scoped restriction

Police/security and executive authorities continue arbitrary arrest, surveillance, protest/meeting restrictions and harassment of journalists, opposition supporters and defenders. In March 2026 constitutional lawyer/opposition leader Lovemore Madhuku and activists were violently attacked in the presence of uniformed police while opposing presidential-term-extension changes. Civil-society regulation grants broad executive powers.

## 3. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

Zimbabwean courts repeatedly produced contrary outcomes in politically sensitive cases: opposition/human-rights defendants were acquitted or released, the High Court quashed convictions over an allegedly unsanctioned political gathering and the death penalty was abolished in law with re-sentencing underway.

## 4. Attribution and exclusions

The finding follows coercive executive/security/police projects. Independent courts and legal/remediation functions are excluded absent separate evidence.

## 5. Adversarial determination

**DOWNGRADE R → S.** Repeated judicial counterweight makes whole-State attribution inaccurate.

## 6. Review triggers

End to political detention/harassment, police accountability, narrowing of coercive civil-society controls and demonstrated respect for judicial outcomes and constitutional political competition.
