# Tunisia

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**R — State apparatus Restricted.**

## 2. Evidence supporting the determination

In 2026 authorities intensified court-ordered NGO suspensions/dissolution threats, asset freezes and prosecutions affecting rights, migration, anti-racism, elections, corruption and media groups. Politically motivated mass trials and arbitrary detention continue. Tunisia also withdrew its Article 34(6) African Court declaration, removing future direct individual/NGO access to that regional remedy. Current migration reporting describes racialized exclusion, discriminatory detention, collective expulsions and ill-treatment.

## 3. Counter-evidence

Lawyers, courts and independent civil society continue to challenge abuse. However, legal/judicial tools are themselves central to the current repression and regional remedy access has been weakened.

## 4. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD R.**

## 5. Review triggers

Restoration of NGO/civic space, release/remedy for political cases, independent judicial safeguards, reversal/remediation of discriminatory migration enforcement and restored regional/domestic remedy access.
