# Singapore

> **Governance record only.** This dossier does not itself create licensing restrictions. Only a versioned Schedule expressly incorporated by a software release has operative effect.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope.** Current information-control and execution projects remain active, but statutory/judicial review and unrelated State functions make whole-State `R` inaccurate.

## 2. ECL criteria engaged

Current review concerns:

- coercive information-control projects materially burdening protected political, journalistic or rights expression;
- public-order/assembly enforcement materially suppressing protected peaceful civic activity;
- capital-punishment/execution projects where software would materially facilitate irreversible coercive harm.

## 3. Evidence supporting `S`

In March 2026 Singapore brought the first criminal prosecution for communicating alleged false statements under POFMA against government critic Jay Ish'haq Rajoo. In May 2026 rights organisations called for charges against Kokila Annamalai over alleged non-compliance with a POFMA order to be dropped. Government ministries continued issuing POFMA Correction Directions during 2026.

Capital-punishment operations also remained current. Singapore executed Lingkesvaran Rajendaren for a drug offence on 11 February 2026, and additional drug-related executions remained scheduled during the year.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

POFMA provides a statutory route to appeal ministerial directions to the High Court. Singapore's Court of Appeal has established an objective framework under which a direction may be set aside, and the judiciary continues to hear constitutional and capital cases.

These judicial mechanisms do not erase the coercive project but materially defeat recursive attribution to the entire State apparatus.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions

The active scope covers **POFMA/information-control projects materially criminalising or coercively burdening protected political, journalistic or rights expression; public-order/assembly enforcement materially suppressing protected peaceful civic activity; and capital-punishment/execution projects, particularly drug-offence executions, where ECL-covered software would materially facilitate the coercive process**.

Courts, POFMA judicial appeals, independent legal defence and unrelated public administration are excluded.

## 6. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD `S` after prior `R → S`.** Current coercive projects remain specific and active; judicial review continues to make a whole-State designation overbroad.

## 7. Review / removal triggers

**Move toward `U/N`** on repeal/narrowing or demonstrably non-coercive implementation of POFMA against protected expression, durable rights-compliant assembly practice, moratorium/abolition or material remediation of the relevant execution system and preservation of effective judicial challenge.

**Re-escalation toward `R`** requires evidence that judicial/remedial channels themselves become materially captured or incapable of providing meaningful contrary outcomes across the implicated systems.
