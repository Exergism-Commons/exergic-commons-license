# Singapore

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope.**

## 2. Evidence preserving scoped restriction

POFMA gives ministers substantial information-control powers, while public-order legislation, restrictions on assembly/expression and capital-punishment operations remain material ECL concerns where software would directly facilitate coercive conduct.

## 3. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

Singapore courts exercise judicial review over POFMA directions and constitutional claims. Court of Appeal litigation involving The Online Citizen/SDP preserved a judicial route to set directions aside and analyzed constitutional limits on ministerial powers. This makes a whole-State designation inaccurate.

## 4. Attribution and exclusions

The finding is limited to identified information-control, public-order, execution and political-enforcement projects. Courts and unrelated State services are excluded absent separate evidence.

## 5. Adversarial determination

**DOWNGRADE R → S.**

## 6. Review triggers

Narrowing/repeal or non-coercive implementation of POFMA/public-order systems, abolition/remediation of relevant execution systems and durable judicial safeguards.
