# Singapore

> **Governance record only.** This dossier does not itself create licensing restrictions. Only an exact Schedule incorporated with an exact ECL version has operative effect.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope.** Current POFMA/information-control and qualifying public-order/assembly enforcement remain reviewable as specific coercive projects. The former capital-punishment/execution component is removed as an independent ECL basis under the ECL 0.2 consistency rule.

## 2. ECL 0.2 criteria engaged

Current scope principally engages **§5.6 Systematic coercive domination and unlawful political repression** where POFMA, speech-law or public-order enforcement is materially used to criminalize, punish or systematically suppress protected political, journalistic, rights or peaceful civic activity.

A separate surveillance implementation may engage **§5.1** if it is materially used for repression, persecution, arbitrary detention or suppression of lawful dissent.

Capital punishment is **not a standalone ECL category**. An execution-related technology or project would enter ECL scope only if it independently satisfied §5.2, §5.6 or another operative criterion on its facts.

## 3. Evidence supporting `S`

In March 2026 Singapore brought the first criminal prosecution for communicating alleged false statements under POFMA against government critic Jay Ish'haq Rajoo. In May 2026 rights organisations called for charges against Kokila Annamalai over alleged non-compliance with a POFMA order to be dropped. Government ministries continued issuing POFMA Correction Directions during 2026.

Those current information-control/enforcement facts are sufficient to keep the project under scoped ECL review without relying on the death penalty as a separate designation theory.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

POFMA provides a statutory route to appeal ministerial directions to the High Court. Singapore's Court of Appeal has established an objective framework under which a direction may be set aside, and courts continue to hear constitutional claims.

These mechanisms materially defeat whole-State `R` and must remain outside the coercive scope when acting independently.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions

The active scope covers **POFMA/information-control projects and public-order/assembly enforcement only insofar as they materially implement unlawful political repression or systematic suppression of protected civic activity under ECL 0.2**.

Courts, POFMA judicial appeals, independent legal defence, unrelated public administration and capital-punishment activity that does not independently satisfy an ECL criterion are excluded.

## 6. ECL 0.2 delta determination

**UPHOLD / NARROW `S`.** The scoped political-repression basis survives. The `capital-punishment/execution` component is removed to make Singapore consistent with the explicit ECL 0.2 rule that punishment, including capital punishment, is not prohibited merely because of its severity or legal character.

## 7. Review / removal triggers

Move toward `U/N` on repeal/narrowing or demonstrably rights-compliant/non-repressive implementation of POFMA and durable rights-compliant assembly practice. Re-escalation toward `R` would require evidence that independent judicial/remedial channels become materially captured and that qualifying coercive conduct becomes cross-institutional.
