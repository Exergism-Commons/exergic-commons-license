# Nauru

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**U — Under Review.** Nauru can no longer remain a clean `N` while offshore asylum/resettlement arrangements are currently operating on its territory and serious deprivation-of-liberty/living-condition concerns persist. The evidence does not yet separate Nauruan and Australian control sufficiently, nor identify a direct ECL §5 technology/prohibited-use nexus, for stable `S`.

## 2. ECL criteria tested

Review focused on coercive technological capture, unlawful targeting, surveillance and data systems used to administer migration detention/transfer without meaningful contestability.

## 3. Evidence supporting review

Current 2026 reporting documents asylum seekers/refugees remaining on Nauru under Australia-funded arrangements, with severe living-condition and work/mobility concerns. The UN Human Rights Committee has previously treated Australia as exercising effective control in parts of the offshore system, highlighting the attribution problem rather than eliminating Nauru's need for review.

## 4. Counter-evidence and attribution uncertainty

The central legal/operational architecture is heavily financed and directed by Australia; historic abuses cannot simply be reassigned to Nauru. Current evidence is insufficient to determine which Nauruan organs, if any, operate a qualifying ECL prohibited project independently of Australian policy.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions

Review follows specific Nauruan governmental participation in current offshore-processing/resettlement administration. Nauruan people, ordinary public services and conduct controlled by Australia are excluded absent separate proof.

## 6. Adversarial determination

**ESCALATE `N → U`.** The live regime defeats a confident no-basis finding, but fragmented cross-State control and ECL-project fit remain unresolved.

## 7. Review triggers

Escalate to `S` if a specific Nauruan agency/project is shown materially deploying surveillance, targeting or non-contestable technological control to facilitate unlawful detention/transfer. Return to `N` if current arrangements cease or evidence confirms no qualifying Nauruan project.
