# Papua New Guinea

> **Governance record only.** No licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination
**U — Under Review.**

## 2. ECL criteria engaged
Suppression of protected expression/assembly, coercive displacement and extractive-project domination.

## 3. Evidence supporting continued review
Recent records include restrictions on media/assembly, weak accountability and serious environmental/extractive and displacement concerns. A 2026 UPR submission reports many accepted reforms remain unimplemented.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions
The current evidence is dominated by governance failure, private/non-State violence and under-implementation rather than a clearly attributable systematic coercive State project.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions
No State apparatus is restricted. Review follows specific police/public-order, media-control or extractive-enforcement projects when State coercion is established.

## 6. Adversarial determination
**RETAIN U.**

## 7. Review / removal triggers
Escalate on current systematic media/protest repression or coercive displacement/extractive enforcement; move to `N` on effective institutional reform and non-recurrence.
