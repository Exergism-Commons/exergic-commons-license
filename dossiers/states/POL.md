# Poland

> **Governance record only.** This dossier does not itself create licensing restrictions. Only a versioned Schedule expressly incorporated by a software release has operative effect.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope.** The current finding is limited to the Belarus-border international-protection/pushback system and related access-exclusion measures where they materially defeat individualized protection or independent monitoring safeguards.

## 2. ECL criteria engaged

- coercive targeting through summary return or denial of individualized protection;
- irreversible or effectively unreviewable coercive capture at the border;
- obstruction of independent scrutiny where access restrictions materially prevent humanitarian, journalistic or rights monitoring.

## 3. Evidence supporting the determination

Poland's temporary restriction on lodging applications for international protection at the Belarus border remained active at the cutoff. A Council of Ministers regulation dated 13 July 2026 extended the mechanism again after parliamentary consent. Amnesty International has assessed the underlying framework as effectively codifying unlawful pushbacks. Separately, the government extended the Belarus-border buffer zone through 31 August 2026.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

The prosecution of the Hajnówka humanitarian activists ended with a final acquittal after the prosecutor withdrew the appeal in March 2026. Courts and prosecutorial review therefore provide concrete counter-evidence against treating humanitarian assistance, the justice system or migration policy generally as Restricted.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions

Scope covers **Border Guard/interior-administration projects materially refusing or bypassing individualized access to international protection, carrying out unlawful summary returns/pushbacks, or using border access restrictions to materially obstruct independent humanitarian, journalistic or rights monitoring without adequate safeguards**. Courts, prosecutorial remediation, humanitarian assistance and unrelated migration/public-order functions are excluded.

## 6. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD / NARROW S.** The active border project survives review, but prior public-order/humanitarian-criminalisation language is narrowed in light of final acquittal and functioning judicial/prosecutorial counterweights.

## 7. Review / removal triggers

Repeal/non-use of the asylum-suspension mechanism, individualized non-refoulement review, cessation of unlawful pushbacks, effective monitoring access and durable compliance with judicial/international-protection safeguards.
