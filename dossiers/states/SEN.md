# Senegal

> **Governance record only.** No licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination
**U — Under Review.**

## 2. ECL criteria engaged
Suppression of protected expression/assembly and accountability obstruction.

## 3. Evidence supporting continued review
Journalists/commentators and environmental protesters have faced detention or prosecution for expression/assembly; media suspensions have occurred.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions
The Supreme Court held a ministerial media suspension unlawful, the Constitutional Council opened a path to prosecute 2021–24 killings/torture, police were arrested after a custody death, and new detention/accountability institutions were created.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions
No State/public-order system is restricted. Review follows specific expression/protest enforcement or obstruction of accountability.

## 6. Adversarial determination
**RETAIN U.** Current coercion and active remediation coexist at a level that does not justify stable `S` or `N`.

## 7. Review / removal triggers
Escalate on systematic renewed speech/protest detention or impunity; move to `N` on durable decriminalization, judicial compliance and accountability.
