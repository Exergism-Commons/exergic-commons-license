# Bosnia and Herzegovina

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope, strictly attributed to identified Republika Srpska projects.** Bosnia and Herzegovina as a State is not a Restricted Party.

## 2. Evidence supporting the determination

The Office of the High Representative reported deterioration in Republika Srpska's media environment through April 2026: re-criminalized defamation increasingly produced criminal cases against journalists and chilling effects, alongside political pressure, intimidation, SLAPPs and disproportionate inspections. The Council of Europe Commissioner separately warned about inflammatory speech from the highest RS political leadership.

## 3. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

The national Constitutional Court remains a meaningful rights guarantor and entered a 2026 EU/CoE programme to strengthen remedies/enforcement. National institutions developed a 2026–2030 journalist/media-protection framework with police, prosecutors, journalists and civil society, including journalist-safety coordination work in Banja Luka.

## 4. Attribution and exclusions

Scope follows only RS entity-level coercive media/political projects. The State of Bosnia and Herzegovina, its Constitutional Court, Federation institutions, national journalist-protection mechanisms and unrelated RS public services are excluded absent separate evidence.

## 5. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD S with strict sub-State attribution.**

## 6. Review triggers

Decriminalization/non-use of defamation against protected journalism, effective journalist protection, cessation of retaliatory pressure/inspections and compliance with constitutional/human-rights judgments.
