# Italy

> **Governance record only.** This dossier does not itself create licensing restrictions. Only a versioned Schedule expressly incorporated by a software release has operative effect.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope.** The finding follows specific public-order/preventive-policing, detention and migration-externalization projects when they materially produce ECL-prohibited coercive effects. Italy and Italian institutions are not Restricted generally.

## 2. ECL criteria engaged

- coercive targeting of protected peaceful protest / civil disobedience;
- arbitrary or seriously abusive detention;
- coercive migration transfer/externalization into a substantiated serious-abuse risk;
- disproportionate surveillance or preventive restrictions where materially used to suppress protected civic activity.

## 3. Evidence supporting the determination

Law No. 54 of 24 April 2026 converted Decree-Law No. 23 on public security, police functions, immigration and international protection. Amnesty International's June 2026 legal analysis identifies provisions it considers incompatible with international human-rights obligations. The broader 2025/26 evidence record also documents disproportionate restrictions on peaceful protest, serious concerns in prison and migrant detention, and continued externalization/cooperation arrangements where migrants and refugees may face grave abuse.

This supports a project-specific finding: the existence of a restrictive law is not itself enough; the ECL scope follows implementation that materially produces coercive or rights-destructive outcomes.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

Italy has strong judicial counter-institutions. Constitutional, ordinary and EU courts have reviewed detention and migration arrangements, including decisions constraining external processing and ordering release where safeguards were inadequate. Investigations and remedies concerning detention and law-enforcement abuse also remain available.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions

Covered scope: implementation of public-order/preventive-policing powers materially used against protected peaceful protest; detention practices amounting to serious ill-treatment or arbitrary confinement; and migration-externalization/return projects materially facilitating detention or transfer into systems with a substantiated serious-abuse/non-refoulement risk.

Excluded absent independent evidence: the Italian State as a whole, the judiciary, rights-protective investigations/remedies, ordinary policing, and migration administration that provides individualized lawful review.

## 6. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD / DEFINE S.** Current 2026 legislation and continuing detention/externalization concerns support `S`, while active judicial constraints make whole-State or whole-police attribution inaccurate.

## 7. Review / removal triggers

- repeal or rights-compliant implementation of the relevant public-security measures;
- sustained proportionality in protest policing and preventive restrictions;
- effective safeguards, conditions and remedies in detention;
- individualized non-refoulement review and effective judicial control over migration externalization/return.
