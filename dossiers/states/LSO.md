# Lesotho

> **Governance record only.** This dossier does not itself create licensing restrictions. Only a versioned Schedule expressly incorporated by a software release has operative effect.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope.** The current finding concerns correctional abuse/conditions and specifically attributable public-order or speech-enforcement projects, not the Lesotho State generally.

## 2. ECL criteria engaged

- coercive targeting / unlawful violence;
- political repression of protected expression or peaceful assembly;
- deprivation of meaningful contestability and remedy in detention settings.

## 3. Evidence supporting the determination

The Commission of Inquiry into the December 2023 Maseru prison-break aftermath found that Lesotho Correctional Service officers subjected hundreds of detainees to physical assault and other ill-treatment, with one death and permanent disability in another case, and recommended disciplinary and criminal action. The issue remains current rather than purely historical: an April 2026 Ombudsman follow-up reported that only about 9% of 78 correctional recommendations issued in 2023 had been implemented, while overcrowding, healthcare and resource failures persisted.

The 2025 record also includes arrest and sedition prosecution of social-media activist Tšolo Thakeli after criticism of government economic policy and police restrictions on peaceful protest/public meetings.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

The Ombudsman is an independent constitutional oversight institution and its investigations provide meaningful review. The Ministry of Law and Justice has acknowledged reform needs, some recommendations have been acted on and rehabilitation/construction measures have been announced. These remediation and oversight functions weigh strongly against whole-State attribution.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions

Scope follows **LCS detention/conditions, abuse and accountability failures materially involving cruel or degrading treatment**, and **specific police/public-order/sedition enforcement materially suppressing protected non-violent expression or peaceful assembly**. Courts, the Ombudsman, remediation projects, ordinary public services and the population/nationality are excluded.

## 6. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD / DEFINE S.** A downgrade would be premature because official 2026 follow-up still records severe unresolved correctional-system deficiencies and low implementation of remedial recommendations. The existence of active oversight and reform, however, prevents expansion to the State generally.

## 7. Review / removal triggers

Review immediately on substantial implementation of Ombudsman/Commission recommendations, credible criminal or disciplinary accountability for correctional abuse, durable reduction of serious overcrowding/health failures, or material reform of sedition/public-order enforcement. Removal is justified when the attributable coercive projects cease or are durably remediated.
