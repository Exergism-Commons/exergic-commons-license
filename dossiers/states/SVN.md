# Slovenia

> **Governance record only.** No licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination
**S — Restricted with defined scope.**

## 2. ECL criteria engaged
Systematic discriminatory domination, coercive administrative targeting and expanded surveillance/police control.

## 3. Evidence supporting determination
In January 2026 the Security Law was used to seize critical social assistance from more than 1,000 people/families, disproportionately affecting Roma communities. Police also announced high-risk areas with expanded monitoring/data powers. This is current deployment, not proposal.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions
Civil society, the Supreme Court and Constitutional Court are actively challenging the law. Those functions materially constrain attribution and are excluded.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions
Scope is limited to Security Law enforcement materially stripping subsistence benefits, discriminatory vehicle/property enforcement, and associated enhanced policing/data projects. Courts, legal challenges and unrelated administration are excluded.

## 6. Adversarial determination
**ESCALATE U → S.** The previously prospective risk became operational in 2026.

## 7. Review / removal triggers
Suspend/remedy discriminatory seizures, invalidate/narrow coercive provisions, impose independent limits on enhanced policing/data powers and provide effective Roma equality remedies.
