# Somalia

> **Governance record only.** This dossier does not itself create licensing restrictions. Only a versioned Schedule expressly incorporated by a software release has operative effect.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope.** The finding follows specifically attributable federal NISA/police/information-control and coercive eviction projects; Somalia's fragmented authority makes whole-State attribution inaccurate.

## 2. ECL criteria engaged

- political repression and coercive targeting of protected journalism/peaceful activism;
- arbitrary detention and intimidation;
- deceptive/information-control projects materially suppressing independent reporting;
- coercive displacement where State-attributable eviction operations deny basic safeguards.

## 3. Evidence supporting the determination

Amnesty's 2025/26 Somalia record documents security-force attacks, harassment, intimidation and arbitrary arrests of journalists and government attempts to censor reporting on insecurity and forced evictions. In April 2026 NISA officers arrested activist Sadia Moalim Ali in connection with peaceful activism; she remained detained under extended investigative orders.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

Somalia remains institutionally and territorially fragmented. Somaliland authorities, Al-Shabaab and other armed/de facto actors require separate attribution. Courts, civil-society organisations, journalists and independent monitoring functions contest federal action and are not part of the implicated apparatus merely by operating in Somalia.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions

Scope covers **federal NISA, police, information-control and other attributable federal/local security projects materially using arbitrary detention, violence, threats, censorship or prosecution against protected journalism/peaceful activism, and specifically evidenced forced-eviction operations involving unlawful coercion or denial of basic safeguards**. Somaliland, Al-Shabaab and non-attributable clan/armed actors are excluded and require separate actor/project assessment.

## 6. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD / DEFINE S WITH FRAGMENTED ATTRIBUTION.** Current federal coercive projects remain sufficiently evidenced, but actor separation is mandatory.

## 7. Review / removal triggers

Release/remedy for peaceful-expression detainees, durable protection of journalists, repeal/non-use of vague censorship directives, lawful eviction safeguards and effective independent oversight of NISA/police detention.
