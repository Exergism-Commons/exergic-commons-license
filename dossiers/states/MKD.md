# North Macedonia

> **Governance record only.** This dossier does not itself create licensing restrictions. Only a versioned Schedule expressly incorporated by a software release has operative effect.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope.** Current evidence supports a narrow border/detention finding, not a generic restriction of North Macedonian police, prisons or government.

## 2. ECL criteria engaged

- coercive targeting/unlawful violence in border enforcement;
- denial of meaningful asylum/contestability through summary pushbacks;
- torture or ill-treatment in detention where specifically evidenced;
- accountability failures.

## 3. Evidence supporting the determination

Amnesty International's 2025/26 record reports the continuing practice of unlawful pushbacks from North Macedonia to Greece at the southern border. The latest CPT baseline also identified credible allegations of police ill-treatment and longstanding detention deficiencies.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

North Macedonia is implementing a multi-year EU/Council of Europe penitentiary reform programme through December 2026. The programme strengthens external oversight, healthcare, rehabilitation, rules of engagement and reporting. In July 2026 senior prison-police officers received mandatory training on preventing ill-treatment, with participation from the specialised prosecutorial unit responsible for offences committed by police/prison-police personnel.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions

Scope follows **border-control operations materially carrying out unlawful summary pushbacks or associated violence, and identified police/prison-detention practices involving ill-treatment or serious accountability failures**. The wider police/prison administration, specialised prosecutor, courts and reform/oversight projects are excluded absent separate evidence.

## 6. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD / NARROW S.** The pushback project remains sufficiently current, but active oversight/reform materially narrows what can responsibly be attributed.

## 7. Review / removal triggers

Independently verified cessation of unlawful pushbacks; effective access to asylum/shelter; credible investigation/remedy for police ill-treatment; and demonstrated operation of external oversight and the penitentiary reform framework.
