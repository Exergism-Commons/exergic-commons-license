# Malta

> **Governance record only.** This dossier does not itself create licensing restrictions. Only a versioned Schedule expressly incorporated by a software release has operative effect.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope.** The finding concerns specific migration-detention, maritime-return and related coercive projects, not Malta or its institutions generally.

## 2. ECL criteria engaged

- arbitrary or seriously punitive detention;
- coercive targeting and unsafe transfer/refoulement risk;
- deprivation of meaningful contestability;
- criminalization of protected resistance where specifically attributable.

## 3. Evidence supporting the determination

Amnesty's 2025/26 Malta record reports continued automatic detention of many irregular maritime arrivals/asylum seekers on revised grounds, largely ineffective appeal processes, detention conditions the CPT characterized as deeply punitive and allegations of staff violence. It also records delayed/non-assistance at sea and continued Malta-Libya cooperation in interception/return toward Libya, which is not considered a safe place of disembarkation in the cited record.

The `El Hiblu` prosecution remained active into 2026 against people accused after opposing return to Libya, preserving a current question about whether prosecution is being used against protected resistance to an unsafe or unlawful return.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

Maltese and European courts provide material review. Malta's Constitutional Court ordered a retrial in the `Easter Monday push back` case, and Council of Europe/European human-rights mechanisms supervise detention and migration cases. These judicial/remedial functions are specifically excluded from restriction.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions

Scope follows **immigration-detention practices amounting to arbitrary or seriously punitive confinement; maritime non-assistance, pushback or Libya-cooperation projects materially facilitating unlawful return to a substantiated serious-abuse risk; and prosecution projects only insofar as they materially criminalize protected resistance to such a return**. Courts, legal defence, independent monitoring and rights-remediation functions are excluded.

## 6. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD / DEFINE S.** Current evidence supports identifiable coercive migration/detention projects while functioning judicial remedies make broader institutional attribution inaccurate.

## 7. Review / removal triggers

Review on replacement of automatic detention with individualized necessity/review, effective appeal safeguards, rights-compliant search/rescue and disembarkation policy, enforceable non-refoulement safeguards in Libya cooperation, or resolution/remedy of prosecutions premised on protected resistance to unsafe return.
