# Ecuador

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope.**

## 2. Evidence supporting the determination

UN communications in 2026 concern alleged excessive force, arbitrary detention, torture, intimidation, enforced disappearance and property destruction during `Exterminio Total`, and broader concerns over intelligence legislation, terrorism offences, repeated states of exception, privacy, oversight and civic freedoms.

## 3. Counter-evidence

Ecuador replied to the relevant UN communications in May/July 2026 and constitutional/electoral judicial institutions continue to operate. The replies are credited but do not yet demonstrate project remediation.

## 4. Attribution and exclusions

Scope follows substantiated military public-security operations, intelligence/surveillance systems lacking adequate independent safeguards and emergency/counterterrorism enforcement producing prohibited conduct. Unrelated military/civil public activity is excluded.

## 5. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD / DEFINE S.**

## 6. Review triggers

Independent San Martín investigation, judicial review/remediation of intelligence/emergency powers, accountability and non-recurrence safeguards.
