# Nigeria

> **Governance record only.** This dossier does not itself create licensing restrictions. Only a versioned Schedule expressly incorporated by a software release has operative effect.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope.** The finding attaches to specifically evidenced coercive security units/projects, not to Nigeria, Nigerians, the Nigeria Police Force or armed forces generically.

## 2. ECL criteria engaged

- coercive targeting / unlawful violence;
- arbitrary detention and enforced disappearance;
- torture and serious custody abuse;
- coercive suppression of peaceful assembly where specifically evidenced;
- obstruction of accountability.

## 3. Evidence supporting the determination

Amnesty International's February 2026 investigation into the Imo State Police Anti-Kidnapping Unit (`Tiger Base`) documents prolonged arbitrary detention, torture, extortion, enforced disappearances, removal of children without records, degrading conditions and deaths in custody. The broader 2025/26 record also documents unlawful force against peaceful protesters and civilian harm from security operations.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

Nigeria's National Human Rights Commission remains an active quasi-judicial counter-institution. In 2026 it published national human-rights dashboards, issued a civic-space advisory directed at police and public authorities, and continued torture-prevention/detention-oversight work as Nigeria's National Preventive Mechanism.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions

Scope follows **identified police, military or other public-security units/facilities materially participating in torture, enforced disappearance, arbitrary or secret detention, unlawful lethal force, serious custody abuse or material obstruction of investigation/accountability**. NHRC, courts, independent oversight/remedial functions, ordinary public services and security units lacking separate evidence are excluded.

## 6. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD / NARROW S.** Current 2026 evidence strongly preserves a project/unit-level restriction, but federal/state fragmentation and active human-rights oversight make generic whole-service or whole-State attribution inaccurate.

## 7. Review / removal triggers

Closure or compliant reform of abusive facilities such as Tiger Base; traceable custody/access-to-counsel safeguards; credible prosecutions/remedies for torture, disappearance and unlawful killings; and sustained rights-compliant protest/security operations.
