# Montenegro

> **Governance record only.** No licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination
**N — No current state-level basis.**

## 2. ECL criteria engaged
Prior review concerned police facial-recognition surveillance without adequate legal safeguards.

## 3. Evidence supporting review
Police procurement/planning for facial recognition created a credible risk of repressive surveillance.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions
The independent data-protection authority suspended deployment, and 2026 legislative amendments were developed to align internal-affairs/security rules with GDPR safeguards. Current evidence does not show resumed coercive deployment.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions
No police surveillance project is currently Restricted. Future deployment remains separately reviewable.

## 6. Adversarial determination
**DOWNGRADE U → N.** Independent intervention prevented the project from crossing into stable coercive use.

## 7. Review / removal triggers
Reopen on facial recognition or mass biometric surveillance deployed without lawful necessity, independent authorization/audit and effective remedy.
