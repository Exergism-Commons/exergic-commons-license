# Samoa

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**U — Under Review.** A 2026 UN Special Procedures communication concerning mandatory religious observance and proposals to ban non-Christian religions makes the prior clean `N` premature. Current evidence does not yet establish sufficiently deployed enforcement or a technology/prohibited-use nexus for `S`.

## 2. ECL criteria tested

Review focused on systematic discriminatory technological enforcement, repressive surveillance/targeting of religious minorities, deceptive information control and coercive technological capture.

## 3. Evidence supporting review

OHCHR Special Procedures communication `WSM 1/2026` raised concerns about a proclamation imposing mandatory religious observance and proposals to ban non-Christian religions as potentially inconsistent with Samoa's international obligations.

## 4. Counter-evidence and uncertainty

Samoa continues treaty-body engagement, including 2026 work under the Convention on Enforced Disappearance. The reviewed record does not establish that the proposed religious restrictions became a materially deployed technological/coercive system meeting ECL §5.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions

Review follows the specific religious-observance/legal project and any enforcement system, not Samoa, Samoan people, Christianity or public administration generally.

## 6. Adversarial determination

**ESCALATE `N → U`.** Current legal/political risk requires review, but capability/proposal is not treated as deployed prohibited use.

## 7. Review triggers

Escalate to `S` on systematic enforced religious targeting using surveillance, automated discrimination or other qualifying coercive technology. Return to `N` if proposals are withdrawn/invalidated or remain non-coercive without an ECL project.
