# Laos

> **Governance record only.** This dossier does not itself create licensing restrictions. Only a versioned Schedule expressly incorporated by a software release has operative effect.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope.** Current evidence supports restriction of identifiable political-security, disappearance, transfer and religious-persecution projects. It does not justify treating all Lao State functions or every alleged attack on an exiled critic as proven State conduct.

## 2. ECL criteria engaged

- political repression and enforced disappearance;
- coercive targeting / unlawful detention;
- destruction of meaningful contestability through concealment or impunity;
- coercive transfer into a substantiated serious-abuse risk;
- discriminatory religious persecution.

## 3. Evidence supporting the determination

In February 2026 critic Sisay Luangmonda, known as Bao Mor Khaen, disappeared after a reported arrest by soldiers; his body was subsequently found. Human Rights Watch reported in March 2026 that authorities had not provided his family information about his whereabouts after the reported arrest and had not at that point initiated an adequate investigation or forensic examination.

The wider 2025/26 record also documents restrictions on expression and peaceful assembly, persecution of Christians, and the arrest and transfer of Myanmar nationals to the Myanmar military despite serious-risk concerns. UN Special Procedures have additionally raised possible transnational repression against an exiled Lao defender, but that alleged State linkage remains a matter requiring proof rather than a settled attribution.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

Laos participated in its Universal Periodic Review and accepted recommendations concerning investigation of enforced disappearances. Some alleged attacks on critics have unresolved attribution. The ECL record therefore distinguishes substantiated State/security conduct from plausible but unproven proxy or transnational activity.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions

Covered scope follows attributable political-security detention/disappearance projects; failure or obstruction in investigating disappearances where State custody or agents are materially implicated; coercive transfers into a substantiated disappearance/torture/serious-abuse risk; and specifically evidenced discriminatory religious-persecution enforcement.

Unproven proxy conduct, unrelated public services, the Lao population/nationality and allegations lacking sufficient State attribution are excluded.

## 6. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD / DEFINE S.** The February 2026 disappearance/death record and continuing transfer/religious-enforcement concerns preserve `S`, while attribution limits make a whole-State finding insufficiently precise.

## 7. Review / removal triggers

- independent investigation and accountability in disappearance/death cases;
- durable protection of critics and defenders;
- non-refoulement-compliant transfer procedures and individualized review;
- remediation of discriminatory religious enforcement;
- credible clarification and accountability where transnational-repression allegations become attributable.
