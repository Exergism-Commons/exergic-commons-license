# Laos

> **Governance record only.** This dossier does not itself create licensing restrictions. Only a versioned Schedule expressly incorporated by a software release can have operative effect.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted in governance, but still blocked from Schedule rendering.** Fresh 2026 evidence preserves a current discriminatory-detention concern. The remaining obstacle is not lack of severity; it is that the publicly available current project identity is deliberately withheld for the safety of the affected people.

## 2. ECL criteria engaged

- political repression and enforced disappearance;
- coercive targeting / unlawful detention;
- destruction of meaningful contestability through concealment or impunity;
- coercive transfer into a substantiated serious-abuse risk;
- discriminatory religious persecution.

## 3. Evidence supporting the determination

In February 2026 critic Sisay Luangmonda, known as Bao Mor Khaen, disappeared after a reported arrest by soldiers; his body was subsequently found. Human Rights Watch reported in March 2026 that authorities had not provided his family information about his whereabouts after the reported arrest and had not at that point initiated an adequate investigation or forensic examination.

Fresh reporting on 29 June 2026 states that **six young Christians remained detained approximately six months after December 2025 arrests in northern Laos** and were being pressured to renounce Christianity as a condition of release. Four of the original ten detainees were reportedly released after recanting. This is sufficiently recent to preserve the `S` governance concern.

## 4. Why the Schedule gate remains

The June 2026 source deliberately withholds the village, district and detainees' real identities for security reasons. The report therefore cannot support an objectively knowable Schedule identity without defeating the source's protective redaction or guessing the location/unit.

Separately, no sufficiently authoritative post-March public update was found establishing whether the Sisay investigation remains absent or materially deficient today. ECL does not infer continuing non-investigation merely from silence.

## 5. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

Laos participated in its Universal Periodic Review, accepted recommendations concerning enforced disappearances and submitted its initial report under the Convention against Torture in June 2026. Those forms of engagement are relevant counter-evidence even though they do not resolve the specific cases above.

Some allegations of transnational repression also remain attribution-contested and are not silently incorporated into the restricted scope.

## 6. Attribution and exclusions

Governance scope follows attributable political-security detention/disappearance projects; obstruction of disappearance investigation where State custody/agents are materially implicated; coercive high-risk transfers; and specifically evidenced discriminatory religious-persecution enforcement.

No Schedule freeze may be generated for the current religious-detention record until the project can be identified without speculation. Unproven proxy conduct, unrelated public services, the Lao population/nationality and allegations lacking sufficient State attribution remain excluded.

## 7. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD `S`; KEEP STATUS/KNOWABILITY GATE.** Downgrading would ignore fresh 2026 continuing-detention evidence. Freezing an unnamed district or a broad Lao police/security/religious-enforcement class would violate the ECL knowability and narrowing rules.

## 8. Unlock / review triggers

- publication by a reliable source of a sufficiently precise district, facility, unit or finite project identity for the continuing religious detention;
- authoritative current-status confirmation of non-investigation/non-remedy in the Sisay case;
- a new named attributable disappearance, detention, coercive-transfer or discriminatory religious-enforcement project; or
- credible remediation sufficient to change the underlying `S` governance determination.
