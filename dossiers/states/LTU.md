# Lithuania

> **Governance record only.** No licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination
**S — Restricted with defined scope.**

## 2. ECL criteria engaged
Unlawful coercive return, arbitrary/de facto detention and denial of protection access.

## 3. Evidence supporting determination
Current 2026 review material documents continuing pushbacks, restricted access to asylum and detention concerns including children. The practices are attributable to border/migration enforcement rather than merely historical.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions
Lithuanian courts remain an important check; in 2026 the Supreme Administrative Court found a specific pushback unlawful. That supports scoped rather than whole-State attribution.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions
Scope follows border-guard/migration operations materially carrying out unlawful pushbacks, blocking individualized protection or imposing arbitrary/de facto detention. Courts and unrelated administration are excluded.

## 6. Adversarial determination
**ESCALATE U → S.**

## 7. Review / removal triggers
End pushbacks, ensure effective asylum access and judicial remedy, prohibit arbitrary child detention and implement court rulings.
