# Thailand

> **Governance record only.** This dossier does not itself create licensing restrictions. Only a versioned Schedule expressly incorporated by a software release has operative effect.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope.** Current restriction is tied to political-expression/public-order enforcement and coordinated State cyber/information-control projects, not to Thailand generally.

## 2. ECL criteria engaged

- political repression through criminalisation/detention of protected expression and peaceful assembly;
- deceptive or coercive information operations targeting civil society/opposition;
- coercive public-order enforcement where protected activity is materially suppressed.

## 3. Evidence supporting the determination

In February 2026 a Bangkok criminal court sentenced four democracy/free-expression activists to 32 months' imprisonment under lèse-majesté-related charges arising from peaceful political speech. Current review material also documents continuing use of section 112, sedition, computer-crime and assembly provisions against protected expression and peaceful activism.

The 2025 record additionally documented a coordinated police/military `Cyber Team` used to tarnish and undermine civil-society and opposition actors. This remains relevant because it identifies a specific State information-control project rather than merely a restrictive legal capability.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

Courts have granted bail in some cases; prosecutors dropped the Paul Chambers lèse-majesté case in 2025; and soldiers were convicted for torture in a separate case. These are genuine accountability signals and weigh against any whole-State attribution.

The earlier refoulement component is not retained as an active 2026 restriction absent sufficiently fresh recurrence at the cutoff. Historical unlawful returns remain a review trigger rather than a permanent project finding.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions

Scope covers **police, prosecutorial, public-order and cyber/information-control projects materially criminalising or intimidating protected peaceful expression/assembly, including section 112/sedition/computer-crime enforcement when used against protected speech, and coordinated State cyber operations targeting civil society/opposition**.

Courts exercising independent remedy, torture-accountability proceedings, unrelated policing/public administration and the Thai population/nationality are excluded.

## 6. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD / NARROW S.** Current expression/public-order and cyber-control evidence survives review; a generic active refoulement component does not.

## 7. Review / removal triggers

Durable non-use/reform of criminal provisions against protected speech, release/remedy in expression-only cases, cessation of coordinated cyber targeting and effective investigation of unlawful protest repression. Re-expand review if new attributable unlawful refoulement occurs.
