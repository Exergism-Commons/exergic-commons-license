# Moldova

> **Governance record only.** This dossier does not itself create licensing restrictions. Only a versioned Schedule expressly incorporated by a software release has operative effect.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope.** Current scope is limited to persistent pretrial-detention and ill-treatment/remedy failures in attributable criminal-justice systems.

## 2. ECL criteria engaged

- arbitrary or insufficiently contestable deprivation of liberty;
- torture/ill-treatment and coercive targeting;
- ineffective investigation and remedy that materially preserves coercive conduct.

## 3. Evidence supporting the determination

Council of Europe work in June 2026 records that repeated European Court judgments identified a pattern of violations concerning pretrial detention and that the lack of relevant and sufficient reasons in decisions ordering or extending detention remains a **systemic problem**. It also identifies unresolved enhanced-supervision cases concerning ill-treatment/torture to extract confessions and ineffective investigation/remedy.

These are current and institutionally attributable problems sufficient to preserve a narrow `S`.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

Moldova is actively participating in Council of Europe criminal-justice reform. The Ombudsman/National Preventive Mechanism remains an active oversight channel. A 2026 journalist-safety action plan and other media reforms provide substantial current counter-evidence against retaining the first-pass broad media-regulatory scope.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions

Scope follows **pretrial-detention practices materially lacking individualized necessity/proportionality** and **identified law-enforcement/detention projects involving ill-treatment/torture or ineffective investigation/remedy**. The broad media-regulatory component is removed from current scope. Conduct in Transnistria is not attributed to Moldova absent independent attribution evidence. Courts performing genuine remediation, the Ombudsman/NPM and reform/accountability projects are excluded.

## 6. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD / NARROW S.** The detention/ill-treatment basis remains current, while evidence of active reform and media-rights safeguards defeats the broader original scope.

## 7. Review / removal triggers

Review on durable compliance with individualized pretrial-detention reasoning, effective investigation/prosecution/remedy for torture or ill-treatment, implementation of European Court judgments and sustained operation of independent preventive oversight.
