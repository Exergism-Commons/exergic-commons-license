# Paraguay

> **Governance record only.** This dossier does not itself create licensing restrictions. Only a versioned Schedule expressly incorporated by a software release has operative effect.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope.** Current evidence supports specific protest/defender and Indigenous-rights enforcement findings, not a generic State or police restriction.

## 2. ECL criteria engaged

- coercive suppression of peaceful assembly and defender activity;
- discriminatory domination against Indigenous communities;
- coercive displacement/land enforcement;
- denial of meaningful consultation/FPIC;
- civic-control measures where materially used to suppress protected association.

## 3. Evidence supporting the determination

Amnesty International's 2025/26 assessment reports repression of social protest, criminalisation of defenders, structural discrimination and setbacks in Indigenous rights. Current 2026 reporting also documents unresolved Avá-Guaraní territorial/displacement claims and criminalisation concerns affecting Ayoreo community leadership.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

Paraguay retains courts, electoral institutions, public accountability bodies and civil-society organisations capable of contesting State action. The record does not justify treating private agribusiness conduct as governmental conduct without attribution, nor restricting all police/public administration.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions

Scope follows **specific police/prosecutorial/public-administration projects materially repressing peaceful protest or criminalising protected defender activity, and Indigenous-land/eviction or development-enforcement projects materially involving coercive displacement, discriminatory denial of recognised rights or serious FPIC failures**. Unrelated public services, independent judicial/remedial functions and non-attributable private conduct are excluded.

## 6. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD / DEFINE S.** The evidence remains sufficiently current for a scoped finding, but only where coercive State attribution is concrete.

## 7. Review / removal triggers

Durable protection of peaceful assembly/defenders; dismissal/remedy of retaliatory prosecutions; effective Indigenous land recognition/restitution and FPIC safeguards; and accountability for unlawful police/public-authority coercion.
