# Argentina

> **Governance record only.** No licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination
**S — Restricted with defined scope.**

## 2. ECL criteria engaged
Coercive targeting, repressive surveillance and suppression of protected peaceful assembly.

## 3. Evidence supporting determination
The 2025/26 record documents continuing injuries allegedly caused by State agents during demonstrations. Federal measures adopted in 2025 expanded police/intelligence access to social-media monitoring, State databases, identity detention and intelligence cross-referencing without ordinary judicial safeguards. The combination is sufficiently current and attributable for a project-level finding.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions
Federal courts, prosecutors, legislative contestation, civil society and provincial institutions remain capable of reviewing or resisting executive/security action. Argentina therefore does not support a whole-State finding.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions
Scope is limited to federal police/intelligence/public-order projects materially using unlawful force, arbitrary or warrantless detention, coercive surveillance or political monitoring against protected activity. Courts, independent accountability, provincial/unrelated services and the population are excluded.

## 6. Adversarial determination
**ESCALATE U → S.** Current deployment now supplies the persistence and attribution missing from the first pass.

## 7. Review / removal triggers
Narrow/repeal warrantless surveillance/detention powers, independently accountable protest policing, effective remedies for unlawful force and sustained non-use against protected activity.
