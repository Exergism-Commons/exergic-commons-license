# France

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope.** The judicially blocked Nice algorithmic CCTV system is explicitly **not** part of the current restricted scope.

## 2. Evidence preserving scoped restriction

The Interior Ministry stated in January 2026 that the national Olympic-derived algorithmic video-surveillance experiment was proposed for continuation through 31 December 2027 for defined events/locations. Parliament in 2026 considered additional measures to generalize/extend algorithmic video protection. Only enacted/materially deployed systems count; legislative proposals alone do not create restriction.

## 3. Counter-evidence with binding effect

On 30 January 2026 the Conseil d'État held that Nice's algorithmic processing of public-street CCTV at school entrances was not authorized under existing law; CNIL had previously reached the same conclusion. That project therefore leaves the ECL scope.

## 4. Adversarial determination

**NARROW S.** Retain only currently authorized/materially deployed law-enforcement VSA systems that actually satisfy ECL surveillance criteria and separately evidenced abusive public-order projects.

## 5. Removal trigger

Expiry/termination without replacement, binding safeguards that remove the ECL-relevant surveillance characteristics or project-specific judicial/administrative remediation.
