# Colombia

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope.** Generic conflict-security attribution is removed.

## 2. Evidence supporting the determination

Amnesty's 2025 investigation of 398 jurisdictional conflicts found continuing Military Criminal Justice interference capable of obstructing ordinary investigations into alleged abuses by police/armed forces. The CAT in 2026 assessed prior conflict/detention recommendations as only partially implemented.

## 3. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

Colombia ratified OPCAT and maintains ordinary prosecutors/courts, defender-protection policies and transitional-justice/accountability structures. Much present conflict violence is attributable to non-State armed groups and is excluded.

## 4. Attribution and exclusions

Scope follows specific military-jurisdiction obstruction and substantiated State-force abuse/impunity projects, not Colombia's security sector generally.

## 5. Adversarial determination

**NARROW S.**

## 6. Review triggers

Consistent ordinary-jurisdiction handling, OPCAT implementation, effective prosecution/remedy and end to military-jurisdiction obstruction.
