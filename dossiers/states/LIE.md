# Liechtenstein

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**N — No current State-level or defined-project basis.** The current record contains ordinary equality and institutional-rights gaps but no materially deployed ECL §5 project.

## 2. ECL criteria tested

Repressive surveillance, unlawful software-enabled targeting, automated discriminatory domination, deceptive population manipulation and coercive technological capture were tested.

## 3. Evidence requiring scrutiny

As a highly digitized small State, future government AI/data systems remain reviewable if their purpose or deployment becomes coercive.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

Liechtenstein's statutory human-rights monitoring body published its 2025 report in June 2026, documenting both progress and gaps; marriage equality took effect in 2025 and the State signed the Council of Europe AI Framework Convention. These are material contrary signals.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions

No Liechtenstein institution, population or nationality is designated through this dossier.

## 6. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD `N`.** No current prohibited-use project is established.

## 7. Review triggers

Reopen on political surveillance, automated discriminatory denial, unlawful targeting or coercive public-sector AI/data infrastructure.
