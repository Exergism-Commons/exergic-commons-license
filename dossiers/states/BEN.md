# Benin

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope.** The generic `police/protest` component of the first-pass finding is removed absent more precise current evidence.

## 2. Evidence supporting the determination

Digital Code offences concerning false news/electronic harassment continued to be used against protected expression; HAAC suspended media outlets and political critic Julien Kandé Kansou was prosecuted after critical/electoral speech. UN Special Procedures raised the detention/prosecution of journalist/defender Hugues Comlan Sossoukpè after his alleged abduction/forced transfer; as of July 2026 he remained detained amid serious fair-trial concerns.

## 3. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

HAAC suspensions of Le Patriote and Bénin Web TV were later lifted, demonstrating reversibility. Formal legal procedures remain available, though the Sossoukpè record raises concerns about counsel, notice and prolonged detention before CRIET.

## 4. Attribution and exclusions

Scope follows specific Digital Code prosecutions, HAAC/media-control actions and CRIET/counterterrorism/detention processes materially suppressing protected journalism, political expression or rights-defence. Unrelated policing and ordinary public functions are excluded.

## 5. Adversarial determination

**NARROW S.**

## 6. Review triggers

Digital Code reform, independent remedy in Sossoukpè/comparable cases, proportionate media regulation and cessation of counterterrorism tools against protected expression.
