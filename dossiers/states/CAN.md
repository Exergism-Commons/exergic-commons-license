# Canada

> **Governance record only.** No licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination
**U — Under Review.**

## 2. ECL criteria engaged
Potential discriminatory domination, coercive return/detention and institutional targeting.

## 3. Evidence supporting continued review
The 2025/26 record documents systemic racial/Indigenous disparities and restrictive migration/return practices. Certain development/policy changes also raise Indigenous self-determination concerns.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions
Courts, rights commissions, Indigenous litigation, federalism and new Black/Indigenous justice initiatives provide substantial contrary evidence and remediation capacity.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions
No Canadian State apparatus is restricted. Review follows specific return/detention or coercive Indigenous-rights projects only when deployment and attribution are proven.

## 6. Adversarial determination
**RETAIN U.**

## 7. Review / removal triggers
Escalate on systematic coercive return/detention or discriminatory targeting; move to `N` on durable remedy, rights-compliant migration practice and effective Indigenous consent safeguards.
