# Japan

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**U — Under Review.** Japan's prior clean `N` is no longer satisfactory after the 2025 resumption of executions and continuing documented problems with prolonged pretrial detention/`hostage justice`. Those practices are severe; however, ECL §5 does not prohibit punishment or detention merely because it is harsh or rights-incompatible, so a defined software/prohibited-use nexus must still be established before `S`.

## 2. Formal Exergism assessment

Pilot assessment: [`../../exergism/assessments/JPN.json`](../../exergism/assessments/JPN.json).

The formal status is **`insufficient_evidence`**, not a numerical midpoint. The dossier does not yet identify a sufficiently defined Software-enabled system, deployment or Restricted Project whose exergic variables can be bounded without manufacturing precision. This formal result supports keeping the case under review rather than forcing either a clean `N` or a mathematically cosmetic `S`.

## 3. ECL criteria tested

Review tested whether criminal-justice technology materially enables unlawful targeting, repressive surveillance, data-driven discrimination or irreversible coercive technological capture.

## 4. Evidence supporting review

Japan executed a death-row prisoner in June 2025 after nearly three years without executions. Current 2026 reporting also describes prolonged pretrial detention designed to pressure confessions, while the Tokyo High Court found investigation/prosecution unlawful in the Ohkawara Kakohki case.

## 5. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

Courts have produced major corrective outcomes, including acquittals and findings of unlawful investigation/prosecution, and litigation continues against prolonged detention practices. These mechanisms defeat any whole-State inference and materially constrain the case for a scoped technological restriction.

## 6. Attribution and exclusions

Review follows only specific execution, detention/interrogation and future data/technology systems. Courts, legal defence, reform work, ordinary administration and the Japanese population are excluded.

## 7. Adversarial determination

**ESCALATE `N → U`.** The coercive record is too current to close confidently, but existing evidence is insufficient to identify an ECL §5 Restricted Project. This dossier also flags a cross-dossier consistency question: capital punishment must not be treated as ECL-prohibited in one State and irrelevant in another without an explicit software/prohibited-use theory.

The formal layer reinforces that distinction by refusing to score an undefined technological object.

## 8. Review triggers

Escalate to `S` only if a specific technology/system is shown materially enabling unlawful targeting, repressive surveillance, non-contestable coercive capture or another §5 use. Return to `N` if review confirms no such nexus despite the underlying criminal-justice concerns.

A future scorable assessment requires enough evidence to bound the exact object's `P`, `A`, `V_ep`, `L`, `O`, `U`, `C`, `S`, `R` and `Ecol` values.
