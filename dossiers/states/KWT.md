# Kuwait

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**R — State apparatus Restricted.**

## 2. Evidence supporting the determination

Parliament remains suspended and legislative changes proceed through executive decree. Current reporting documents repression of expression/assembly, arbitrary detention, systematic Bidun discrimination, large-scale nationality revocations, summary deportations and executions. An April 2026 nationality-law amendment expanded revocation grounds tied to State interest/national security, and authorities announced a decree stripping more than 1,200 people of nationality without stated reasons.

## 3. Counter-evidence

Formal courts and nationality-review mechanisms exist, and authorities characterize many revocations as fraud/dual-nationality enforcement. The concentration of legislative power while Parliament is suspended materially weakens the principal political counter-institution.

## 4. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD R.** The coercive record spans executive law-making, nationality administration, police/cybercrime enforcement, detention and discriminatory legal status.

## 5. Review triggers

Restoration of parliamentary oversight, transparent individualized nationality procedures with remedy, end to discriminatory Bidun coercion, release/remedy for critics and durable due-process protections.
