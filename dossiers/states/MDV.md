# Maldives

> **Governance record only.** No licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination
**S — Restricted with defined scope.**

## 2. ECL criteria engaged
Coercive information control and suppression of protected journalism/expression.

## 3. Evidence supporting determination
The 2025 Broadcasting/Media Regulation framework created broad powers to fine journalists, suspend licences or broadcasts and close media on vague grounds. The system is enacted and institutionally deployable, rather than merely proposed.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions
Courts, media organizations and political contestation remain available; the record does not justify whole-State or generic public-order restriction.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions
Scope follows media/broadcast regulators and enforcement projects materially censoring protected journalism, compelling sources or using vague rules to suppress dissent. Courts and unrelated functions are excluded.

## 6. Adversarial determination
**ESCALATE U → S.**

## 7. Review / removal triggers
Narrow/repeal vague censorship powers, preserve source protection and judicial review, and demonstrate non-coercive implementation.
