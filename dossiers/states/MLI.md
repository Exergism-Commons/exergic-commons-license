# Mali

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**R — military/transitional governing apparatus.**

## 2. Evidence supporting the determination

The post-2020 military transition was extended by five years in 2025. Current reporting describes further curtailment of expression/assembly/association, arbitrary detention and disappearance of opposition/civil-society figures and unlawful attacks on civilians by government forces/allies. In February 2026 an appellate court upheld former prime minister/opposition leader Moussa Mara's imprisonment over a social-media post supporting detained political/civil-society figures.

## 3. Counter-evidence

Mali maintains formal courts, a Justice Ministry and a stated National Charter for Peace and Reconciliation. The review did not find evidence that these structures independently restrain the military political-coercion system.

## 4. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD R.**

## 5. Review triggers

Restoration of genuine political competition, independent judicial remedies, end to disappearance/political detention and verified civilian transition/accountability.
