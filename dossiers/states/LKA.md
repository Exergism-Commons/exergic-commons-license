# Sri Lanka

> **Governance record only.** This dossier does not itself create licensing restrictions. Only a versioned Schedule expressly incorporated by a software release has operative effect.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope.** Current scope follows actual coercive use of counterterrorism/detention powers and specifically evidenced security/intelligence harassment; reform proposals are not themselves treated as prohibited projects.

## 2. ECL criteria engaged

- political repression and arbitrary/prolonged detention under counterterrorism powers;
- repressive surveillance/intimidation of protected journalism, memorialisation and defender activity;
- torture/ill-treatment and custody-death accountability failures;
- coercive targeting of war-affected minorities seeking accountability.

## 3. Evidence supporting the determination

The current administration continued to use the Prevention of Terrorism Act while promising repeal. UN Special Procedures and Amnesty raised concerns about the proposed 2026 replacement law, including risks around arbitrary detention, privacy, defenders, torture and disappearance. Amnesty also issued a July 2026 urgent action after deaths and allegations of torture/ill-treatment involving transferred prisoners. Current reporting describes continued surveillance/intimidation of war-affected Tamil communities and defenders.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

The government has publicly committed to repeal the PTA and advance replacement legislation. The Human Rights Commission of Sri Lanka retains `A`-status and National Preventive Mechanism functions; the Office on Missing Persons remains operational; and broader justice/institutional reforms are being pursued. These developments materially narrow attribution.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions

Scope covers **actual use of PTA/counterterrorism powers materially producing arbitrary/prolonged detention, repression of protected journalism/memorialisation/defender activity or coercive surveillance; detention/prison projects involving torture, serious ill-treatment or custody-death accountability failures; and specifically evidenced security/intelligence harassment of war-affected minorities seeking accountability**. A draft law is not Restricted merely because it is proposed; only enacted or implemented coercive provisions enter active scope. HRCSL/NPM, OMP, courts and independent reform/remedial work are excluded.

## 6. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD / DEFINE S; REFORM AS COUNTER-EVIDENCE.** Active coercive practices survive review, but the reform process and functioning counter-institutions make a broader or static finding inaccurate.

## 7. Review / removal triggers

Repeal of the PTA and adoption/implementation of rights-compliant replacement law, cessation of coercive minority/defender surveillance, independent investigation of torture/deaths in custody, effective OMP/accountability outcomes and robust NPM access.
