# Morocco

> **Governance record only.** This dossier does not itself create licensing restrictions.

## 1. Current determination

**S — Restricted with defined scope**, limited to police, intelligence, prosecutorial, judicial and digital-surveillance systems materially used against peaceful dissent and Sahrawi human-rights activity, including in Western Sahara.

## 2. ECL criteria engaged

Political repression, retaliatory surveillance, coercive detention/prosecution, suppression of self-determination advocacy and intimidation of defenders/journalists.

## 3. Evidence supporting the determination

UN Special Procedures communications in 2025–2026 continued to document alleged repression of Sahrawi defenders and journalists by attributable State structures. A March 2026 communication concerned alleged retaliation and professional exclusion of lawyer/defender M'hamed Hali over views supporting Western Saharan self-determination.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions

Morocco is pursuing criminal-procedure reforms framed around fair-trial rights, expanded Justice Ministry/National Human Rights Council victim-protection cooperation and continues royal pardons that reduce sentences or release prisoners. These are meaningful improvements but have not specifically remedied the conduct underlying the scoped designation.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions

The finding is not a restriction of Morocco as a country, Moroccan people, ordinary public administration or unrelated judicial/public services. It follows the specified coercive projects and materially participating organs.

## 6. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD S.** General reform is meaningful counter-evidence, but the specific Western Sahara/dissent conduct remains sufficiently current and attributable.

## 7. Review / removal triggers

Independent investigation/remedy of retaliation cases, cessation of surveillance/prosecution of peaceful Sahrawi/dissent activity, enforceable fair-trial reforms and demonstrated non-recurrence.
