# Uruguay

> **Governance record only.** No licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination
**U — Under Review.**

## 2. ECL criteria engaged
Potential cruel/inhumane detention, institutional capture and obstruction of public accountability.

## 3. Evidence supporting continued review
Prison overcrowding exceeds 16,000 detainees, conditions are frequently inhumane and mortality remains high; four prisoners died in a 2025 fire. Access to public information is also restricted.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions
Uruguay retains a Parliamentary Commissioner/prison dialogue mechanisms, courts and prosecutions of former military/police officials for crimes against humanity. The current record better supports severe capacity/governance failure than a deliberately deployed ECL prohibited project.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions
No prison system or State apparatus is restricted. Review follows any specific detention project where deliberate coercive denial, abuse or accountability obstruction is established.

## 6. Adversarial determination
**RETAIN U.**

## 7. Review / removal triggers
Escalate on attributable deliberate ill-treatment/systematic denial of care or obstruction of oversight; move to `N` on sustained decongestion, mortality reduction and effective independent monitoring.
