# Tanzania

> Governance record only; no licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination

**R — State apparatus Restricted.**

## 2. Evidence supporting the determination

UN Special Procedures material covering the October 2025 elections into 2026 describes alleged excessive/lethal force, widespread extrajudicial killings and injuries, arbitrary detention, enforced disappearances, internet shutdowns and intimidation of journalists/defenders/civil society across the country. The communication concerns roughly 701 identified/unidentified alleged victims and a geographically broad security response.

## 3. Counter-evidence

Formal investigative and judicial institutions exist and authorities publicly committed to examine election-period violence. At the cutoff the review did not identify public prosecutions, binding findings or reparations sufficient to show effective constraint.

## 4. Adversarial determination

**UPHOLD R**, with a mandatory accountability-milestone review.

## 5. Review triggers

Credible independent investigation, prosecutions, public findings, compensation, institutional reform, restoration of civic/political space and safeguards against recurrence.
