# Chile

> **Governance record only.** No licensing effect by itself.

## 1. Current determination
**U — Under Review.**

## 2. ECL criteria engaged
Unlawful force, coercive public-order targeting and obstruction of accountability.

## 3. Evidence supporting continued review
Serious protest-policing abuses and incomplete accountability remain unresolved, and reform implementation warrants continued scrutiny.

## 4. Counter-evidence and exergic institutions
Courts, prosecutors, democratic oversight and police-reform mechanisms remain active. The cutoff record does not establish sufficiently fresh systematic deployment to convert the historical pattern into a current stable `S`.

## 5. Attribution and exclusions
Review follows named Carabineros/public-order operations and accountability failures only. The Chilean State, courts and unrelated policing are excluded.

## 6. Adversarial determination
**RETAIN U.**

## 7. Review / removal triggers
Escalate on renewed systematic unlawful protest force or accountability obstruction; move to `N` on durable reform, prosecutions/remedy and non-recurrence.
