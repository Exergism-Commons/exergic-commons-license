# Person dossiers

Individual natural-person designations are exceptional, evidence-based and narrowly scoped. Use [`_TEMPLATE.md`](_TEMPLATE.md).

A person is **not** Restricted merely because of office, employment, organizational membership, nationality, family relationship, sanctions-list presence, an arrest warrant or association with a Restricted Party/Project.

## Heightened person-level rule

Before proposing a person for a Schedule, the dossier must distinguish:

- allegation;
- official reasonable-grounds or warrant finding;
- charge/indictment;
- adjudicated fact;
- conviction; and
- public admission.

A warrant or sanctions entry is authoritative evidence of a formal proceeding/listing but is not a declaration of criminal guilt and does not automatically satisfy ECL's designation threshold.

Prefer organization/project restriction where it addresses the actual risk without imposing a permanent personal designation. A professional/project-specific relationship may instead create Covered Associate status under the exact operative license when the knowledge/material-participation requirements are met.

Only an exact incorporated Schedule can create a Restricted Party. The 2026 cross-entity reconciliation record is `../../reviews/2026/consistency/cross-entity-reconciliation.md`.
