# Person name

> **Governance record only.** This dossier has no licensing effect by itself. Individual designations are exceptional and require an exact incorporated Schedule.

## 1. Current determination

State `R`, `S`, `U` or `N`. A person is not restricted merely because of office, employment, organizational membership, sanctions-list presence, an arrest warrant, nationality, family relationship or association.

## 2. Exact identity

Record identity carefully enough to avoid namesake ambiguity. Include public role/organization only as needed for disambiguation.

## 3. ECL criteria engaged

Map alleged personal conduct or command/material-participation role to exact operative ECL criteria.

## 4. Evidence supporting the determination

Distinguish allegation, reasonable-grounds/warrant finding, charge, adjudicated fact, conviction and public admission. Never convert an arrest warrant into a finding of guilt.

## 5. Counter-evidence / due-process considerations

Record denials, acquittals, withdrawn allegations, uncertainty, role changes and the presumption of innocence where applicable.

## 6. Attribution and capacity limits

State whether scope is personal, command-specific or project/professional. Organizational membership alone does not recursively create personal restriction.

## 7. Adversarial determination

Apply a heightened specificity standard because person-level errors are uniquely harmful. Explain why a project/organization designation would or would not be sufficient instead.

## 8. Review / removal triggers

Include dismissal/acquittal, warrant withdrawal, corrected identity, role cessation, remediation and new evidence.

## 9. Sources

List stable primary/authoritative sources.

## 10. Procedural history

Record proposals, issues, Schedule history and related organization/project dossiers.
