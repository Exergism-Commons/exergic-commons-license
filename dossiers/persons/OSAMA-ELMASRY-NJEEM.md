# Osama Elmasry Njeem

> **Governance record only.** No licensing effect by itself. This dossier does not declare criminal guilt.

## 1. Current determination

**U — Under Review for person-level designation.** The ICC warrant provides unusually strong official reasonable-grounds evidence concerning alleged personal/command responsibility for conduct that, if established, fits ECL §5.2/§5.6. But an arrest warrant is not a conviction, and the organization/project restriction can address much of the licensing risk without prematurely imposing a permanent personal Restricted Party designation.

## 2. Exact identity

The ICC identifies **Osama Elmasry Njeem**, also known as **Osama Almasri Njeem**, as a person alleged to have been in charge of prison facilities in Tripoli. A stable Schedule would require exact identifying information from the ICC record to avoid namesake ambiguity.

## 3. ECL criteria engaged

The alleged conduct potentially engages:

- **§5.6 Systematic coercive domination** — imprisonment, torture/cruel treatment, persecution and severe coercive detention; and
- **§5.2 Coercive targeting** — alleged murder/sexual violence or operational direction materially facilitating unlawful violence.

## 4. Evidence supporting continued review

The ICC states that Pre-Trial Chamber I issued an arrest warrant on 18 January 2025. Its public summary says Njeem is suspected of crimes against humanity and war crimes including murder, torture, rape/sexual violence, imprisonment and persecution at Mitiga Prison. The Court states that the warrant alleges the crimes were committed personally by Njeem, ordered by him, or committed with his assistance by SDF/RADA members.

The warrant therefore supplies a formal judicial reasonable-grounds record, not merely press allegations.

## 5. Counter-evidence / due-process considerations

The proceedings are not a conviction and the presumption of innocence remains applicable. The ICC's reasonable-grounds threshold and ECL's licensing designation threshold are legally distinct.

The current record reviewed for ECL does not include a final merits judgment. Organization/project restriction of the SDF/RADA detention apparatus can address the immediate structural risk while preserving person-level caution.

## 6. Attribution and capacity limits

Review follows only the alleged personal/command role described in the ICC record. It does not restrict family members, colleagues or every SDF/RADA member through association.

## 7. Adversarial determination

**RETAIN `U` rather than importing the old blanket ICC-warrant class.** The evidence is serious enough to defeat `N`, but the heightened person-level standard and availability of organization/project restriction counsel against automatic `R` solely from warrant status.

## 8. Review / removal triggers

Escalate only after stronger individualized evidence or adjudicated findings sufficient under the ECL person-level standard. Reassess on surrender/confirmation proceedings, acquittal/dismissal, warrant withdrawal, corrected identity or other material judicial developments.
