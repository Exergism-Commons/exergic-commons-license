# ECL Terminology

This document provides repository-wide governance terminology. The operative legal definitions remain those in the exact ECL license version applied to software.

## Licensing terms

**Restricted Party** — a person, entity, governmental body, military body or other organization expressly designated by the exact Restricted Parties Schedule incorporated by a software release.

**Restricted Project** — under ECL 0.2-DRAFT, either a project expressly designated by the exact incorporated Schedule or a project that becomes restricted through Material Participation, direction, material benefit or circumvention involving a Restricted Party or relevant Covered Associate.

**Covered Associate** — a project-specific material associate as defined by the operative license. Association is not recursively transitive without limit.

**Prohibited Use** — conduct prohibited directly by the operative ECL license regardless of actor or project designation.

**Independent Remediation Activity** — the narrowly defined ECL 0.2-DRAFT safe harbour for genuinely independent investigation, judicial review, audit, legal defence, prosecution of official abuse, disclosure or remediation of prohibited/designated conduct. It is not a general `compliance` exception and does not authorize ordinary operations of a Restricted Party.

## Governance outcomes

**R — State apparatus Restricted** — provisional governance conclusion that the relevant governing/state apparatus itself satisfies the ECL threshold. It has no licensing effect until adopted by a Schedule.

**S — Restricted with defined scope** — only identified organs, systems, projects, functions or materially participating structures satisfy the threshold. A future Schedule must translate that governance scope into exact Restricted Parties, Restricted Projects, determinable classes and exclusions.

**U — Under Review / insufficient evidence** — material concerns exist but evidence, attribution, persistence, normative fit or scope is insufficient for a stable designation.

**N — No current state-level basis** — current evidence does not establish a State-level or defined scoped restriction. This is not an ethical endorsement.

## Review outcomes

**UPHOLD** — prior governance finding survives adversarial review.

**NARROW** — the category survives but its scope must be reduced.

**DOWNGRADE** — evidence or normative fit no longer supports the prior tier.

**REMOVE** — current evidence no longer supports restriction.

## Normative effect

Dossiers, registry entries and review outcomes are governance records only. Licensing effect arises only through an exact ECL version plus exact Schedule expressly incorporated by a software release.
