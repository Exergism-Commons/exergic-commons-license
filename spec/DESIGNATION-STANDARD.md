# ECL Designation Standard

> **Status: Draft governance specification.** This document does not independently create licensing restrictions.

## 1. Purpose

A designation must be specific, knowable, evidence-based, reviewable, non-retroactive and no broader than necessary to address the ECL-relevant conduct.

## 2. Required record

A proposed designation should identify:

1. exact actor, project or determinable class;
2. applicable ECL criterion, using the exact operative license version under review;
3. material conduct;
4. institutional or project attribution;
5. persistence, scale and severity;
6. primary/authoritative evidence;
7. material counter-evidence;
8. uncertainty and disputed facts;
9. proposed scope;
10. narrower alternatives considered;
11. remediation, narrowing or removal triggers; and
12. review date or milestone where appropriate.

## 3. Threshold

Political disagreement, controversial speech, nationality, ideology, religion, military status, commercial size or association alone are insufficient. Evidence must establish material and systematic participation, direction, enablement or sufficiently severe conduct substantially matching the operative ECL criteria.

For ECL 0.2-DRAFT this includes, where the exact elements are met, the new §5.6 category for systematic coercive domination and unlawful political repression. A serious human-rights problem does not become ECL-relevant merely by being serious; the conduct must fit an operative criterion or a project/party designation validly grounded under the incorporated license and Schedule.

## 4. Scope discipline

Prefer the narrowest accurate unit of attribution:

```text
project → unit/agency → controlled apparatus → whole governing/state apparatus
```

Do not escalate to a whole-State designation merely because one ministry, police unit, military operation or algorithmic system is implicated. Conversely, a whole-apparatus finding may be appropriate when qualifying conduct is persistent and cross-institutional and counter-institutions do not materially constrain it.

A scoped governance finding must eventually be translated into a Schedule entry that is legally knowable: an exact Restricted Party, an exact Restricted Project, a reasonably determinable class, or an express exclusion from a broader designation. A vague `function` label is not enough by itself for a stable Schedule.

## 5. Counter-institutions

Courts, auditors, ombuds institutions, inspectors general, prosecutors, legislatures and other accountability bodies must be treated as substantive counter-evidence when they produce verifiable rights-protective effects. Formal existence alone is insufficient.

Where a broader designation is otherwise justified, reviewers should identify whether a remedial function should be expressly excluded or can satisfy the operative Independent Remediation Activity safe harbour.

## 6. Adversarial review

Before a stable Schedule designation, reviewers should actively attempt to falsify the proposed result. The prior finding is not presumed correct.

The adversarial review must test both factual sufficiency and **normative fit against the exact license text**. A finding supported by the principles but not by the operative draft is a drafting inconsistency, not a reason to silently invent a prohibition.

## 7. Remediation

A designation should identify realistic narrowing/removal conditions where possible. Cessation, independently validated reform, effective remedy, accountability or attribution correction can justify narrowing or removal.

## 8. Schedule knowability

Governance review determines whether a designation should be proposed. Once an exact Schedule is incorporated by a software release, licensees should be able to determine applicability from that Schedule without independently re-litigating the underlying evidence.

## 9. Licensing boundary

A governance determination becomes operative only when an exact versioned Schedule expressly adopts it and a software release expressly incorporates that Schedule. The exact operative ECL version and exact Schedule version must be read together.
