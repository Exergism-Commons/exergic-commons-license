## ECL-native method

All dossiers use ECL's own standard. Freedom House, V-Dem, geopolitical alignment, ideology, nationality, religion, or similar external classifications are **not decision rules**. External sources may be evidence or leads only.

The full common schema is defined in **#1**. It evaluates internal and external exergy, institutional attribution, persistence, primary/authoritative evidence, contrary evidence, uncertainty, and reviewability.

Possible reasoned outcomes are:

- No current state-level basis
- Restricted with defined scope
- State apparatus Restricted
- Under Review / insufficient evidence

Opening a dossier has **no licensing effect**. Only a specific `RESTRICTED-PARTIES.md` Schedule incorporated by a software release can create an operative restriction. State findings concern institutional/governmental apparatuses, never populations or nationalities.
