## 1. Interpretation and exclusions

For this partial draft:

- a named State entry restricts only the exact governing/coercive apparatus class stated below, not the population, nationality, territory or independent private actors;
- materially independent judicial, audit, legal-defence, accountability and qualifying `Independent Remediation Activity` remain excluded unless separately and expressly listed;
- controlled or associated entities are included only when the entry states an objectively knowable control rule or when ECL Material Participation independently applies;
- no external sanctions, warrant or watch list is incorporated dynamically;
- organizational affiliation, employment, residence, nationality, family relationship or remote association does not create status by itself; and
- project entries bind only the frozen project boundary and actors materially participating in that project under the operative ECL terms.
