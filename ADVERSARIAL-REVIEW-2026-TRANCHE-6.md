# ECL Adversarial Review 2026 — Tranche 6

Review cutoff: **2026-08-11**

This tranche reviews four whole-State/de-facto-governing `R` findings in transitional or authoritarian systems where formal institutional change could potentially undermine the original attribution.

## Results

### #101 Madagascar — **UPHOLD R, LIMITED TO CURRENT MILITARY/TRANSITIONAL GOVERNING APPARATUS**

**Counter-evidence credited:**

- Madagascar had functioning constitutional institutions before the October 2025 military takeover. The High Constitutional Court has issued independent legal decisions, including rejecting legislation it found legally defective.
- The new authorities promised political transition within a maximum two-year period and initially announced releases of people imprisoned for opposing the previous government.

**Evidence against downgrade:**

- The current military authorities took power following the October 2025 coup and dissolved most State institutions except the National Assembly.
- By March-April 2026, Amnesty documented a deepening crackdown by the military authorities against Gen Z activists and civil-society members using vague offences such as criminal conspiracy, threats to national security and destabilization; the record includes arbitrary arrest/detention and alleged enforced disappearance.
- Security-force killings and other abuses from the 2025 protests remain without effective accountability.

**Adversarial finding:** the `R` result survives **for the current military/transitional governing and security apparatus**. It should not be read as a permanent designation of all Malagasy institutions across future constitutional orders. A genuine civilian transition, restoration of independent constitutional institutions, release/remedy for political detainees and prosecutions for protest killings should trigger immediate re-review.

### #105 Mali — **UPHOLD R**

**Counter-evidence credited:**

- Mali maintains formal courts, a Justice Ministry and a stated National Charter for Peace and Reconciliation. These institutions and policy processes are evidence that legal/governance structures exist beyond military operations.

**Evidence against downgrade:**

- The political transition following the 2020 coup was extended by five years in 2025.
- Amnesty's 2025/26 record describes further curtailment of expression, peaceful assembly and association; arbitrary detention and enforced disappearance of political opposition and civil-society figures; and unlawful attacks on civilians by government forces and allies.
- In February 2026 an appellate court upheld the imprisonment of former prime minister/opposition leader Moussa Mara on charges linked to a social-media post expressing solidarity with detained political/civil-society figures.
- A July 2026 Central Sahel review describes authoritarian governance without effective political opposition and systematic repression of critical voices in Mali, Burkina Faso and Niger.

**Adversarial finding:** the formal institutions identified have not shown sufficiently independent or effective restraint of the military governing apparatus. Political/judicial coercion is central rather than peripheral. `R` survives.

Evidence/counter-evidence:
- https://www.amnesty.org/en/location/africa/west-and-central-africa/mali/report-mali/
- https://www.amnesty.org/en/latest/news/2026/02/mali-moussa-mara/
- https://www.amnesty.org/en/documents/afr05/1319/2026/en/

### #118 Myanmar — **UPHOLD R**

**Counter-evidence credited:**

- The military-backed authorities completed an electoral process and installed a formally elected government/president in 2026.
- In August 2026 they allowed an unusual Red Cross/foreign representative visit to detained former leader Aung San Suu Kyi, and regional diplomacy has begun exploring possible talks.

**Evidence against downgrade:**

- The electoral process excluded genuine opposition, operated under laws criminalizing criticism/disruption of the election and was conducted amid military control and large areas where voting was impossible.
- The military-backed government continues to detain Suu Kyi and other political prisoners and has rejected ASEAN demands for unconditional release and continued special-envoy access.
- Military offensives intensified even while regional actors explored talks, and the underlying conflict/repression architecture remains controlled by the same military leadership that seized power in 2021.

**Adversarial finding:** election formalities and limited diplomatic gestures do not constitute independent counter-institutions or effective remediation. `R` survives for the military-backed/de facto governing, security, detention and electoral-control apparatus. Any genuine negotiated civilian transition or release/restoration of political competition must trigger review.

Evidence/counter-evidence:
- https://www.hrw.org/world-report/2026/country-chapters/myanmar
- Current ASEAN/Myanmar developments reviewed at the 2026-08-11 cutoff.

### #124 Nicaragua — **UPHOLD R**

**Counter-evidence credited:**

- Nicaragua remains subject to UN treaty-body and Special Procedures processes and has occasionally engaged with international mechanisms. Formal constitutional and judicial institutions remain in place.

**Evidence against downgrade:**

- A March 2026 UN Special Procedures communication describes a broad and persistent pattern including arbitrary detention, enforced disappearance, ill-treatment/torture, arbitrary deprivation of nationality, forced exile, censorship, closure/confiscation of assets and repression of peaceful religious expression and assembly.
- 2026 UN material also treats return to Nicaragua as carrying serious risks for political dissidents and defenders.
- Treaty-body follow-up letters continued in 2026, while the record does not show effective domestic judicial/security reform or accountability capable of restraining the systems underlying the designation.

**Adversarial finding:** the prohibited conduct spans executive, security, judicial/administrative, nationality, property-confiscation and civic-space systems. Formal institutional existence does not establish effective independence or remediation. `R` survives.
